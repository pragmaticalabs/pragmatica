### Fixed (2026-10-10 — #2086: the divergent-tail cut reads the head inside the append lock)
- **The recovery segment and the truncation witness could miss a record the cut removed.** `StreamPartitionManager.cutRing` read the ring's head before it entered the ring's ordered section; an append that landed between that read and the lock was removed by the cut but was in neither the segment nor the witness, and the reported count and range were short. (The quarantine fence for replicated appends is enforced inside the lock, so only an owner-local append can land there.) The head is now read inside the section, once, for the witness, the segment and the report, and the reported last offset is the one actually cut. `[verified: aether/aether-stream/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/aether/stream/StreamPartitionManagerDivergentTailTest.java]`
- Pinned the stream-name filter of the destroy-path close of a standing `stream-divergent-tail-cut-refused`: destroying one stream leaves another stream's standing warning open.
