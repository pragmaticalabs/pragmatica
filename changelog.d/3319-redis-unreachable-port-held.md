### Fixed (2026-11-17 — #3319: Redis unreachable-server tests failed when another process took the freed port)
- **The unreachable-server tests no longer guess a free port** (#3319). They bound a server socket, read its port and closed it; a parallel module (`-T 1C`) could be handed the same ephemeral port in between and the connect then succeeded (`expected ConnectionFailed but was success`). On Linux the tests now hold the port for their whole duration in a bound, never-listening socket, so a connect is refused by construction. Test-only; no production change. [verified: `RedisConnectionTest`, `RedisClientTest`, `RedisClientCloseTest` under a port-grabbing load]
