### Fixed (2026-11-18 — #3398: DurableEntityForgeTest read an answer from a node whose replica fold was being rebuilt)
- **`DurableEntityForgeTest` read back through the node that accepted the create, which is not the owner, and took a transient refusal for a wrong value.** A non-owner that holds the partition serves the bounded-stale read from its own replica fold; while that fold is rebuilt after a log cut it answers the typed transient `FoldInProgress`, whose response shape carries `amount` 0 exactly like an absent key. The owner had acknowledged the update (`updated`, 999) and every other node returned 999. The four assertions that read an entity right after writing it now poll at once and retry every refusal the product classifies as transient (`Cause.isTransient()`, reported by the test blueprint as `transientFailure`) until a deadline; a non-transient failure fails the test at once, and a never-clearing refusal fails naming the key, the node port and the last response. Test-only change; no product behaviour changed.
