### Fixed (2026-10-18 — #2234: slow re-link after a partition heals while SWIM already sees the peer alive)
- **The per-peer reconcile backoff ignored SWIM.** After a long outage the backoff sat at its 60 s cap, so a healed peer was redialed up to about 77 s later (jitter plus the 5 s tick), although SWIM, on its own UDP socket, already reported it alive.
- While the raw-SWIM liveness gate reports the peer HEALTHY or SUSPECTED, the backoff is evaluated with its cap equal to its initial 5 s value and the attempt time earned at the 60 s cap no longer holds the dial back, so the next tick redials (the next dial is due about 4 s after the last, +-10% jitter so nodes do not re-dial a healed peer in lockstep): at most one handshake per 5 s per peer while SWIM is alive and QUIC is blocked. A peer SWIM does not see alive keeps the doubling to 60 s; an unwired gate reads as not alive. Both the topology and the FSM desired-set reconcile paths use it.
