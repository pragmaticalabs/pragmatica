### Fixed (2026-11-03 — #2613: RollbackManager's unguarded read-modify-put of PreviousVersionValue could lose a committed rollback)
- **A rollback committed between `RollbackManager`'s read of the rollback record and its plain put was overwritten**, losing `rollbackCount`, `lastRollbackAt` and `failedVersions`: the `maxRollbacks` bound and the rule against rolling back onto a failed version did not hold, so a rollback loop could exceed its budget. `resetRollbackCount` had the same shape.
- The record is now written only as a leader transaction, derived from the committed SliceTarget and record on every SliceTarget put while leader (version change or not): it commits iff this node is still the committed leader, the target still equals the one read (whole-value witness) and the record is still the value read. The previous version comes from the notification's `oldValue`, not from the mirror, and a read-ahead notification writes nothing. `[verified: aether/aether-control/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/aether/controller/RollbackManagerGuardedRecordTest.java]`
- A refused write re-derives from the committed state, at most three attempts per chain; a refusal caused by a later version change writes nothing (that change's own notification records it). After the third refusal the leader logs at ERROR and raises the operator warning `rollback-record-reconcile-exhausted` (closed by the INFO `rollback-record-reconciled`). `[verified: RollbackManagerRecordReconcileTest]`
- Stated guarantee: a version change is recorded exactly within one leader's tenure; across a handover the new leader's activation derives it with `previous = the record's current version`, which can skip an intermediate version no leader recorded; on exhaustion the record lags until the next SliceTarget update or leader activation. `[mechanism: RollbackManager.deriveRecord]`
- Behaviour change: a node that is not the committed leader (`LeaderKey`) no longer writes the record at all, where the plain put landed unconditionally.
