### Added (2026-11-17 — #2529: JBCT naming rules for technology words in type names and package segments)
- **`JBCT-NAM-06` (type name) and `JBCT-NAM-07` (package segment)**, both at INFO and business mode only, flag a technology, pattern or layer word in a name: `Repository`, `Dao`, `Controller`, `Adapter`, `Gateway`, `Client`, `Impl`, `Service`, `Manager`, `Helper`, `Util`, `Jooq`, `Jpa`, `Jdbc`, `Postgres`, `Http`, `Rest`, `Kafka` in type names; the same words plus `internal`, `utils`, `common`, `persistence`, `usecase`, `domain`, `application`, `infra`, `integration`, `bootstrap`, `config` in package segments. Matching is on whole camel-case parts (`Restaurant` is not flagged for `Rest`). INFO never fails a build. [verified: `jbct/jbct-lint/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/jbct/lint/cst/rules/TechnicalNameRuleTest.java`]
- **`[lint.naming] add / remove`** extends or narrows the word lists through the parent chain of `jbct.toml`; an unknown key under `[lint.naming]` or a non-word entry is refused by the loader. [verified: `jbct/jbct-core/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/jbct/config/NamingConfigTest.java`]
