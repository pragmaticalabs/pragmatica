### Changed (2026-11-26 — #3754: a drain or eviction has one pending attempt per target)
- **The cluster topology manager keeps one pending attempt per drain or eviction target.** A repeated or raised request for a target whose attempt is still in flight joins that attempt's outcome instead of opening a second one.
- **An attempt's effects take place only while the manager still holds that attempt.** This covers the registry write, the failure untrack and the grace reap.
- **A refused witness, a deactivate or activate, and a cancellation each resolve the attempt's outcome exactly once.**
- **The leader's drain goes through the membership state machine as a `DrainRequested` event.**

  `[mechanism: ClusterTopologyManagerRecord.DrainAttempt seq match under the manager's monitor; DrainDecision computed under the monitor, its action run after release]`
