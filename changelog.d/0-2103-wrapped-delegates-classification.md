### Changed
- `Cause.Wrapped` now reports its origin's classification: `isTerminal()` and `isTransient()` answer what the origin answers (false when there is no origin), and the bare `Causes.cause(message, source)` does the same with its source. A wrapper around a timeout is therefore transient, and a retry policy that retries transient causes retries it. A wrapper needing another answer overrides explicitly: `PublishOutcomeUnknown` and `ForwardError.OutcomeUnknown` stay neither terminal nor transient (a write whose outcome is unknown must not be re-driven by a transient-only policy), and the terminal wrappers `RemoteTierError.Rejected`, `ForwardError.NotRetried` and `S3SharedTier.SharedTierUnavailable` stay terminal and never transient. `RetryOn` and `RetryMethodInterceptor` now warn that a retry policy needs an idempotent method (#2103).
