### Fixed (2026-11-09 — #2569: a refused management forward reply gave the client 503 "outcome unknown" for a publish that succeeded; part of #2583 P2)
- **The receiver's reply to a forwarded management request was sent once and its outcome ignored.** A FORWARD-lane backpressure refusal of that one frame (a few milliseconds long) dropped the only reply, the entry node's 2.5 s hop timer fired, and the client got 503 "outcome unknown" for a request the governor had served (the first publish to a new stream, ember-shard-1).
- `ManagementServer` now re-sends the same reply while the transport proves it did not write it (`BackpressureRefused`, `NoPeerState`, `ConnectionDead`): hold 250 ms, doubling to a 4 s cap, +-20% jitter, reset by a `Sent` (the `RefusalBackoff` ruled for #2574, held per peer). It stops when the requester's budget cannot cover the next hold (one WARN) and never re-sends an `EncodeFailed` frame. A reply that was written is not re-sent: a frame lost after `Sent` is still the requester's timeout to report (outcome unknown). The requester ignores a late or duplicate reply, so a re-send that races an earlier copy is harmless.

- Operator recovery: none needed for a refused reply; a client that still sees 503 "outcome unknown" (reply refused for good, or lost after being written) must treat the request as possibly applied and read back or use its idempotency key.
