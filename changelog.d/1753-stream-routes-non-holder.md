### Fixed (2026-11-26 — #1753, #1963: stream routes answered "no such stream" / "not owned by this node" on a node that does not hold the rings)
- **A stream route gives the same answer whichever node receives it** (#1753, #1963). `STREAM_PARTITION`, `STREAM_CONSUMERS`, `STREAM_GET` (`/info`) and `STREAM_READ` decided a stream's existence from the answering node's own partition manager, so a delegate that held the stream metadata-only answered `Stream partition is not owned by this node` and one that had not materialized it answered 404, while the holder answered 200. Every stream route now resolves the stream through ONE catalog lookup (registry entry or committed stream config); unknown means the catalog has no such stream (404), a catalog-known stream the owner cannot be asked about is 503, and partition bounds are the owner's. `STREAM_CONSUMERS` moved from `StreamRoutes` to `StreamApiRoutes` (same route, same path) and now reports the owner's `(head, tail, count)` per partition instead of the answering node's ring.
