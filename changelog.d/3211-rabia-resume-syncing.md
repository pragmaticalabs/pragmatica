### Fixed (2026-11-14 — #3211: a Rabia voter paused while Syncing resumed as Idle without a sync round)
- **A pause taken before activation resumes into synchronisation** (#3211, part of #2287). `RabiaEngine` pauses a `Syncing` engine on quorum loss too; on quorum return it entered `Idle` directly, with no sync round, no adoption, no restart self-vouch and no participation record, so restarted nodes behind a decided slot could vote on that slot again. `Paused` now records whether the engine had activated: an activated engine resumes to `Idle`/`Observing` as before, one paused from `Syncing` starts a fresh sync round and serves only through `activate`, which writes the participation record. Until then it answers sync requests `COLD`. [verified: `RabiaPauseWhileSyncingTest` (engine-level and three-engine re-decide scenarios, red on the old resume)]
