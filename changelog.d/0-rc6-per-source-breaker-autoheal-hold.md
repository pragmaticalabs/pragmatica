### Changed (2026-11-05 — rc6 f5b: per-source provisioning breaker, auto-heal switch on worker and community paths)
- **The provisioning circuit breaker is one per source binding.** A source whose provisions fail three times in a row opens only its own breaker; another source keeps provisioning. Triggers act on the right source: `setDesiredCount` and a node joining reset the source concerned, a leader handoff and the NORMAL phase transition reset all, and a refused provision (admission deferral, provider failure) counts against its own source. A refusal counted before a source is resolved is reported as `(unresolved)` and gates nothing. `GET /cluster/topology/circuit-breaker` keeps its aggregate fields and adds `sources`; `GET /cluster/provisioning` adds the same under `circuitBreaker.sources`; the reset takes an optional `{"source": "<name>"}` (CLI: `--source`) and resets every source without it. [verified: ClusterTopologyManagerPerSourceBreakerTest]
- **Auto-heal disabled now holds the worker and community paths too.** The worker/spot reconcile makes no provision or terminate, and the community placement begins no operation, on the cluster's own initiative; the operator is told once per source/role or community (`auto-heal-scaling-held`) and enabling auto-heal resumes them (`auto-heal-scaling-resumed`). Not held: an operator-directed retirement, and a desired count or community `target_size` committed while auto-heal was disabled (the disable records the configuration version; any later committed version releases the hold, for every worker source and community). `AutoHealStateValue` gains `disabledAtConfigVersion`. [verified: ClusterTopologyManagerWorkerReconcileTest, CommunityPlacementReconcilerTest]
