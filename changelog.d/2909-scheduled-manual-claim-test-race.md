### Fixed (2026-11-08 — #2909: ScheduledSingleModeNoOverlapTest manual-run claim test failed intermittently)
- **The test raced its own leader's fire settling.** A fire gives up its durable cluster claim and records its outcome before it gives up its in-process claim (`ScheduledTaskManager.fireFixedRate` releases `ctx.inFlight` in a callback that runs after the fire's promise settles, on another thread). `manualRun_takesTheClusterClaim_soItNeverOverlapsAFireOnAnotherNode` waited for the recorded outcome, then asserted the old leader could claim the task again; when that callback had not yet run, the leader refused on its own finished fire's in-process claim. That refusal is the safe direction (a brief spurious "claim held" for a manual run on the same node, never a double run), so product code is unchanged. The test now waits for the leader's in-process claim to be free before the manual-run assertions, which also makes "a manual run holds the claim over a fire" depend on the cluster claim alone. `[verified: aether/aether-invoke/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/aether/invoke/ScheduledSingleModeNoOverlapTest.java]`
