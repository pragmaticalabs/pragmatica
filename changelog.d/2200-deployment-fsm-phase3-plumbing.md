### Added (2026-10-14 — #2200: deployment state machine rewrite, phase 3 tracer PR B: the plumbing)
- **KV notifications carry the revision of the batch that produced them.** `KVStore.notificationRevision()` (beside `isReplaying()`) returns, inside a dispatch, the slot of the committed batch, never the `committedRevision` field, which only reaches the slot after the batch has published. A consumer that read a snapshot at revision r can therefore discard exactly the notifications the snapshot already holds. Mutations without a slot carry the unchanged revision. No existing notification or subscriber changes.
  [verified: `integrations/cluster/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/cluster/state/kvstore/KVStoreNotificationRevisionTest.java`]
- **The runtime parts of both deployment FSMs exist, are constructed and are tested, and are bound to nothing.** A mailbox with one consumer thread and drop-older coalescing, generation-stamped timers, the write path (one `apply` per step, settlement mapping, the pre-submit leader check), a snapshot cut at a revision, the node and cluster interpreters with a `Close`-only stop, a per-artifact ordered local-operation executor with stale-incarnation handling, the slice runtime adapter for the steps the tracer walks (the others fail with `unwired in phase 3 tracer`), and the translator from FSM effects to events, operator warnings, audit lines and metrics. Nothing registers them with a node, so no running cluster changes behaviour.
  [verified: `aether/aether-deployment/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/aether/deployment/wiring/`, `aether/node/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/aether/node/deployment/EffectTranslatorTest.java`]
- **Named limits.** The FSMs raise operator-warning codes that are not constants of `OperatorWarningCode`; the translator logs and counts such a warning (`aether_deployment_fsm_warnings_unmapped_total`) and does not publish it as an operator event until the codes are catalogued. The command builders of the slice runtime adapter are copies of the legacy ones, pinned against them by a golden test.
