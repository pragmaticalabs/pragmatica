### Fixed (2026-10-08 — #2011: Rabia re-commits an already-committed batch when its NewBatch arrives after the Decision)
- **A voter that learned a slot's Decision before the originator's `NewBatch` arrived put the committed batch back into `pendingBatches`**, where it was proposed and committed a second time on every voter, re-applying an acknowledged older write over newer ones (a KV key went 125 -> 46 on all three nodes in `HierarchicalDecisionReplayTest`). `doHandleNewBatch` now consults a bounded ledger of committed (batch id, correlation ids) and drops a batch whose correlation ids were all committed; a batch with some new correlation id is admitted with the committed ones stripped, so an identical resubmission (same commands, new correlation id) still commits. `learnProposedBatch` (Propose and snapshot-restored pending) applies the same filter; local submit is not filtered. [mechanism: `CommittedBatchLedger`, filled in `RabiaEngine.commitChanges`, the single commit funnel] [verified: `RabiaReorderedDeliveryTest.newBatchDeliveredAfterItsDecision_isNotCommittedASecondTime`, `proposalCarryingACommittedBatch_isNotRequeued`, `identicalCommandsSubmittedAgainAfterCommit_commitAgain`; `CommittedBatchLedgerTest`]
- **`NewBatch` is no longer offline-buffered.** The peer offline buffer (10,000 frames, no TTL) re-delivered buffered frames on reattach, so a NewBatch could arrive minutes after its slot decided, or reach a restarted voter with an empty ledger. `NewBatch` now implements `NoOfflineBuffering`; a missed one is repaired by the proposals that carry the batch. The `NoOfflineBuffering` javadoc no longer claims consensus traffic tolerates a late frame. [verified: `PeerStateTest.offerOutbound_rabiaNewBatch_isNeverBuffered_whileOtherConsensusFramesStillAre`]
- **Measured on a dedicated Linux host, interleaved, same test:** `HierarchicalDecisionReplayTest` passed 12 of 12 with the fix and 5 of 8 on the unfixed tip (3 failures with the older value stuck).
- [unverified: ledger window] The ledger keeps the last 65,536 committed batch ids; a NewBatch still in flight is delayed by at most the consensus send retry budget (about 5 s), which that covers up to about 13k commits/s. [unverified: snapshot restore] Batches committed in slots a snapshot restore skipped are not in the ledger. [unverified: concurrent identical submissions] If two identical submissions are merged and the committing Decision carried only one correlation id, a late copy of the other is admitted.
