### Fixed (2026-11-07 — #2817: RestoreWindowMintForgeTest bound fixed ports inside the ephemeral range)
- **`RestoreWindowMintForgeTest` probes a free port block below the ephemeral floor** (#2817). Its literals 33900/34000/34100 lay inside the Linux ephemeral range (32768-60999), so a concurrent outbound connection could hold 34100 ("Failed to bind to port 34100"). It now scans 10400-10800 (registered in `TEST_PORT_ALLOCATION.md`) and retries a start that loses a port on a fresh block. Test-only.
