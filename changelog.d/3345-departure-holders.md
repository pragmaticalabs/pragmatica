### Fixed (2026-11-18 — #3345: a departing node could count a key delivered to a node that no longer held it)
- **A departing node's push counted a key as safe on an ack from any receiver**, including one that had restarted, left the ring or was draining itself, so the node could halt holding the last live copy. A key now counts as delivered only by a holder: a receiver in the current view whose ack carries the process token recorded for its current tenure and that is not draining. A view change, a lost holder (token mismatch, a "draining" nack, a failed pre-exit probe) and each exit attempt re-plan the owed pages; acks and nacks never do. The owed set is frozen at the drain fence: a key written after it is not owed. [mechanism: `DeparturePushRun` records holders per target tenure and re-plans from `L = self + drainSet` on exactly those triggers]
- **One key a receiver refuses no longer starves the rest of its page.** A page nacked or unanswered as a whole is split in halves down to single keys, at most log2(page size) extra rounds per page; a single key still refused stays owed and is reported at the deadline. [mechanism: `DeparturePushRun` splits a nacked or timed-out page of several keys]
- **A halt resolves the push at once**: the run observes the departure window's halt on its own timer, at least once per resend interval, instead of only at a page send or its deadline. Nothing is sent past the attempts deadline, and the deadline task is released when the push resolves. [mechanism: `DeparturePushRun` halt watch and deadline timer, cancelled together on resolve]
- **Wire shape:** the departure ack (`MigrationDataAck`) gains `bootToken`, `storeGeneration` and `draining`. A mixed-version cluster of rc5 builds across this change is not supported.
