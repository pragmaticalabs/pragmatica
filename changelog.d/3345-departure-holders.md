### Fixed (2026-11-18 — #3345: a departing node could count a key delivered to a node that no longer held it)
- **A departing node's push counted a key as safe on an ack from any receiver**, including one that had restarted, left the ring or was draining itself, so the node could halt holding the last live copy. A key now counts as delivered only by a holder: a receiver in the current view whose ack carries the process token recorded for its current tenure. One predicate decides resend, split, exit and close; at a close a key is reported delivered only when its holder also passed a probe sent after the acknowledgement. The owed set is frozen at the drain fence: a key written after it is not owed. [mechanism: `DeparturePushRun` records holders per target tenure; every decision asks one `safe` predicate]
- **The plan is re-made from one view of the ring.** A view change, a lost holder (token mismatch, a "draining" nack, a failed pre-exit probe) and each exit attempt re-plan the owed pages from a single ring snapshot per re-plan; acks and nacks never do. A page lives in one tenure of its target: a page of an earlier tenure is dead at the next re-plan and its keys are paged again. [mechanism: `DeparturePushRun` observes the ring once per re-plan and tags every page with its target's tenure]
- **One key a receiver refuses no longer starves the rest of its page.** A page nacked or unanswered as a whole is split in halves down to single keys, at most log2(page size) extra rounds per page; a single key still refused stays owed and is reported at the deadline. A slow target costs n * ceil(delay / resend wait) key-sends (64 keys at twice the wait: 128; 524 before this change). [mechanism: `DeparturePushRun` splits a nacked or timed-out page and resends only keys not yet safe at the target]
- **A deadline close sends one probe round** to the holders whose acknowledgement no passed probe covers, and may end up to one probe deadline after the window. A sole key still owed at the deadline raises the at-risk event. [mechanism: `DeparturePushRun` probes once at the deadline, then resolves with its owed set]
- **A halt resolves the push within one resend interval**: the run observes the departure window's halt on its own timer instead of only at a page send or its deadline. Nothing is sent past the attempts deadline, and the deadline task is released when the push resolves. [mechanism: `DeparturePushRun` halt watch and deadline timer, cancelled together on resolve]
- **Wire shape:** the departure ack (`MigrationDataAck`) gains `bootToken`, `storeGeneration` and `draining`. Receivers still send zero for the store token and false for the draining flag; the sender checks them, and receivers fill them in a later change. A mixed-version cluster of rc5 builds across this change is not supported.
