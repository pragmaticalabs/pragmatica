### Fixed (2026-11-20 — #3471: ClusterTestPortsTest released-port control observes the cause)
- **`ClusterTestPortsTest`'s released-port controls no longer re-probe the port after the allocator refused it.** The re-probe raced a contender that binds and releases the port inside the allocator's own probe (third occurrence: #2902, #3188, #3471). `ClusterTestPorts` now records, per refused candidate, the cause (`LOCK_HELD`, `PROBE_SAW_BUSY`, `PROBES_ALL_FREE`), and the controls assert on that cause: a refusal while every probe said free fails, a probe that saw the port busy passes. A thread-bound probe seam lets a test run the contender inside the probe deterministically. Test-only; no production change.
