### Fixed (2026-11-24 — #2755: a stream owner that has not applied its ownership record stamped Epoch.ZERO and was refused as a stale epoch)
- **An owner whose store holds no ownership record yet is refused with the transient `StreamError.OwnershipNotApplied`, not `StaleEpochAppend`.** `KvStreamOwnerEpochSource` answers `Epoch.ZERO` while the record is unapplied; once the partition high-water had observed the committed epoch, the fence refused the zero stamp as a deposed writer. The fence now classifies a presented `Epoch.ZERO` (no owner holds that epoch) as "ownership not applied" (`Cause.Transient`, 503 on the read-route status table), so a caller no longer reads an apply lag as a fencing event. A real older epoch is still `StaleEpochAppend`. [verified: `StreamPartitionManagerTest$RecoveryEpochFence`, `BatchPublishPreAppendGuardsTest#staleEpoch_fencesTheWholeRunBeforeAppend`]
- Limit: a FORWARDED publish reaches the publisher as a permanent remote failure for this cause until `StreamForwardHandler` lists it as retryable.
