### Fixed (2026-11-26 — #3754: the node deployment FSM's timers outlived a stopped node)
- **The node deployment FSM had no production stop.** The node's stop (`DeploymentWiring.stop()`, in the FSM2-close step of `AetherNode.stop`) now calls `NodeDeploymentManager.stop()` under LEGACY, handed over through the wiring (`DeploymentWiring.nodeStop`, point `B13.ndm.Stop`; a recorded drop under FSM2, where the legacy node FSM is unbound). It dispatches `Shutdown`; entry into `Stopped` ends the FSM's one `OwnerLifetime` (one per `NodeDeploymentContext`, so a quorum flap does not cancel the bounded park escalation). A node stop that leaves the FSM in `Dormant` still ends it. [verified: aether/aether-deployment/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/aether/deployment/node/fsm/NodeDeploymentStopLifetimeTest.java]
- The four timers (routing remediation, activation remediation, parked-start escalation, FAILED-write retry) are armed through that lifetime; a task armed before the stop never runs its body after it. [verified: NodeDeploymentStopLifetimeTest]
- A body already past the lifetime's wrapper read at stop is narrowed, not fenced: every consensus write of the FSM goes through one door that refuses once the lifetime ended, and the park escalation checks again before it raises. The leader writes the same `NodeArtifactKey`, so a receiver-side fence for a stale node remains open (reported on #3754). [mechanism: LifetimeGatedCluster refuses `apply` after `OwnerLifetime.end()`]
