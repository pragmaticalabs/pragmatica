### Fixed (2026-11-11 — #3028: a worker kept a slice loaded after the leader removed it)
- **A removed slice is force-cleaned whenever it is loaded locally** (#3028, gap 1). `NodeDeploymentState.Active` unloaded a slice on the removal of its entry only when the local deployment state was ACTIVE, so a LOADED, ACTIVATING or ROUTING slice stayed loaded after the leader re-placed it, on the replay burst of a catch-up and on a live incremental remove alike. Cleanup now keys on `SliceStore` holding the slice. [verified: `FalseDeathWorkerCatchUpTest` arms B and F (red with the ACTIVE-only rule)]
- **A slice whose remove was dropped while Dormant is swept when quorum returns** (#3028, gap 2). A new `CatchUpSwept` event of the node deployment FSM unloads every slice `SliceStore` holds without a delivered `NodeArtifactKey` for the node; `Active` runs it on entry, before reactivation, and after each delivered KV replay burst (`KVStore.onReplayEnd`); `Dormant` ignores it. A swept slice is dropped from the reactivation list, so it neither republishes routes nor draws a FAILED write for a key the leader removed. [verified: `FalseDeathWorkerCatchUpTest` arm C]
- **A LOAD waits for an in-flight force-cleanup of the same artifact** (#3028), and a second cleanup request for an artifact joins the one in flight. Before, a put landing during the cleanup found the slice "already loaded" and the cleanup then unloaded the assigned slice. [verified: `FalseDeathWorkerCatchUpTest` arm E]
