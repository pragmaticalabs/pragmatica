### Changed (2026-11-26 — #3801: the port-0 socket rule is enforced on compiled test classes, not source text)
- **A test that binds a socket outside `HeldPort` now fails the build, whatever its port argument.** The `port-bind-gate` module holds the helper (`org.pragmatica.portbind.HeldPort`: the socket comes back open and holds its port while open) and a bytecode scan; the root pom's `port-bind-gate` profile runs the scan over every module's `target/test-classes` right after test-compile and names each offending `class:line`. A literal port, a computed one, `0`, an aliased address, a method reference (`ServerSocket::new`) and a call through a subtype are the same call to it. [mechanism: java.lang.classfile reads the invoke instructions and method-handle constants; the receiver type is resolved to its supertypes through the module's test classpath]
- Exceptions are listed with a reason in `port-bind-gate/src/main/resources/org/pragmatica/portbind/allowlist.txt`; an entry that excuses no call site fails its module's scan.
- Reach: the profile activates on `src/test`, so the `with-e2e` and `with-cloud-tests` modules are scanned by every build that compiles them (a `package -Pwith-e2e,with-cloud-tests` run printed a scan for each module; `aether/forge/forge-tests` was the one that held unmigrated sites, now migrated or listed). The two `examples/url-shortener*` builds have no root parent and carry the binding in their own poms. 27 allow-list entries are pick-then-close helpers kept as named debt (#1826 parts b and c): the gate stops growth, it does not remove them.
