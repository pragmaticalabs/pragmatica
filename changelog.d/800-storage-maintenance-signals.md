### Changed (2026-11-11 — #800: storage maintenance failure and progress signals)
- **A storage maintenance failure now raises an operator warning and its recovery.** A failed deactivation of the leader-pinned group raises `storage-maintenance-not-stopped` (closed by `storage-maintenance-stopped`); a demotion, garbage-collection or promotion pass that throws raises `storage-maintenance-pass-failed` once per part, subject `storage-maintenance/<part>` (closed by `storage-maintenance-pass-recovered`), and no longer keeps the passes after it from running.
- **A failed activation or deactivation of the leader-pinned group is retried** on the next three maintenance ticks (the budget restarts with each leader change).
- **`GET /api/v1/storage` carries a `maintenance` block**: last run time, outcome, consecutive failed ticks, last failure, and cumulative demoted, promoted and collected block counts. A failed promotion cycle fails the tick it is counted in, as a failed demotion or garbage-collection pass does.
- **A maintenance pass that does not complete is a signal.** A watchdog on its own timer raises `storage-maintenance-pass-overdue` when no maintenance tick completed within three maintenance intervals (subject `storage-maintenance/tick`) or one promotion cycle has run that long (subject `storage-maintenance/promotion`); the next completed tick or cycle raises `storage-maintenance-pass-completed`.
- **`GET /api/v1/health` carries a `storage-maintenance` component** reporting the leader's maintenance (`/health` is forwarded to the leader; a follower's own failures surface in its per-node metrics and operator warnings), `DEGRADED` on a failed-tick streak or an overdue pass. It never changes `status`, `ready` or `/health/ready`: a maintenance fault does not take a serving node out of rotation.
- **Prometheus gauges** `aether_storage_maintenance_consecutive_failed_ticks`, `aether_storage_maintenance_seconds_since_last_pass` and `aether_storage_maintenance_last_outcome{outcome=...}` on `GET /api/v1/metrics/prometheus`.
