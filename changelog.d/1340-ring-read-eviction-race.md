### Fixed (2026-09-20 — #1340: OffHeapRingBuffer: a concurrent eviction makes a read return ANOTHER offset's record)
- **A ring read racing the wrapping writer returned the record of `offset + capacity` under the requested offset's label** — well-formed, decodable and silently wrong; `read` checked the tail once and then copied slots with no re-check, so a slot reclaimed mid-copy was read after its overwrite. Measured at rc4 on a 64-slot ring: 3,499 of 23,984 tail reads torn.
- `read` and `readSlice` now re-read the tail AFTER the copy and fail `CursorExpired` when it has passed the requested offset — the same typed refusal a read that arrived after the eviction already gets, so `PartitionedStreamAccess` reroutes it to the sealed tier as before. The writer publishes the advanced tail with a store-store fence before overwriting the slot, and the new head with a release fence after the slot's stores, so the post-copy check holds under the JMM rather than only on x86 `[mechanism: store-store fence after the tail store / acquire fence after the copy — the message-passing pair; arm64 mutation reds 3/4 (fence removed) and 1/6 (fence moved before the tail store)]`. The race itself is `[verified: aether/aether-stream/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/aether/stream/OffHeapRingBufferReadEvictionRaceTest.java — unit-level, x86 and arm64]`. Head-side ordering (a reader seeing the new head before the slot's index entry) is `[mechanism: release fence before the head store, acquire fence after the head read]` — not inducible on the x86 host where CI runs.
- **A data ring that is EXACTLY full with its tail at a non-zero position overwrote the tail record without evicting it** (found in review): the space check reconstructed the live byte count from two ring-relative positions and read `headEnd == tailDataPos` as empty, so `read(tail)` returned the newest record under the tail's offset — the same symptom, no race. The ring now keeps the tail's absolute data position in its header and counts live bytes exactly (`dataWritePos - dataTailPos`) `[verified: aether/aether-stream/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/aether/stream/OffHeapRingBufferExactlyFullTest.java — unit-level]`.
