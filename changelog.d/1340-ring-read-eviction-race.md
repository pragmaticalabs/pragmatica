### Fixed (2026-09-20 — #1340: OffHeapRingBuffer: a concurrent eviction makes a read return ANOTHER offset's record)
- **A ring read racing the wrapping writer returned the record of `offset + capacity` under the requested offset's label** — well-formed, decodable and silently wrong; `read` checked the tail once and then copied slots with no re-check, so a slot reclaimed mid-copy was read after its overwrite. Measured at rc4 on a 64-slot ring: 3,499 of 23,984 tail reads torn.
- `read` and `readSlice` now re-read the tail AFTER the copy and fail `CursorExpired` when it has passed the requested offset — the same typed refusal a read that arrived after the eviction already gets, so `PartitionedStreamAccess` reroutes it to the sealed tier as before. The writer publishes the advanced tail with a store-store fence before overwriting the slot, and the new head with a release fence after the slot's stores, so the post-copy check holds under the JMM rather than only on x86 `[verified: aether/aether-stream/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/aether/stream/OffHeapRingBufferReadEvictionRaceTest.java]`. Head-side ordering (a reader seeing the new head before the slot's index entry) is `[mechanism: release fence before the head store, acquire fence after the head read]` — not inducible on the x86 host where the test runs.
