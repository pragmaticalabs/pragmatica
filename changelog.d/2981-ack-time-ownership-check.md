### Fixed (2026-11-10 — #2981 direction B: a deposed but live stream owner could acknowledge a record the new owner never held)
- **A stream owner that had not yet applied its own deposal still acknowledged writes.** It admitted appends from its stale view and gathered confirmations from its OLD in-sync replica set, while the new owner, whose catch-up probe is a snapshot, could already have activated without them: an acknowledged record lost (N2, found by the TLA+ model of the stream protocol, invariant `AckedNeverLost`; not reproduced at runtime).
- At `confirmation_factor` ≥ 2 an acknowledgement now completes only if, after a no-op consensus round that starts once the replicas confirmed, the committed ownership record still carries the ownership the append was admitted under (owner, owner epoch, ownership term, transfer stamp) and names this node. Otherwise the writer gets an unknown outcome: `PublishOutcomeUnknown` for a local or forwarded publish (origin: the new `StreamError.OwnershipMovedBeforeAck`), `ReplicationBarrierUnmet` for an entity append, the same as when the round times out; a retry with the same message ID is routed to the new owner and may append a duplicate (the stream contract's at-least-once retry). This holds for a planned move and for the failover of an owner that is still running, independently of the election rule `[mechanism: Rabia applies in one order, so a round that starts after the acknowledgement resolved sees every ownership change committed before it; StreamPartitionManager.awaitReplication → AckOwnershipCheck; pinned by aether/aether-stream/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/aether/stream/AckTimeOwnershipCheckTest.java and aether/node/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/aether/node/EntityLogAckOwnershipCheckTest.java]` `[design intent — unverified at runtime]`.
- **Cost:** one consensus round per resolved batch of acknowledgements per partition, never per record: every acknowledgement one replica ack releases shares a round, an acknowledgement that resolves while a round is in flight waits for the next one, and at most one round per partition is in flight `[mechanism: BatchedAckOwnershipCheck; pinned by AckTimeOwnershipCheckTest]`. A deposed owner on the minority side of a network partition can no longer acknowledge at all at `confirmation_factor` ≥ 2, because its round cannot commit.
- Limits: `confirmation_factor` 1 runs no check and keeps its documented loss window. For an owner that CRASHED, what it acknowledged before the move is held by the new owner only because the election picks inside its ISR and the maximal ISR covers every committed member, where #2993 is a known gap. The deposed owner still appends until it applies the move (it only cannot acknowledge); cutting admission with a lease is deferred.
