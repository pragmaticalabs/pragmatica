### Added (2026-11-20 — #3303: one capture of the final fold of a closed parent)
- **`EntityFold.capture` answers the contents, claim and lineage of a closed parent's final fold from one instance, or a typed refusal** (#3303, A4b). It refuses unless the partition's rebuild resolved successfully, the log head is the closed offset, and the published fold is applied through exactly that offset, committed through it and current; all three fold facts are re-read after the copy, so a cut or an advance during the copy refuses. It never saves or offers a checkpoint, and nothing calls it yet. No wire change. [verified: `EntityFoldCaptureTest`]
