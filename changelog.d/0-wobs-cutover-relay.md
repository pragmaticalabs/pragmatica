### Changed (2026-10-29 — workers run on the replica feed, with a governor relay)
- **Workers now hold a full replica of the cluster state, fed by the core replica feed, and a community's governor relays it to its members.** A worker follows its community's announced governor when the governor is serving and a core otherwise; a node that becomes the governor re-points to a core, and serves its members only when it is current against that core. A governor that refused, went silent or failed is left alone for 10 s before it is asked again. Worker readiness, the app HTTP admission gate, the drain gate and the governor's eligibility now come from the replica readiness alone; the metadata projection no longer polls or drives anything, and the engine latch `authorizePassiveClient` and the `ReplicaCutover` switch are gone. A replica that re-parents from its own anchor keeps its already counted heartbeats, so a governor dying does not by itself drop its members' readiness; they age out under the 30 s bound. A node that stops serving now dismisses its subscribers with `NOT_SERVING`, and a source whose store was reset (it is stopping or restarting empty) answers `NOT_SERVING` instead of `INCARNATION_MISMATCH`, so its subscribers keep their position and resume from their anchor on another source instead of re-taking a snapshot.
