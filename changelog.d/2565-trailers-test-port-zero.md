### Fixed (2026-11-02 — #2565: AppHttpServerTrailersTest.chunkedRequest_withTrailers_reachesTheLocalSliceWithThem flakes: Connection refused under load)
- **Two app-listener tests lost their port under load and reported it as "Connection refused".** `AppHttpServerTrailersTest` and `AppHttpServerForwardNotAppliedTest` probed a port free on loopback, released it, and bound it later on every address. Another socket could take the port in between, and both tests discarded the failed start. Test-only change: each listener now binds port 0, the test reads back the port it really bound, and a failed start fails the test with its cause. [verified: `aether/node` `AppHttpServerTrailersTest`, `AppHttpServerForwardNotAppliedTest`] [mechanism: the kernel picks a free port inside the bind, so no other socket can take it between a probe and the bind]
