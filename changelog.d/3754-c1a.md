### Fixed (2026-11-26 — #3754: a deposed leader could terminate or create a node after losing leadership)
- **The capacity terminate and the create's dispatch are now fenced behind one activation accessor and a witnessed leader transaction.**
  `CapacityControlledLifecycle` reads (leader present, leadership generation) through a single synchronized accessor at submit and again in the
  continuation; the external effect is issued only after a leader transaction with an empty mutation list and the stored leader as its read
  witness is accepted, and only if the elapsed time since submit does not exceed the configured apply timeout (`WitnessExpired` otherwise).
  The applier's leader equality refuses a witness naming another leader.
