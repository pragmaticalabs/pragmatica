### Fixed (2026-09-14 — #280: retry was idempotency-blind, logging leaked request/result content, metrics flags were ignored)
- **R26 — the retry interceptor retried every failure.** `RetryMethodInterceptor` handed each call to core `Retry`, which stops only on `Cause.Terminal`; a slice's own business verdict (`InsufficientFunds`) is unclassified and was re-driven as eagerly as a timeout, on methods that may not be idempotent. The interceptor cannot tell a business failure from an infrastructure one by origin, so retry is now **opt-in by classification**: core gains `Cause.Transient` (`isTransient()`, the mirror of `Cause.Terminal`; `Causes.transientCause(String)` beside `Causes.terminal`), `RetryConfig` gains `retry_on` — `TRANSIENT` (default: only a cause that declares itself transient is retried) or `NON_TERMINAL` (the behaviour before this change, for a method whose failures are all infrastructural but not yet classified) — and **core `Retry` gains `execute(operation, Predicate<Cause> retryable)`, the policy evaluated on EVERY failure inside the loop**: a failure the policy refuses ends the call at once with that cause, on attempt one or attempt N alike, at DEBUG (neither the loop giving up nor a terminal verdict, which stay WARN). The interceptor is `retry.execute(() -> method.apply(request), retryOn::retries)`; `max_attempts` counts calls at the method, the first included. (Round 1 applied the policy to the first failure only and then handed the budget to the plain loop, so a business verdict on attempt two was re-driven to the budget — review of #1088, B1.) - **BEHAVIOUR CHANGE.** A retry-intercepted method whose failure is an UNCLASSIFIED cause is no longer retried; only a `Cause.Transient` is. Existing `@Retry`-style declarations over unclassified causes stop retrying unless the section sets `retry_on = "NON_TERMINAL"` (explicit opt-in — retrying an unclassified failure is a decision, never a default, the #665 rule) or the cause is classified. When the policy declines a non-terminal cause it logs ONE DEBUG line through SLF4J (`org.pragmatica.aether.resource.interceptor.RetryMethodInterceptor`) naming the cause type and the policy — on whichever attempt it happens — so a retry that "stopped working" is findable without being a per-business-failure WARN. [verified: `RetryInterceptorPredicateTest` — default declines an unclassified cause (1 attempt), retries a `Cause.Transient` (3), `NON_TERMINAL` retries the unclassified (3), a `Cause.Terminal` is never retried under either policy (1 each); reverting the policy or the budget guard reddens the named pin] `RetryConfigTomlBindingTest`'s end-to-end fixture failed with a generic cause whose MESSAGE said "transient" and whose type said nothing; it now implements `Cause.Transient` — the fixture says what the prose always claimed.
- **Classification pass — every cause family a retry interceptor can see, and what the default now does with it.** The space: causes a slice method can return at CALL time — its resource clients' errors, invocation errors when it calls another slice, core utility errors, and its own business verdicts. Node-internal, CLI, Forge, provisioning-time and config-binding families (`SliceLoadingFailure`, `ResourceProvisioningError`, `ConfigError`, `TopicConfigError`, `EntityProvisioningError`, `DeploymentError`, `AbTestDeploymentError`, WAL/segment/replication internals…) never reach a slice method's result and are out of the space.
