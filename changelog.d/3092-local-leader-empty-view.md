### Fixed (2026-11-19 — #3092: local-mode eviction of the leader with an empty eligible view kept the evicted leader)
- **A local-mode node no longer re-adopts an evicted leader when the eviction's eligible view is empty** (#3092). The stored view keeps its content under an empty view, so adoption re-elected the evicted node and the FSM then dropped the eviction as already applied, leaving `Led` on a node absent from the eligible view. An eviction with an empty eligible view now adopts nothing and goes straight to the FSM, which loses the leader and waits for re-election.
