### Changed (2026-11-13 — #2583 P2: stream forward handler reads the outcome of every reply it sends)
- **A refused or dropped forward reply was invisible**: the eight reply sends of `StreamForwardHandler` discarded the `WriteOutcome`, so a reply the transport did not accept surfaced only as a timeout at the requester.
- Every reply now reads its outcome. A reply that is not `Sent` moves the new `StreamReadForwardMetrics.recordReplyNotSent()` counter and opens one WARN per requester (closed by an INFO when replies are accepted again), never one WARN per message. A reply whose send promise failed is counted the same way. [verified: aether/aether-stream/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/aether/stream/forward/StreamForwardReplyNotSentTest.java]
- The eight `StreamForwardHandler` sites left the transport-outcome allow-list; a regression to a blind send fails `TransportOutcomeDisciplineTest`. [verified: aether/node/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/aether/node/TransportOutcomeDisciplineTest.java]
- A reply the transport provably did not write (`WriteOutcome.provablyNotSent()`) is re-sent after a `RefusalBackoff` hold (250 ms doubling, +-20% jitter, reset on `Sent`), at most four times (about 3.75 s, inside the requester's 5 s publish timeout). An ambiguous outcome (connection dead, a failed send promise) is never re-sent; the requester's own timeout and retry own that case. [verified: StreamForwardReplyNotSentTest]
- `StreamReadForwardMetrics.replyNotSent()` is the read side of the counter, on the instance the node assembly shares with the forward handler. A departed requester's open WARN episode is evicted on `MembershipDecision.NodeRemoved` (new `StreamForwardHandler.onNodeRemoved`, routed in `StreamAssembly`), so the per-requester state is bounded by the live cluster.
- A re-send is skipped, and its refusal opens no new episode, for a requester that left (`NodeRemoved`) since the reply was first sent; a fresh request from that node clears the mark.
