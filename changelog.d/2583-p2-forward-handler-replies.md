### Changed (2026-11-13 — #2583 P2: stream forward handler reads the outcome of every reply it sends)
- **A refused or dropped forward reply was invisible**: the eight reply sends of `StreamForwardHandler` discarded the `WriteOutcome`, so a reply the transport did not accept surfaced only as a timeout at the requester.
- Every reply now reads its outcome. A reply that is not `Sent` moves the new `StreamReadForwardMetrics.recordReplyNotSent()` counter and opens one WARN per requester (closed by an INFO when replies are accepted again), never one WARN per message. There is no re-send: the handler holds no requester deadline to bound one, and the requester's own timeout and retry own recovery. [verified: aether/aether-stream/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/aether/stream/forward/StreamForwardReplyNotSentTest.java]
- The eight `StreamForwardHandler` sites left the transport-outcome allow-list; a regression to a blind send fails `TransportOutcomeDisciplineTest`. [verified: aether/node/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/aether/node/TransportOutcomeDisciplineTest.java]
