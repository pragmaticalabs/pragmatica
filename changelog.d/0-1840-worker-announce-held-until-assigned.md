### Fixed
- A worker no longer announces itself to the cores' SWIM seeds before its activation directive is committed (#1840). The cores' membership scope admits no worker that is not a governor, so every ANNOUNCE round of the join loop (up to 60, 500 ms apart, per seed) was dropped on arrival. The worker's identity is still seeded at transport-ready; the announcement to the seeds is released when its own directive is committed. Cores announce at transport-ready as before.
- A held worker is now visible: the hold and the release are logged at INFO with the elapsed time, and a worker still without its directive after the community-absence window raises the operator warning `worker-assignment-overdue` (naming the node and the recovery), closed by `worker-assigned` when the directive commits.
