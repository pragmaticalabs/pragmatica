### Changed (2026-11-21 — #3458: a refused read names the cause of the removal)
- **`ReadRefusal` gained a `cause` component of the new `@Codec` enum `RemovalCause` (wire tag 3017), appended last, and `StreamError.CursorExpired` carries it beside its reason.** The wire generation is bumped: the join guard (`JoinGenerationGuard`) refuses a node of the new generation joining beside handshaken members of an older one, because they cannot decode the extra component; an old node rejoining a new-generation cluster is stopped only by the upgrade procedure.
- `RemovalCause` holds eleven removal causes (four retention, seven lost), `UNRECORDED` (a real removal whose raiser states no cause), `NOT_APPLICABLE` (a refusal that is not a removal) and `UNKNOWN` (reserved for decoding a code this node does not know).
- `CursorExpired.reasonOf(RemovalCause)` is the one switch from a cause to its reason, and the compact constructor refuses a pair that disagrees and `NOT_APPLICABLE`. The `(requested, tail)` and `(requested, tail, reason)` forms stay and pass `UNRECORDED`; decoding never throws, and every kind other than `CURSOR_EXPIRED` carries `NOT_APPLICABLE`.
- The raise sites that name their cause and the tests follow in two companion changes of the same merge unit.
