### Fixed (2026-10-18 — #2063: operator drain entries never leave DrainCommandRegistry)
- **A node that was drained, left and came back under the same id was told to drain again.** An operator-commanded drain stayed in the leader's `DrainCommandRegistry` for the life of the process: the only removal paths covered drains the topology manager started itself, and node ids come from config, so a rejoin under the same id inherited the old command. The entry is now cleared when the drain episode ends, on three membership FSM edges: into DEAD (the departure the drain was for), out of DEPARTING back to MEMBER (a higher-incarnation `SwimHealthy` refuted the drain, or the DEPARTING timeout withdrew an undelivered one), and out of DEAD (a drain commanded on an already-DEAD node is ignored by the FSM and left no edge of its own, so the rejoin inherited it). The hook is `AetherNode.clearOperatorDrainOnDeath`, called from the existing transition listener. `[verified: aether/node/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/aether/node/AetherNodeOperatorDrainClearTest.java drives a real MembershipFsm through each edge]`
