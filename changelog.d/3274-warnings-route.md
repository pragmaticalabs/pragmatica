### Added (2026-11-16 — #3274: operators could not read the open FSM warnings of a committed state)
- **`GET /api/v1/warnings` and `aether warnings` return the open FSM warnings of one committed state, with the answering core's committed revision.** Core nodes only, no forwarding. The core refuses (503, a distinct detail each) when it is a worker, holds no quorum, is at revision 0, or its revision label is not exact. Recovery: query another core or retry; the not-exact refusal clears at the core's next snapshot install. `[mechanism: one KVStore monitor hold reads revision, exact flag and rows together; tests WarningsRoutesTest, KVStoreExactLabelTest]`
- **KVStore now tracks whether its content is exactly the state at its committed revision (`exact`).** A restore that adopts a frontier past the installed content (`StateMachine.onPhaseAdopted`, called by the Rabia restore path) clears it; the next snapshot install sets it. Staleness of a served answer is the core's apply lag, unbounded.
