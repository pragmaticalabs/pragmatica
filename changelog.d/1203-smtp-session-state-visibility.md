### Fixed (2026-09-29 — #1203: `SmtpSession.state` was read across threads without a visibility guarantee)
- **`SmtpSession.state` is now `volatile`, like `channel` (#1185).** It is written on the Netty event loop and read by `onTimeout` on the timer thread, and the command timeout fires on every send, even after success. A stale read could re-run the terminal path on a finished session. [mechanism: JMM visibility; no deterministic test can observe a missing `volatile`]
- The outcome of a reply racing the timeout is unchanged and is decided by the promise's own compare-and-set (`PromiseImpl.resolve`), not by this field. A timeout that lands after DATA was accepted (while the session waits for the QUIT reply) still closes the socket, and it leaves the success standing. [verified: integrations/net/smtp/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/net/smtp/SmtpSessionTest.java — `TimeoutAfterSuccess`; an early return on a resolved promise leaves the socket open and fails this test]
