### Fixed (2026-09-13 — #1040: one blueprint stream declaration produced two live rings under two spellings)
- **The stream engine keyed slice-declared streams by the bare `resources.toml` section name while every management route addressed them by the qualified `namespace:stream:version` identity**, so `StreamConfigKey.streamConfigKey(qualified)` could never find the config the slice had committed under the bare name. The catalog path then correctly reached its documented "no committed entry" case and minted a second ring from management defaults. Measured on a live 5-node cluster: one declaration, two rings holding different events — bare `repl-failover-events` (1 partition, RF=2, honouring the blueprint) and `org.pragmatica.aether.test.test-stream-repl:repl-failover-events:1.0.0` (4 partitions, RF=1). A declared `min-sync-replicas = 2` durability contract was silently void for everything written through the catalog, and `GET /api/v1/streams` listed only the ring the application was not writing to.
- The slice/engine path now materializes a declared stream under its engine key. `StreamPublisherFactory` and `StreamAccessFactory` resolve the alias at the head of provisioning, and the declarative-consumer registration (`NodeDeploymentState.resolveStreamName`) resolves it the same way, so the publish side, the read side and the subscriber registration land on one ring. [verified: `aether/aether-stream/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/aether/stream/StreamEngineKeyQualificationTest.java`]
- The address is **looked up**, not re-derived: `BlueprintStreamAddresses` reads the alias→`ResourceAddress` bindings `BlueprintService` already publishes at deploy time, which is the same map the catalog identity descends from. Re-deriving the namespace from the slice's Maven coordinates would have pinned `ResourceVersion.defaultVersion()` for a blueprint declaring an explicit `version`, and would have namespaced an `External` (`source = …`) reference into the wrong namespace — relocating the divergence rather than removing it. [verified: `aether/node/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/aether/api/routes/StreamEngineKeyDivergenceTest.java`]
- The bare/qualified reduction had been written out twice (`StreamManager.engineKey`, `SystemStreams.isForbiddenEngineKey`) and applied on one side of the boundary only. It is now one function, `StreamEngineKey.engineKey`, that all three consumers call. [mechanism: single implementation; both former copies delegate]
- `system` streams keep their bare key. `SystemStreams.isForbiddenEngineKey` gates management-api writes on that exact spelling, so the reduction is a security boundary rather than a naming preference; it is pinned in both directions — the bare form is recognised, the qualified form is not. [verified: `StreamEngineKeyDivergenceTest.SystemNamespace`]
- **Behaviour change for cross-blueprint stream sharing.** Several blueprints each declaring a bare `[streams.X]` used to converge on one ring by accident; they now get one ring each. Sharing must be stated with `source = "<owner-namespace>:<stream>:<version>"` on the consuming blueprints — the existing `StreamResource.External` form. `examples/notification-hub` is migrated accordingly. Conversely, unrelated blueprints that happened to pick the same alias (`test-persistence` and `test-stream` both declare `test-events`) no longer collide.
- Not covered: a consumer declaring `version = "latest"` has no published binding, because `BlueprintService.resolveOwnedAddress` omits `Latest` specs — they have no concrete address until resolved against the live registry. Such a declaration still materializes under the bare alias. [design intent — unverified]
