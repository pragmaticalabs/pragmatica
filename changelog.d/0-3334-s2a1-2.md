### Changed (2026-11-20 — #3334: per-partition retention share and the extended adoption rule)
- **Behaviour change:** a stream's `max_bytes` and `max_count` are the whole stream's bounds, and each partition now applies its share, `bound / partitions` (never below 1), where each partition applied the whole bound before. A byte- or count-bounded stream with P partitions keeps about 1/P of what it kept before; a stream that relied on the old reading multiplies its bound by P. `max_age` and mode `all` (every declared bound exceeded, each on its share) are unchanged, and entity streams keep their node default age. [mechanism: `SealedRetentionRule.reclaimablePrefix` takes the partition count; `SealedRetentionRuleTest.PartitionShare`, and the enforcer arms in `DeclaredRetentionEnforcerTest`]
- A committed config of the same life now also replaces the enforced one when the partition count and durability are equal and its `[retention]`, `[hot]` or `expected_ingress` differs (before: only strictly stronger durability). Durability is compared per factor: **reversal of #1883**, a config that raises one factor and lowers the other (for example replication/confirmation 3/3 to 5/1) was adopted before and is now not adopted, and `STREAM_CONFIG_CHANGE_NOT_APPLIED` names it (ST 2.5 L251, "durability not weaker"). A config that changes an immutable key (`[truncation]`, `max_event_size`, `encryption_key_id`) is not adopted either, alone or riding with a retention, `[hot]` or `expected_ingress` change. The enforced per-partition share divides over the partition count of the enforced life, never over the count of a committed config that was not adopted. [mechanism: `StreamPartitionManager.adopts`, `committedRetention`; `StreamLifeAuthorityTest`, `CommittedRetentionTest`]
- `StreamRetentionSource.policyOf` and `StreamPartitionManager.committedRetention` answer an `EnforcedRetention` (the policy and the partition count) instead of a bare `RetentionPolicy`. No wire shape changes.
