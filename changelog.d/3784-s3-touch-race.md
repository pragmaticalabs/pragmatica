### Fixed (2026-11-26 — #3784: a touch whose block was deleted under it failed as "rejected")
- **A refused touch-marker PUT on a block deleted under the touch is `false`, not `Rejected`** (#3784). The S3 tier mapped any refused marker PUT to a terminal "check credentials" verdict, so a touch racing a delete failed in CI (S3 tests, round 3 of `deleteSentAtTheTouchWrite_neverResurrectsTheBlock`). The touch now looks at the block again after a refusal: gone means `false`, the contract for an absent block; still held keeps the refusal. The store's exact status was not captured (not reproduced in about 29,500 local races), so the fix does not depend on it.
