### Fixed (2026-11-05 — #2672: operator runbooks called management routes that do not exist)
- **The runbook `curl` commands now name real management routes.** `scaling.md` drained a node with `POST /admin/drain`; it is `POST /api/v1/nodes/drain/{id}` (optional `?force=true`). `troubleshooting.md` changed log levels with `POST /admin/logging` and a form body; it is `POST /api/v1/logging/levels` with a JSON `{"logger","level"}` body and `DELETE /api/v1/logging/levels/{logger}` to reset. Every unprefixed `/health`, `/metrics`, `/cluster/status`, `/slices`, `/routes`, `/thresholds`, `/alerts` and `/controller/config` call in the operator and slice-developer docs gained the `/api/v1` prefix, and the slice-route examples in `deployment.md` point at the application port (8070).
