### Changed (2026-11-01 — #2201 C-03: the stream memory budget is a config key with a memory-derived default; frozen rings regrow; floors keep headroom)
- **`[streaming] max_memory_bytes`** sets the node's off-heap stream memory budget. Unset, it is one eighth of the physical memory the JVM reports (container-aware), clamped to 128 MiB..1 GiB (the formula is a guess); before, it was a fixed 128 MiB, which holds about 50 default-retention partition rings. `STREAM_MAX_MEMORY_BYTES` still overrides it. `[verified: ConfigLoaderTest, StreamMemoryBudgetTest, StreamMemoryBudgetBootTest; EmberMemoryBudgetTripwireTest#bigStreamAtDefaultRetention_isFullyMaterializedOnThreeNodes (the C-03 real, its tripwire deleted)]`
- **A bad budget refuses to start instead of falling back.** A malformed or zero `STREAM_MAX_MEMORY_BYTES` refuses the boot naming the variable, where it silently became 128 MiB (and a negative one was accepted); a written `max_memory_bytes` or `seal_pending_cap_bytes` that is not a positive size refuses the load naming the key. `[verified: StreamMemoryBudgetBootTest#aetherNode_refusesBoot_whenStreamMaxMemoryBytesIsMalformed_beforeBindingAnyPort, ConfigLoaderTest]`
- **The sealer's pending-seal heap cap is decoupled from the budget**: `[streaming] seal_pending_cap_bytes`, by default the smaller of the budget and 256 MiB (a guess), so raising the off-heap budget does not raise the heap the sealer may hold past 256 MiB. `[verified: StreamMemoryBudgetBootTest#aBootedNode_holdsTheVariablesBudget_overTheKey_andItsSealerTheDecoupledPendingCap]`
- **A ring frozen by a refused growth grows again after budget is released** (a stream destroyed, a ring released or closed): the manager moves a freed generation on every release, and a frozen ring that sees it moved asks again at its next append that wraps, after handing over the few records still above its head, so every live record keeps its place. Before, a frozen ring never grew again. `[verified: StreamBudgetThawTest (NT-17)]`
- **Ring growth stops at three quarters of the budget; a ring's floor is admitted against all of it**, so a stream created after the rings have grown still finds room for its floors (the quarter is a guess). Rings that would have grown past the share freeze earlier and hand their oldest events to the sealer sooner. `[verified: StreamBudgetThawTest$FloorHeadroom; EmberStreamFloorHeadroomTest (NT-106, 3 nodes)]`
- **Known limits.** The 1 GiB ceiling is a sizing assumption, not a mechanism (see `configuration.md`). A host under about 1.3 GiB derives the 128 MiB floor, which holds 50 default-retention rings. A frozen ring regrows only at the append that wraps it, so a ring that is not appended to stays at its frozen size. The derivation trusts the JVM's container awareness of the memory MXBean `[unverified: not measured inside a container]`.
