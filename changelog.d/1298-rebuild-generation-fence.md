### Fixed (2026-09-19 — #1298, #1304: projection rebuild lost or double-applied events in flight across it)
- **A fold in flight when `Projection.rebuild()` ran wrote into the reset model under the OLD generation**, and the replay then applied the same event again (a counting fold read 2 for one event); on the single-argument path the late write carried state derived from the pre-rebuild model into the rebuilt one. And a write landing **between the rebuild's generation bump and its reset** was admitted under the new generation, wiped by the reset while its claim stayed DONE, and the replay then suppressed — the event silently missing (#1304). A live write after the reset could also apply out of order ahead of the replay.
- Rebuild is now capture → one-step reset → rewind. `ProjectionStore.resetToNewGeneration(range)` advances the generation, clears the model and enters REBUILDING in ONE indivisible step (`bumpGeneration` and `reset` are gone), so nothing lands between them. The cursor seam is now a `Projection.ReplayCursor` whose `capture()` reports the per-partition replay range before anything is touched and whose `rewind(range)` moves the cursor last.
- `ProjectionStore.write(key, state, generation, position)` is generation-fenced (a stale fold is refused as retryable `ProjectionError.StaleGeneration`; the guarded path fences on its claim's generation), and while REBUILDING admits a write only at its partition's next replay offset: a position already passed is acknowledged without writing, anything else is refused as retryable `ProjectionError.Rebuilding`. The replay is therefore the only writer during a rebuild and applies each captured offset once, in order; the generation goes LIVE when every partition passes its captured head. Ordering decides it, not timing. `[mechanism: one-step reset and replay-order admission in ProjectionStore]`, pinned in-JVM by `ProjectionTest` against an in-memory store.
- Stated limits: a refused live delivery that exhausts its retry budget is dead-lettered and applies when redriven; a replay offset that can never apply holds the rebuild in REBUILDING until the fold is fixed and the rebuild re-run; a failed rewind leaves REBUILDING until a rebuild completes. `[unverified: no production ProjectionStore backing or replay cursor exists yet]`
