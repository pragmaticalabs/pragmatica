### Fixed
- A graceful stop now runs the KV backup stop flush on every node that holds the full state, not only the leader: a whole-cluster stop demotes the leader as its peers go, and the node that acked a write need not be the leader, so a write acked at revision r reaches the backup head if at least one node that applied r completes its flush. A head strictly ahead within the same incarnation covers the node (no warning); a head at a higher incarnation, a failed push or an overrun still raises `BACKUP_STOP_FLUSH_FAILED`. A push that loses a race (including the server-side `failed to update ref` answer, previously not recognised as a rejection) is decided again until the 10 s stop deadline instead of twice; workers, which hold a replica without the cluster configuration, only stop (#2506).
