### Changed (2026-10-29 — #2201 PR-0b part 1: the sealed tier bounds what a stream declares; retention runs on every node; inert keys refused at activation; part of #2258)
- **BREAKING (pre-GA flag day): the stream config's `RetentionPolicy` gains a `declared` component (`RetentionDimensions`, wire tag 712).** The codec is positional, so old and new nodes cannot read each other's stream configs, and a new node cannot read a KV snapshot or backup taken before the upgrade. Upgrade by coordinated replacement and recreate the streams. `[verified: RetentionDimensionsWireTest (every declared set round-trips; bytes encoded at rc5 d6267fc9fa do not decode as written); the re-recorded wire baseline differs in exactly the RetentionDimensions TAG and SHAPE lines and the RetentionPolicy SHAPE line]`
- **Behaviour change: sealed-tier retention now bounds every dimension a stream DECLARES, in its retention mode (owner ruling Q6 = B; Q13 = A).** `retention = "count"` declares the count, `"time"` the age, `"size"` the bytes, `"compound"` each of `max_count`, `max_bytes`, `max_age` it names. Before, the leader applied one node-wide 24 h age to the segments it held, whatever each stream declared, so a `count` or `size` stream's sealed history was cut at 24 h and a `time` stream with a shorter age kept 24 h. Count and bytes are measured over the sealed segments newer than each segment (count by offset span), so the tier keeps at least the declared count or bytes; only a prefix is reclaimed, never past a hole or the entity checkpoint floor. A section with no `retention` key, an API-created stream and an `entity:` stream declare nothing and keep the node default age (24 h), today's rule; a durable topic and its dead-letter stream declare their age only, so their ring-sizing count and bytes do not cut them. `[verified: SealedRetentionRuleTest; DeclaredRetentionEnforcerTest; StreamConfigParserTest$DeclaredRetention; DurableTopicSubstrateTest]`
- **Behaviour change: retention runs on every node over its own sealed segments, from node start to node stop.** It was started only on the leader (`StreamingCoordinator.activate`), so a non-leader's disk was never reclaimed by retention, and a node that lost and regained the leadership never ran retention again. `[verified: StreamingCoordinatorTest (reactivation); EmberSealedTierRetentionTripwireTest (3 nodes, each reclaims its own copy)]`
- **A stream a node has no readable retention policy for is withheld, never reclaimed by a default.** Retention reads the committed config the node has applied; a node that has not applied it yet (just restarted, lagging), or whose build cannot read the stream's retention mode, reclaims nothing of that stream and raises `stream-retention-withheld` after two passes, closed by `stream-retention-resumed`. A rebuilt segment's size is learned in the same block read that learns its age, so the byte bound applies after a restart. A segment whose age cannot be read on any pass raises `stream-segment-unreadable` once (closed by `stream-segment-unreadable-reclaimed`) and takes the age of the next newer segment instead of holding the history for ever.
- **Behaviour change: the ring age tick (PR-0c) now sweeps only streams that declare an age** (#2201 I-12): `time` streams, `compound` streams naming `max_age`, durable topics and their dead-letter streams. `size`-typed, API-created and cluster-events streams are no longer swept by age: their 24 h was a filled-in default. `[verified: RingAgeTickTest]`
- **Behaviour change: a stream key or a consumer key with no runtime effect is refused at slice activation as it is at deploy** (#2201 E-02): `encryption_key_id`, `compression` other than `none`, `auto_offset_reset` other than `earliest`, `consistency = "strong"`, and any non-default consumer key (`batch_size`, `processing`, `on_failure`, `checkpoint_interval`, `max_retries`, `dead_letter`, `read_preference`). The deploy validator and the activation binder judge them by one table (`InertKeyRules`), so a configuration that reaches a node without passing the validator (pushed through the KV layer) no longer binds with a key that silently does nothing. `[verified: InertStreamConfigParityTripwireTest (11 cases, flipped from tripwires); StreamSectionBindingTest$InertKeysAreRefused]`
- Not yet: the deletion of an expired segment's block from the shared DHT tier, and the bound on that tier (#2258, PR-0b parts 2 and 3). Retention drops the segment's ref and index entry as before; the DHT copies remain.
