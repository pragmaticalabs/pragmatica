### Fixed (2026-10-31 — #1875: SegmentSealer retries outlived their manager's close)
- **A stopped node's segment sealer kept retrying against its closed WALs.** Nothing closed the sealer, and its retry pauses were scheduled without a handle, so seals of a stopped node went on failing with "Partition WAL is closed" WARNs. Node stop now closes it after its bounded stop seal: the age timers and retry pauses are cancelled, nothing failed is retried, and a failure after close is not a WARN. `[verified: SegmentRunFormingTest#closeCancelsScheduledSealRetries]`
- **The ring index-corruption test's log capture is pinned to its own logger.** The JVM-wide capture the issue names was already replaced by #2313; a regression test now emits another logger's WARN while the capture is open. `[verified: OffHeapRingBufferIndexCorruptionTest#indexCorruptionLogCapture_ignoresOtherLoggers]`
