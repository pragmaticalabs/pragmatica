### Changed (2026-10-19 — part of #249: unknown storage sub-sections are refused at load)
- **A `[storage.<instance>.<sub>]` section that is not `[storage.<instance>.shared]`, or a `[storage.encryption.<sub>]` other than `[storage.encryption.keys]`, now refuses the config at load**, naming every such section. Before, `[storage.<a>.<b>]` was read as a storage instance literally named `<a>.<b>`, and an unknown `[storage.encryption.<sub>]` was ignored. A typo such as `[storage.content.shard]` or `[storage.encryption.kyes]` no longer boots without the tier or the keys it meant. **Config-breaking:** a config carrying such a section must drop or rename it. [verified: `aether/aether-config` `ConfigLoaderSharedTierTest$SubSections`]
- A shared-only instance's paths are derived once, when the node starts, and every reader uses them: `[storage.artifacts.shared]` alone keeps the stream segments and WAL under the default root instead of the working directory. [verified: `aether/node` `MainDerivedStorageTest`]
