### Fixed (2026-09-14 — #1151: `PiggybackBuffer.peekUpdates` drained the deque non-atomically, so readers saw an empty buffer mid-tick and the isolation-era expiry could miss an in-flight FAULTY verdict)
- **A peek emptied the buffer for every other thread.** `peekUpdates` `pollFirst`ed every entry out of the `ConcurrentLinkedDeque`, aged it, and re-added the survivors only after the loop, with no lock. The class doc said "thread-safe", which was true of each call and false of what a concurrent reader observed: between the drain and the requeue, `size`, `faultyCount` and `expireFaultyUpdates` all saw nothing. The probe tick peeks every period and the transport thread peeks on every inbound ping, so the window opened continuously. Measured on the buffer alone: a peek-only ticker against a `faultyCount()` reader returned 0 on 61% of 8.5M reads with one FAULTY entry buffered and never evicted. [mechanism: `SwimProtocol.probeTarget`, `handlePing`, `relayPingReq` peek on the tick and transport threads; `clearIsolationOnReconnect` expires on the rejoin path]
- **The product consequence is the S06 hazard the expiry exists to prevent.** `expireFaultyUpdates` racing a peek did not see the FAULTY verdict the peek was holding; the peek then re-added it, and an isolation-era death verdict survived the rejoin purge to be gossiped into the healed cluster. The `dropped` count it reported was also wrong under the race (`before - after` spanned the drain).
- **Every buffer operation now runs under the buffer's monitor**, and the deque is a plain `ArrayDeque`: `addUpdate`, `peekUpdates`, `size`, `faultyCount` and `expireFaultyUpdates` are mutually exclusive, so a peek — age and requeue included — is atomic to every other thread. Chosen over an immutable-snapshot CAS rebuild because a peek mutates every entry's dissemination count and rotates the queue, so a snapshot would be rebuilt and retried on every tick and every inbound ping for no gain; the only contention is the probe tick against the transport thread at protocol cadence, where an uncontended monitor costs nanoseconds. Correctness wins on a path that is not hot. [verified: `PiggybackBufferConcurrencyTest.faultyCount_neverReadsZero_whileAFaultyEntryIsBuffered_underAConcurrentPeekTicker` — 1M concurrent reads, 0 read 0; `expireFaultyUpdates_racingAPeek_dropsTheFaultyVerdict_everyRound` — 20k rounds, 0 verdicts survive expiry and `dropped` is 1 in every round. Both red at the previous head: 60–77% zero-reads, 7–20 surviving verdicts per 20k rounds; both red again with the `synchronized` removed]
- **The class doc's thread-safety claim now states the guarantee it earns:** atomic operations under one monitor, never an observable mid-peek state.
