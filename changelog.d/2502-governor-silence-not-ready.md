### Fixed (2026-11-19 — #2502: a silent governor cost a member about 2.7 s of not-ready)
- **A deliberate move to a governor no longer arms the readiness carry window** (#2502). The carry window (old heartbeats kept for a bounded grace across a re-parent) was armed on every resume, including a deliberate rebalance to the announced governor; when that governor granted and then stayed silent with its link up, the window expired before the silence fall-back to a core, was never renewed, and the member was not ready until the core beat. The window is now armed only when the previous session failed; after a deliberate move the old heartbeats stay counted on their ordinary 30 s freshness bound. Renewal is one rule: any resume while a window is armed renews it up to the cap, so a deliberate move made while a failure-armed window runs does not let it lapse before that move's own silence fall-back. Also pins the serve-gate term of a governor's members to `servable` (a test now goes red if `ready` is substituted).
