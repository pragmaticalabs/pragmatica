### Fixed (2026-11-14 — #3173 #3174: Ember cluster stop reported success while a node still held its ports)
- **`EmberCluster.stop()` no longer succeeds while a node's stop is unresolved.** A node whose stop expires its bound is still running, with its QUIC port bound; the cluster stop now fails naming it. Stops already in flight on nodes removed from the registry (`killNode`, a self-drain) are tracked until they resolve, and `stop()` awaits them too. A retried `stop()` after a failure is not cleanup: it awaits the leaked node's pending stop and fails naming it again until that stop resolves.
- **A self-exiting node is reported (`exitCodeOf`) only after its stop resolved, however late.** A second exit report for a node whose stop is pending waits for that stop; a stop that resolves after its bound still records the exit. `killNode` keeps swallowing the bound expiry. Harness-only (Ember).
