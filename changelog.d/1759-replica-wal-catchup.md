### Fixed (2026-11-08 — #1759: an owner behind a budget-deferred replica could not catch up from it)
- **A replica that held a partition without a ring (paced, or deferred for off-heap budget) answered a registered replica's catch-up read as "not materialized".** An owner whose only source was such a replica stayed behind and could not activate. The replica now serves the read from its own WAL, read-only: no ring is created and no off-heap budget is taken. The page is shipped with the WAL's own owner-epoch history. [verified: `aether/aether-stream/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/aether/stream/ReplicaWalCatchupTest.java`] at the node level (replica handler to owner backfill in one JVM); the multi-node Ember form needs a per-node off-heap budget the harness does not have yet [design intent — unverified on a live cluster].
- **New read-only WAL scan** (`AppendLog.readRange`, `Opener.readRange`/`readEpochHistory`, `StorageInstance.readLogRange`/`readLogEpochHistory`): nothing is opened, recovered or cut, so it is safe beside a later materialization of the same log. A torn or damaged frame before the page is full fails the whole page (`WalError.LogDamaged`), never a partial range; a page after the first continues from the previous page's end position, and a stale position falls back to a full scan. [verified: `integrations/storage/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/storage/AppendLogReadRangeTest.java`]
- **Owner-side lineage clip.** An owner that catches up from a replica installs only the records within its committed epoch lineage: records a later committed epoch re-assigned (`AlignedRecovery#reassignedFrom`) are served but not installed. [verified: `aether/aether-stream/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/aether/stream/OwnerCatchupCommittedLineageTest.java`]
- Recovery for a damaged WAL on the replica: the read answers unavailable and the owner tries another source; materializing the partition on the replica reopens the log and cuts a torn tail.
