### Fixed (2026-10-18 — #2240: Hetzner list calls read only the first page)
- **Past 25 entries the client saw a silent prefix of the inventory.** `HetznerClient`'s list calls issued one GET and discarded `meta.pagination`; the destroy sweep, the CTM inventory and genesis discovery sit on them, and the sweep is the net for what the ledger missed. All eight list calls (servers, SSH keys, networks, firewalls, load balancers, floating IPs, including the label-selector variants) now follow `next_page` to the end and concatenate the pages; a failing later page fails the call, and so does a `next_page` that repeats or goes backwards or a listing past the 100-page cap (`HetznerError.PaginationFailed`, naming the endpoint and the pages read); a truncated list is never returned as success. `[verified: integrations/cloud/hetzner/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/cloud/hetzner/HetznerClientTest.java PaginatedLists]`
- `api/Pagination` now binds Hetzner's snake_case fields. Retry and 429 handling are not part of this change (#1839).
