### Added (2026-11-20 — #2835: A4 inventory fields on `inspectLog`, for AD7)
- **`AppendLog.inspect` / `StorageInstance.inspectLog` now report the owner-epoch inventory of a log read-only**, beside the WAL extent: `lastEpoch` (the key of the last history entry a held record reaches; a ghost entry above the head never ranks, as in the provenance comparison), `firstStart` (the first entry's start, `-1` for no history) and `historyDigest` (`AppendLog.historyDigestOf`: SHA-256 hex over `<token> <startOffset>\n` of every entry, oldest first, ghosts included; an empty history digests the empty input). `low`/`head` stay the WAL's on-disk offsets. A damaged epoch sidecar fails the look with `EpochHistoryCorrupt`. The volume is left byte-identical. `LogExtent` keeps its four-argument constructor (no history).
