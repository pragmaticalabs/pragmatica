### Fixed (2026-11-23 — #3588: adoptedViewSequence dropped on voter readmission and let an older commit displace the leader)
- **A readmitted voter lowered its adopted leader sequence and then followed a queued older commit** (#3588, finding A). A passive core adopts the `LeaderKey` record's own sequence when it pulls it, which can be ahead of the sequence it has observed through pushes. On readmission, `AwaitingKvSync` re-adopted the held leader as a sequence-less commit and recorded only the observed sequence. The `Led` swap fence then let a queued push for an older leader swap `Led` to it until the newer push arrived. If consensus was not yet ready, `QuorumWaiting` adopted that older push outright.
- Every adoption now takes one staleness rule, `Passive`'s since #3581: a commit with a real sequence that is below the adopted one, or equal to it for another leader, is skipped. `QuorumWaiting`, `AwaitingKvSync`, `Electing` and `ReElecting` now apply it, and `Led`'s FIX-2 fence already did. Every adoption also records the sequence by one rule, `max(adopted, commit, observed)`, so the adopted sequence never drops within a node's life. That includes the `Led` swap on a sequence-less commit, which used to reset it to 0.
