### Fixed (2026-11-23 — #3588: adoptedViewSequence dropped on voter readmission and let an older commit displace the leader)
- **A readmitted voter lowered its adopted leader sequence and then followed a queued older commit** (#3588, finding A). A passive core adopts the `LeaderKey` record's own sequence when it pulls it, which can be ahead of the sequence it has observed through pushes. On readmission, `AwaitingKvSync` re-adopted the held leader as a sequence-less commit and recorded only the observed sequence. The `Led` swap fence then let a queued push for an older leader swap `Led` to it until the newer push arrived. If consensus was not yet ready, `QuorumWaiting` adopted that older push outright.
- **The KV pull adopted the `LeaderKey` record without its sequence** (#3588, F1). Outside `Passive`, a pulled record was adopted as a sequence-less commit, so the adopted sequence stayed below the record's, and a queued older push for another leader then passed the `Led` swap fence. A record older than the adopted leader was adopted outright too. Every KV pull (the `QuorumWaiting` and `AwaitingKvSync` entries, the grace timeout, `Electing` and `ReElecting`) now carries the record's own sequence, and a record older than the adopted leader is never followed up, so a waiting state goes on to its own no-leader branch (readiness poll, grace timer, election) instead of stopping at a refused adoption.
- Every adoption now takes one staleness rule, `Passive`'s since #3581: a commit with a real sequence that is below the adopted one, or equal to it for another leader, is skipped. `QuorumWaiting`, `AwaitingKvSync`, `Electing` and `ReElecting` now apply it to pushes and KV pulls alike, and `Led`'s FIX-2 fence already did. Every adoption also records the sequence by one rule, `max(adopted, commit, observed)`, so the adopted sequence never drops within a node's life. That includes the `Led` swap on a sequence-less commit, which used to reset it to 0.
