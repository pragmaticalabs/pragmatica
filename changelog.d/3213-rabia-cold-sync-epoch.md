### Fixed (2026-11-14 — #3213: a laggard genesis voter could adopt a phase past a roster change from a COLD sync answer)
- **A COLD sync answer keeps the configuration its own engine persisted** (#3213). A Syncing or Stopped responder answers from its persisted state, and that answer always dropped the voter configuration. A phase past a roster change therefore read as genesis-epoch evidence: a voter that missed the change, still at epoch 0, adopted it from one COLD answer and could decide the next slot under the old roster while the new roster decided it too. State the responding engine persisted itself now keeps the configuration it was saved under. A receiver at a lower epoch then sees a newer epoch, which only a LIVE responder authorizes, and waits for one. A backup loaded across a restart still travels without its configuration (#1526).
