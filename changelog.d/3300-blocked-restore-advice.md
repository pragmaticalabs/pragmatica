### Fixed (2026-11-16 — #3300: the blocked-restore warning advised an exit that does not work)
- **`BACKUP_RESTORE_BLOCKED` advised `restore = "fresh"` even over a committed `IN_PROGRESS` restore marker**, where a restart with it changes nothing (a resumed restore never reads `restore`). The warning now names the step that failed (source unreachable, ref unresolved, backup vanished, commit unreadable, document undecodable, history unreadable, incarnation missing) and the exits, chosen by one fact: is a restore marker committed? With none, fix what the step names (the next retry reads the source again) or restart with `restore = "fresh"`. With one, the pinned commit is named; a restart of any subset of the nodes with `restore = "fresh"` does not abandon it, the restore resumes once what the failed step names is readable again at the source, and if it cannot be repaired there, the exit is to stop ALL nodes and cold-start with `restore = "fresh"` (discards the partly restored state, whole-cluster stop). The warning does not predict whether a retry or a source fix will clear the block. The cause type is now `RestoreError.Blocked`, which carries the failed step. The warning is raised again whenever its text would differ (the failed step or the marker state changed), not when it repeats; a failure arriving after a terminal restore decision was committed is logged at debug only and raises no warning. The runbooks (`backup-recovery.md`, `incident-response.md`) carry the same two cases. The abandon mechanism is #3305. `[verified: BackupRestoreCoordinatorTest.Blocked]`
