### Fixed (2026-11-25 — #2791 part 1: slice-route error responses were not all `application/problem+json`)
- **Every error a slice route answers is now `application/problem+json`, on Aether and on Terra** (#2791, part 1). The router wrote its RFC 9457 bodies as `application/json` (route miss 404, 405, a version-header 400/404, a Cause failure, a validation 422, an unexpected exception 500, an auth refusal with its `WWW-Authenticate`), a response body that could not be serialised was a `text/plain` 500, Terra answered a route miss and a failed dispatch as `text/plain`, a refused admission as `{"error":...}` JSON, and the Aether node's multipart worker failure as `{"error":...}` JSON. All of them now carry a `ProblemDetail` body from the one generated encoder (`ProblemDetailJson.CODEC`; the node's three Jackson encodes and their plain-text fallback are gone), with the status, headers and client text each answer had before. The `type` member is unchanged (`about:blank`): a stable `type` is part 2. Not covered: the listener's request gates (malformed request, body over the limit, request timeout) answer before routing in `http-server` and stay `application/json {"error":...}`; a test pins that as a tripwire.
