### Added (2026-10-28 — #774: dynamic configuration, `Watched<T>` / `Live<T>` engine — types, change feed, release hooks)
- **`Watched<T>` and `Live<T>` slice-api types, and the node's `ConfigChangeFeed`.** `Watched.watch(callback)` runs the registration delivery on the calling thread (the section's record at registration) and then delivers every change to the section as a whole record: serial per subscription, coalesced to the latest, nothing delivered when the record is unchanged. `Live` keeps the latest record for pull access. `ConfigFacade.watched(section, binder)` refuses by default (a no-op would only pretend to watch); a facade without a feed binds once. `DefaultConfigChangeFeed` is fed by the applier (`DynamicConfigManager.onApplied`) after the overlay has taken the change and binds each record from one consistent overlay state. [verified: `DefaultConfigChangeFeedTest`, `LiveTest`, `ConfigFacadeWatchedTest`, `ConfigWatchWiringTest`]
- **Subscriptions follow the slice's lifetime, not its activation.** They are keyed by the declaring slice artifact and released when that artifact is unloaded - before `stop()`, so a failing or timing-out `stop()` cannot skip the release - and when a slice's creation fails after it subscribed. Deactivation and suspension keep them. [verified: `SliceStoreConfigWatchReleaseTest`, `DependencyResolverConfigWatchTest`]
- **Operator events** `config-watch-callback-failed` / `config-watch-callback-recovered` (subject `<artifact>/<section>`): raised on the transition into a failing delivery and on its recovery; the callback timeout is `config.watch.callback.timeout` (seconds, default 30). Nothing yet declares `Watched` from a slice factory: code generation follows.
