### Fixed (2026-11-16 — #3299: Ember and Forge accepted a one-node cluster that can never elect a leader)
- **Ember and Forge refuse a cluster below three nodes at configuration, with a typed error that states the minimum.** `EmberConfig` (the factory, `[cluster] nodes` in `forge.toml`, the `Ember.cluster(n)` builder) and Forge's `CLUSTER_SIZE` / `--cluster-size` reject `nodes < 3` (`EmberConfig.MINIMUM_NODES`, the production floor `ClusterSizeGate.MINIMUM_SUPPORTED_CLUSTER_SIZE`, now public). A one-node cluster reported ready but logged `GUARD[empty-topology]` and never elected a leader, minted a genesis or deployed anything. Forge used to replace a rejected `CLUSTER_SIZE` with the five-node default; it now exits 1 with a FATAL naming the cause. Tests that built unstarted or fake-node `EmberCluster` instances of one or two nodes use three. Breaking (pre-GA): `nodes = 1` or `2` in a forge config is refused.
