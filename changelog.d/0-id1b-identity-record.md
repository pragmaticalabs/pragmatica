### Added
- The per-node identity record (membership overhaul ID-1b): `node-identity/<nodeId>` in the KV, written only by the committed leader by compare-and-set. It carries the lifecycle `INTENDED`, `PROVISIONING`, `ADMITTED`, `DEPARTED`, `TERMINATED`, so a provisioning intent can be committed before the provider call and ends admitted or terminated (`PROVISION_FAILED`, `PROVISION_TIMEOUT`, `CANCELLED`, `VANISHED`); `TERMINATED` is never removed. The leader records each core admission as a shadow (BootTokens still decides and the record is only compared to it), and every core writes an applied retirement to its own identity ledger before the apply returns. Nothing creates intents yet; nodes created at bootstrap or by hand are admitted as before. Workers do not replicate the record.
