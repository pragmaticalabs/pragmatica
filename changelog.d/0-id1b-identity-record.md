### Added
- The per-node lifecycle record (membership overhaul ID-1b, reworked as O3-1): `node-identity/<nodeId>` in the KV holds one value with two facets, the identity of the admitted process and the capacity reservation of the same node id, written only by the committed leader by compare-and-set against the whole value. A released capacity becomes a `FREED` tombstone (with its cause and time) instead of a deleted key, and the record is never deleted. The pure mutation builders (reserve, observe, refuse, free, admit, readmit, depart, terminate) carry the capacity-ledger count in the same transaction. Nothing writes the record yet and nothing reads it: the reservation key, its readers and the identity writer move onto it in the following steps. Workers replicate the record as they do the reservation.
