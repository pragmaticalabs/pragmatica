### Fixed
- **A storage write abandoned at the abort deadline no longer leaves an object or an open writer behind (#2876).** `IngestSink` marks itself ended at its first clean-up; a commit that completes afterwards deletes its object and stops the chain, a writer that is created afterwards is aborted at once, and the chain stops there. The state the clean-up and the late chain share is guarded by the sink's monitor. [verified: `IngestSinkTest` `abort_thenTheHung*`, red at the base]
- **The abort deadline is bounded even when the stall bound is zero.** A route idle bound of zero means "no stall timer", but the wait for a hung `accept` after the server ended the request is now at least 30 s (`BodyStream.ABORT_FLOOR_SECONDS`) instead of unbounded. Zero stays valid for the idle clock, so the config parse is unchanged. [verified: `RequestGateStreamTest.hungAccept_withAZeroBound_isStillAbandonedAtTheFloor`, red at the base]
- **A storage delete is bounded.** Every object delete the upload sink issues (clean-up, and the delete of a late commit) is abandoned after 30 s and logged as a failed delete, so a store whose delete never resolves holds neither the request's admission nor the abandoned chain; the object stays for the reclaimer (A4). [verified: `IngestSinkTest` `*withAHungDelete*`]
- **Docs:** `management-api.md` states that a zero `read_timeout` disables the idle clock only; the abort deadline keeps its 30 s floor.
