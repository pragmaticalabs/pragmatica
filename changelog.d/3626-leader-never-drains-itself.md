### Fixed (2026-11-24 — #3626: the leader could queue a drain of itself on the automatic paths)
- **Only the operator route refused draining the leader**: a replacement retirement (`REPLACED`) or the voter-excluded core trim (`OVERPROVISION_SCALE_DOWN`) of a core that had left the installed voters while it still led reached `drainNode` with the leader's own id, which enqueued the DRAIN command, raised DEPARTING for it and scheduled the grace-terminate backstop against it.
- `ClusterTopologyManager.drainNode` now refuses its own node's id first, for every reason, with the typed `LeaderCannotDrainItself` naming the node and the remedy (move leadership first, then drain); nothing is queued and no backstop is scheduled. The operator route keeps its earlier `LeaderCannotBeDrained` (409): its drain goes through the drain port, not `drainNode`, and it also refuses the leader as seen by a follower.
