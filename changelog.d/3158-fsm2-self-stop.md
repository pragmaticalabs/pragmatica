### Fixed (2026-11-13 — #3158: FSM2 node ignored its own SHUTTING_DOWN lifecycle decision)
- **Under the FSM2 deployment engine a node never stopped on a `MembershipDecision.NodeShuttingDown` naming itself.** The only self-stop was a `NodeDeploymentManager` hook registered as a LEGACY route, which FSM2 counts as a drop. The self-stop is now a node lifecycle route bound once in `AetherNode`, under either engine: it stops the node on a decision naming this node, and does nothing for a decision naming another node or for any other variant; a SHUTTING_DOWN that follows a DRAINING still stops. The manager's `onMembershipDecision` and `setShutdownCallback` (and the LEGACY route `B9.ndm.NodeShuttingDown`) are removed. `MembershipDecision` documents the variant as no longer emitted by the projector, so the route is dormant until a producer returns.
