### Fixed (2026-11-12 — #970: a blueprint apply that never resolved stayed IN_PROGRESS forever)
- **An FSM2 apply that does not resolve (every declared slice ACTIVE) within `[timeouts.deployment] apply_deadline` (default 15m; FSM2 only, LEGACY ignores the key) now reaches a terminal.** Under ALL_OR_NOTHING the apply is rolled back (ROLLED_BACK), under BEST_EFFORT it is closed FAILED with no rollback; both carry the cause `apply-deadline-exceeded` and the slices that are not ACTIVE, and remove the apply record. The clock is the leader's monotonic reading, is never reset by slice progress, restarts on a leader change or a resume, and is paused (up to `apply_schema_pause_max`, default 2h) while a PENDING or MIGRATING datasource of the blueprint holds the schema gate. An unreadable apply record or a non-positive deadline never terminates an apply (re-drive, never condemn). Known limit: leader tenures shorter than the deadline postpone it indefinitely, see #3120. `[mechanism: in-JVM harness test ApplyDeadlineTest; not the live path, FSM2 is test-only until F6]`
- **Operator:** the WARNING `apply-deadline-exceeded` (subject: the blueprint) joins the committed open set of FSM warnings and is closed by `apply-deadline-cleared` when a republish or a removal replaces the terminal. Recovery: republish or delete the blueprint. New keys `apply_deadline` and `apply_schema_pause_max` are validated (deadline positive, pause cap not negative). This is FSM2-core; it does not depend on the F4 reader/source switch. FSM2 is test-only until F6.
