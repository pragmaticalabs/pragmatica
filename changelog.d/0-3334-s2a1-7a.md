### Changed (2026-11-20 — #3334: streams created without TOML get the declaration defaults)
- **Durable topics and API-created streams resolve retention through the declaration defaults** (#3334). A durable topic keeps its 7 d age and now also declares `max_bytes = max(1 GiB, P x 2 x seal_merge_bytes)`; a stream created through the Management API (`POST /streams`, `POST /streams/{namespace}/{stream}/{version}`) with no declaration gets `max_age` 24 h and the same byte bound, both declared, where it had a 1 h / 4 MiB / 10,000-event window before. The request bodies accept the optional fields `maxAge` (a time span such as `"2h"`), `maxBytes` and `maxCount`; a field given is declared and bounds the stream, an absent one takes the default, and the pre-existing bodies bind unchanged. Entity logs keep no user bound. [mechanism: both create paths resolve through `DeclarationDefaults`; the enforced config carries the bounds and the declared dimensions] [verified: `StreamApiRoutesDeclarationDefaultsTest`, `DurableTopicRetentionDefaultTest`, `StreamEntityLogRetentionDefaultTest`, `StreamSectionBindingDeclarationTest`]
- **API-created and topic streams reserve a larger per-partition ring floor.** A stream created through the Management API (explicitly or by publish auto-create) now reserves about 2.66 MB per partition copy at creation, where it reserved about 0.5 MB, and its growth cap follows the new retention (1 GiB at the default) under the existing budget admission, until ring sizing is decoupled from retention. [mechanism: the ring is sized from the stream's retention count and bytes] [verified: `StreamApiRoutesDeclarationDefaultsTest`, `DurableTopicRetentionDefaultTest`]
