### Removed (2026-11-03 — #2617: dead `consensus_fast_path_total` metric and the round-1 fast-path docs)
- **The `fastPathCount` field and the `consensus_fast_path_total` Prometheus gauge are gone.** The Round-2-skipping shortcut was removed in #1390 and its counter in #2303, so the field always read 0. Breaking (pre-GA): the `consensus` block of `GET /api/v1/metrics/comprehensive` no longer carries `fastPathCount`, `/api/v1/metrics/prometheus` no longer exposes `consensus_fast_path_total`, and `RabiaMetrics` / `ManagementApiResponses.ConsensusMetricsResponse` lose the component. `RabiaMetricsCollectorTest.counterMap_carriesTheExactGaugeVocabulary` pins the remaining key set exactly.
- **Docs no longer claim a round-1 fast path.** `01-consensus.md` (table row and sequence diagram), `00-overview.md`, `aether-overview.md`, `feature-catalog.md` and `integrations/consensus/README.md` now say every decision needs round-2 evidence, with the mechanism (`PhaseData.processRound2Completion`, `RabiaEngine.canMakeDecision`) and the pinning tests.
