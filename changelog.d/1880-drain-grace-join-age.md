### Fixed (2026-11-19 — #1880: a configured node that joined late could be drained inside the drain-safety grace)
- **The drain-safety grace now measures time since the member joined** (#1880). Its age was stamped when the membership FSM first tracked the node, which for a configured seed is wiring time, so a seed that only became reachable long afterwards looked mature at once and could be drained as an over-provision scale-down seconds after joining. The age now runs from the first reachability evidence (the existing one-way latch) and is 0 while the member has not been reached.
