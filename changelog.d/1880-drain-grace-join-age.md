### Fixed (2026-11-19 — #1880: a configured node that joined late could be drained inside the drain-safety grace)
- **The drain-safety grace now measures time since the member joined** (#1880). Its age was stamped when the membership FSM first tracked the node, which for a configured seed is wiring time, so a seed that only became reachable long afterwards looked mature at once and could be drained as an over-provision scale-down seconds after joining. The age now runs from the first reachability evidence (the existing one-way latch) and is 0 while the member has not been reached. [verified: `LeaderReconcilerTest$DrainSafetyGrace`, `MembershipFsmTest$MemberAge` (red on the old age)]
- **Every drain of another node now passes the drain-safety grace at one point** (#1880). `ClusterTopologyManager.drainNode` refuses a drain whose target is younger than the grace, for every reason except `JOIN_GRACE_REAP` (a never-joined zombie) and `OPERATOR_COMMAND` (an operator decision). Before, only the reconciler's surplus selection asked the grace, so the voter-excluded core retirement (#3324) retired a just-joined core about 1.5 s after it joined, before its admission had finished; young ephemeral non-owners are no longer exempt either. The reconciler selects victims through the same rule, and its local grace filters are gone. [verified: `ClusterTopologyManagerActuatorTest$DrainSafetyGraceTable` (every reason x age), `LeaderDrainVictimTripwireTest` (red on the base: ldv-4..6 drained)]
