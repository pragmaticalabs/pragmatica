### Fixed (2026-11-16 — #3296: a placement-provisioned worker never reached READY for its operation under FSM2)
- **FSM2 reads community placement policies from the committed cluster config** (#3296). The `[community.<id>]` policies reached the FSM2 worker community choice only through a seam the node wired to an empty map, so every worker of a policy source took the derived `<source>-w-0` community; a placement operation's readiness gate (directive community equal to the operation's) never held and the operation stayed `AWAITING_READY`. The indexer now parses the policies with the committed `ClusterConfigKey` row, as it already did for `[community_policy]`, so the choice follows commit order: the policy commit precedes every placement operation derived from it and every node that operation provisions. The seam is removed. [verified: `CommittedPlacementPolicyTest` (red with the indexer line removed)]
