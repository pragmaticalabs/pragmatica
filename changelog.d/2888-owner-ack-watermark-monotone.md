### Fixed (2026-11-08 — #2888: replication owner accepted a lower ack and let a replica's watermark regress)
- **The owner kept whatever offset the last `ReplicateAck` carried.** `handleAck` overwrote the replica's confirmed offset, so a reordered or duplicated ack (transport retry, the #2882 resend) moved the watermark backwards; only the replica's own latest-ack check prevented it. The owner now refuses an ack below the row's confirmed offset when both were made under the same owner epoch (`ReplicaRegistry.advanceWatermark`); an equal offset is applied, and the persisted watermark follows the surviving row. An ack from an older epoch than the row's is refused whatever its offset; a newer epoch is a new lineage, so its first ack replaces the row, lower offset included. Other `updateWatermark` callers (backfill, rebuild) are unchanged. [verified: `OwnerAckWatermarkMonotoneTest`]
