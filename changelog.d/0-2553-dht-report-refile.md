### Fixed
- A node whose DHT replication report was dropped by the leader (it held the member `Dead`) now files it again once it is back as the same live process, while a replication change is open (#2553). Since #2545 a node dedupes against what it filed, not against the store, so a rejoined member never noticed its report was gone and the open change stayed `APPLYING`. Every node now treats its report as filed only while its own store holds it: a core reads its KV, a worker its replica (only while the replica is ready). A missing report is re-filed no sooner than 1 s after the last filing, then 2, 4, up to 60 s, and the interval resets when the report is seen; with no open change nothing is re-filed. Such re-files are counted (`DhtReplicationSettlement.refileCount`).
