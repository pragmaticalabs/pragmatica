### Fixed
- A node whose DHT replication report was dropped by the leader (it held the member `Dead`) now files it again once it is back as the same live process, while a replication change is open (#2553). Since #2545 a node dedupes against what it filed, not against the store, so a rejoined member never noticed its report was gone and the open change stayed `APPLYING`. Every node now treats its report as filed only while its own store holds it: a core reads its KV, a worker its replica (only while the replica is ready). A missing report is re-filed no sooner than 1 s after the last filing, then 2, 4, up to 60 s, and the interval resets when the report is seen present after the interval has passed (a glimpse of its own re-file before the leader drops it again does not count, nor does a worker whose replica is not ready); with no open change nothing is re-filed, and concurrent callers decide one re-file. Such re-files are counted (`DhtReplicationSettlement.refileCount`).
