### Changed (2026-11-22 — #3458 stream-mem M-2r-1: the ring's tail is one published value R, and a read below it is answered from R)
- **The ring header is owned by `RingHeader`, and the tail moves only by publishing an immutable `RemovalLedger` (R).** R carries the tail, the removal causes of the retained history (16 runs, the lowest dropped into a cause set past that) and `firstHeld`; it is published once per change through one volatile store followed by a store-store fence, so a reader's one load of R answers a read below the tail consistently. A MEMORY ring answers `CursorExpired` with the cause of the removed offset, `LIFE_LOST` for the owner and `BelowCopyTail` for any other reader below `firstHeld`; a PERSISTENT ring (every public factory) answers `CursorExpired(offset, tail, RETENTION)` byte for byte as before. `ReadAs` is the reader's role, minted in exactly two places. A refused step (a tail that does not rise, a cut outside the retained range, a role bound to another ring) is a counted `RingStateViolated`, never `BUFFER_CLOSED`. Nothing in production raises a MEMORY ring yet. [verified: aether/aether-stream/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/aether/stream/OffHeapRingBufferRemovalAnswersTest.java, RemovalLedgerTest.java, RingHeaderTripwireTest.java]
- **Pinned by census and race tests.** A header word is named by `RingHeader` alone, R is assigned in two places, and `ReadAs` is held nowhere (the census exempts `ReadAs.java` only, for its own mint signature); concurrent readers against an evicting writer only ever see an answer of an R the writer logged in their window, R is published once per change and never updated in place. [verified: RingHeaderCensusTest, ReadAsCensusTest, OffHeapRingBufferPublishedRTest] A factory refuses a capacity below 1 with a typed refusal. [verified: OffHeapRingBufferCapacityTest]
