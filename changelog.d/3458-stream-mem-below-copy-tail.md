### Added (2026-11-22 — #3458 stream-mem W-4: `BelowCopyTail`, the neutral "this copy never held it")
- **A type, a wire kind and a row; nothing raises it yet.** `StreamError.BelowCopyTail(firstHeld, tailOffset)` is the refusal of a read below the first offset a copy ever held, made by a caller that is not the owner. It names no removal cause (this copy removed nothing), and its constructor refuses a `firstHeld` above the tail. On the wire it is `ReadRefusal.Kind.BELOW_COPY_TAIL`, placed just before `UNKNOWN`; its row is `NN_RETRY` and it answers 503 (`StreamErrorStatus`), because the owner answers it. [verified: aether/aether-stream/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/aether/stream/forward/ReadRefusalTest.java, aether/node/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/aether/api/routes/StreamErrorStatusTest.java]
- **Wire generation 10 to 11:** `UNKNOWN` moves from ordinal 16 to 17, so an older node reads a newer node's `BELOW_COPY_TAIL` (16) as its own `UNKNOWN` (text) and a newer node's `UNKNOWN` (17) as out of range. Upgrade is stop-the-world (`JoinGenerationGuard.WIRE_GENERATION` bumped from 10 to 11). [verified: JoinGenerationGuardTest.thisBinarysGeneration_isElevenAfterTheBelowCopyTailKind]
