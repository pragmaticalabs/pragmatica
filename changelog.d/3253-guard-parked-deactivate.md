### Fixed (2026-11-14 — #3253: the engine-marker guard no longer lets a parked Activate erase a parked Deactivate)
- **While the engine-marker guard is closed, a cluster `Activate` parked after a `Deactivate` no longer replaces it; both run, in order, when the guard opens.** The pair re-incarnates the cluster FSM (the same rule as a state restore, B14), where the `Activate` alone is ignored by an FSM that is still Active. A later `Deactivate` still supersedes the whole parked sequence (level-triggered). New `EngineMarkerGuard.whenOpenAfterParked` carries this; `whenOpen` keeps its replace semantics. Hardening only: no reachable defect was shown, because leaving OPEN mid-life under FSM2 needs the engine marker removed or changed. `[mechanism: EngineMarkerGuardTest and Fsm2SystemTest, in-JVM; FSM2 is test-only until F6]`
