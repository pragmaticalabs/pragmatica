### Fixed (2026-11-27 — #3041: consumer-group tombstones and records of a deleted stream were never reclaimed)
- **A deleted stream's consumer-group records are reclaimed** (#3041). The leader's coordinator removes every assignment and tombstone whose stream life is not the committed one, on a committed stream-config removal and in a sweep on leader activation; the registry drops its in-memory mirror in step, and a re-join at a smaller partition count re-stamps the tombstones it no longer overwrites, so the tombstone count stays at most groups x partitions of live streams. A `Remove` of an `AssignmentGuarded` key whose witness carries another life's token is refused by the KV applier, so a reclaim that commits late deletes nothing of a newer life.
- **Wire generation 14 to 15:** `ConsumerGroupValue` and `ConsumerGroupTombstone` gain `streamIncarnation` (their SHAPE lines change), so an older node decodes the new records' extra field as a malformed value; `JoinGenerationGuard.WIRE_GENERATION` is bumped and a node at generation 14 is refused at join.
