### Added (2026-11-20 — #3303: the restore-authority cell is fenced by its key in KVStore)
- **KVStore fences the restore-authority cell by its key** (#3303, RA-1). A write to a key that implements `RestoreAuthorityKey` is admitted only when its value is a `RestoreAuthority` that admits it after the stored value. A bare Put or Remove, and an unknown or mismatched value, are refused. The decision reads only replicated state, and it is taken once, in the core's applier; replicas apply the core's judged effects unchanged. No key implements the interface yet, so behaviour is unchanged until RA-3. [verified: v3472 report, 36 of 37 mutants red]
