### Changed (2026-11-26 — #3754: operator and replacement drains report the commit's outcome)
- **`POST /api/v1/nodes/drain|shutdown` and a replacement drain answer from the outcome of the DRAIN request's commit, not from the intent.**
  The leader's DRAIN channel (`DrainPort.request`) now returns a `Promise<Unit>`; "initiated" is reported, audited and emitted only after the
  request succeeded, and a drain that was not queued is audited as `drain not queued: <cause>` and answered with an `HttpError`
  (503 leadership moved or unknown cause, 504 `WitnessExpired`/`ApplyTimeout`, 409 `AttemptCancelled`, 500 `CalledUnderOwnerMonitor`).
  A replacement drain whose target has another caller's request in flight waits for that request's outcome (`DrainPort.inFlight`) and,
  if it is refused, answers the same `HttpError` an opened replacement drain does.
  The slice floor's warnings (forced breach, admitted-after-refusal) are raised only for a drain whose request succeeded; a refused drain raises none.
  The new `FencedDrainSink` (over an unfenced port adapter in this change) is the one sink behind `DrainAssembly.drainPort`.
  The drain is still unfenced: no leadership witness is committed and the route does not wait for one until the AetherNode wiring lands; then it waits
  at most `applyTimeout` (30 s default). `[mechanism: NodeLifecycleRoutes.enqueueOperatorDrain; verified against a scripted witness port: NodeLifecycleRoutesFencedDrainTest, FencedDrainSinkTest]`
