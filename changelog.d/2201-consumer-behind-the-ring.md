### Fixed (2026-10-16 — #2201 C-16: a declarative consumer whose cursor fell behind the ring tail stalled forever, silently)
- **A consumer behind the ring now reads the sealed tier instead of retrying a `CursorExpired` about twenty times a second at DEBUG.**
  The consumer's validated read (`StreamPartitionManager.readServingTiered`, local and owner-forwarded) asks the same sealed-tier decision the pull API uses (`SealedTierFallback`): an offset still being sealed is retried, a sealed one is served bounded by the visible position and continued from the ring. Epoch admission still runs before the tier.
  `[mechanism: exercised by EmberConsumerPathTripwireTest on a 3-node Ember cluster (delivery of all 60 events from cursor 0 under a ring tail of 40); ConsumerBehindTheRingComponentTest in one JVM]`
- **A consumer whose position the partition's owner no longer holds (reclaimed by retention) now moves forward once to the earliest offset the owner holds, and the skip is raised CRITICAL.** This is the single exception to redelivery-never-skip (`865e181df5`), recorded by the owner's ruling `e3aa3b9b94`. The new `stream-consumer-cursor-expired` names the group, the partition, the skipped range and its count; a checkpoint of the new cursor is requested at once. It is closed by the new INFO `stream-consumer-cursor-resumed` (same subject) once the cursor is re-established. A per-consumer stall-instead-of-skip choice is #2209.
  `[mechanism: ConsumerBehindTheRingComponentTest pins the exact count and the checkpoint in one JVM; not a running cluster]`
- **A hole in the sealed range is waited at and raised, never skipped:** `stream-consumer-sealed-range-missing` (WARNING) names the range, and `stream-consumer-sealed-range-restored` (INFO) follows when a read from it succeeds, the cursor moves off it, or the consumer is cancelled. Retention never reclaims at or above a hole, so an unfilled hole holds the group until the range is repaired.
- **New lag signal `stream-consumer-behind-ring` (WARNING, once per episode) with its recovery `stream-consumer-back-in-ring` (INFO).** A new `earliest` group on a stream with sealed history raises one pair by design (owner ruling Q10).
