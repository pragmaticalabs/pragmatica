### Fixed (2026-11-13 — #3168: one sender's frames could arrive out of send order after a momentary unwritable stream)
- **Frames held by backpressure keep their send order** (#3168). A CONSENSUS (or live-channel DHT) frame that found its lane stream unwritable was re-sent by its own 25 ms timer while frames sent after it found the stream writable and were written at once, so they overtook it. A burst did this reliably; a single momentary `isWritable() == false` under host load did it to a paced sender (1 failure in 40 runs at host load 20-25). Each lane stream now keeps its held frames in a `LaneBacklog`: a frame is written directly only when nothing is held and the stream is writable, otherwise it joins the backlog and one drain writes it, head first. Frames refused after the retry budget are still lost after `Sent` and counted as before (`RETRY_GAVE_UP`), now per held frame. Delivery is still best-effort across a reconnect or reopened lane (a new stream has no order relation to the old one).
