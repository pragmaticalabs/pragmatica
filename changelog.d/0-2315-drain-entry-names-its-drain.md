### Fixed (2026-11-27 — #2315: a drain entry survived a restart and rejoin, and drain tracking was unbounded)
- **A drain entry is cleared when, and only when, the drain that set it is done or withdrawn** (#2315). The leader's `DrainCommandRegistry` now pins each drain to a `DrainId`, the target's boot token and its incarnation; every clear names the drain it ends and the registry refuses one that names another. A node that restarts and is MEMBER again before reaching DEAD no longer inherits the drain, a late completion of an old drain no longer clears a newer one, and `AlertManager` prunes its per-node drain tracking on the DEAD edge. An unknown boot token (`0`) is never evidence of a new process. [verified: `AetherNodeOperatorDrainClearTest`, `AetherNodeDrainSetObserverTest`, `DrainCommandRegistryIdentityTest` (a, c, d red on the old code)]
