### Added (2026-10-24 — #2410: upload ingest gate: authentication and per-route limits before the body, upload bounds, concurrency caps)
- **A request is classified on its headers, before any body byte is read, on HTTP/1.1 and HTTP/3.** The listener resolves the route, applies its security policy and its limits, and answers a request that fails any of them at once. An unauthenticated 50 MiB upload with `Expect: 100-continue` is answered `401` with no `100` and its body is never read, where before the whole body was buffered up to `max_request_size` and then rejected. A refused request closes the connection (`Connection: close`) without reading the body into memory. [verified: `integrations/net/http-server` `RequestGateWireTest` and `Http3RequestGateWireTest` (real socket, real QUIC client; the refused client cannot finish sending 50 MiB), `AppHttpServerIngestGateTest` in `aether/node` over both protocols]
- **Per-route body limits.** An inline-table route in `routes.toml` takes `max_size` (`"5GB"` or bytes) and `max_duration` (`"6h"`). A declared `Content-Length` over the limit is answered `413` on the headers; a chunked body (HTTP/3: a stream with no `content-length`) is answered `413` at the byte that crosses it, and the connection is closed within a second. A route that declares no `max_size` takes `[app-http] max_request_size`, and `[app-http.upload] max_size_ceiling` (default `5GB`) caps what a route may declare. The limits are replicated with the route entry, so an ingress that does not host the route enforces them with the strictest limits any serving node declared. [verified: `RequestGateWireTest`, `Http3RequestGateWireTest`, `AppHttpServerIngestGateTest` (undeclared route, ceiling, remote route), `HttpRouteRegistryUploadSpecTest`, `HttpRoutePublisherUploadSpecTest`, `GeneratedRouteIngestLimitsTest`]
- **Upload routes (`consumes` binary or multipart) get their own bounds and caps.** `[app-http.upload]` sets `max_concurrent` (default 64) and `max_concurrent_per_principal` (default 8), past which a request is answered `429` with `Retry-After`; `idle_timeout` (default `30s`) and `min_body_rate` (default `1KB` per second after a first 30 s) answer `408` and close the connection, as does a route's `max_duration` (default 6 hours). Over HTTP/1.1 the 408 is new: the general `min_body_rate` still closes silently. [verified: `RequestGateVirtualTimeTest` (virtual time), `RequestGateWireTest`, `Http3RequestGateWireTest`, `AppHttpServerIngestGateTest`, `UploadAdmissionTest`, `UploadConfigTest`]
- **`routes.toml` inline routes refuse unknown keys.** The loader used to ignore a key it did not know, so `max_sise = "5GB"` left the route at the default limit. An unknown key is now a compile error naming it and the valid keys. `resumable` and `unclaimed_ttl` are refused as not enforced yet. An unknown key under `[app-http.upload]` refuses the configuration. [verified: `RouteConfigLoaderIngestLimitsTest`, `UploadConfigTest`]
- **Limits of this change.** Upload routes still read the whole body into memory before the slice runs; the limits bound that memory, they do not stream it. The classification's authentication is repeated on dispatch (header-only, so repeating it is harmless).
- **Multipart part limits.** An inline route takes `max_files`, `max_file_size`, `max_fields` and `max_field_size`, checked on the decoded body (already bounded by `max_size`): a request that breaks one is answered `413` before the slice runs, on the accepting node or an ingress that does not host the route. The body is decoded once for the check and again when the slice reads it. [verified: `AppHttpServerIngestGateTest` (`maxFiles_*`, `maxFileSize_*`, `maxFields_*`, `maxFieldSize_*`, `remoteRoutesPartLimits_*`, over HTTP/1.1 and HTTP/3), `UploadSpecTest`, `RouteConfigLoaderIngestLimitsTest`, `GeneratedRouteIngestLimitsTest`, `HttpRouteRegistryUploadSpecTest`, `HttpRoutePublisherUploadSpecTest`]
- **An answer with status `413` is no longer rewritten to `500`.** The app listener's status table had no entry for `413`, so a `413` that a handler returned reached the client as `500`. [verified: `AppHttpServerIngestGateTest` part-limit tests, red with the entry removed]
- **Node upload cap saturation raises one cluster event.** When `[app-http.upload] max_concurrent` is reached, the first refused upload raises `upload-node-cap-saturated` (WARNING) and the first upload to finish after it raises `upload-node-cap-restored`; the event is raised once per episode, not once per refused request, and the cluster event aggregator also throttles it per node. A per-principal `429`, a `413` and a `408` raise no event. [verified: `AppHttpServerIngestGateTest` (`nodeCapSaturation_*`, `perPrincipalRefusal_*`, `sizeAndTimeRefusals_*`), `UploadAdmissionTest`, `OperatorWarningCodeTest`]
