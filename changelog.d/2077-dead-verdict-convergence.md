### Fixed (2026-10-09 — #2077: a silently dead replica left the survivors' streams unwritable until the leader alone declared it dead)
- **A FAULTY verdict the live-transport veto deferred was never replayed.** For a never-HEALTHY peer, `SwimProtocol` turns FAULTY into UNKNOWN while the peer's QUIC link is up ("re-checked on the next FAULTY edge"), but a member already FAULTY gets no next edge. When the link later closed, the survivor kept the peer FAULTY until the residency sweep and a gossip re-admit, so its DEAD verdict, and the ISR shrink only the leader commits, came 217 s after the blackhole against 79 s on the survivors that were not holding a deferral. The deferral is now replayed on the tick once the link is gone; a peer whose link stays up is still never departed. [verified: `SwimProtocolColdBootReplayTest.vetoedFaulty_linkDropsWhileStillFaulty_departsWithoutWaitingForSweep`, control `vetoedFaulty_linkStaysUp_isNeverDeparted`]
- **Promotion waited on a peer the node's own membership view had already dropped.** `PartitionBackfill` took its peer set from the replica registry, which keeps a committed-ISR member until the leader commits a shrink, while the owner-activation gate already stopped waiting for a locally DEAD member. Both the promotion contest and the owner catch-up now probe only registry replicas that are live placement members; an empty member view filters nothing, and an unreachable peer that is still a live member still blocks promotion. [verified: `PartitionBackfillTest.backfill_unreachablePeerNotInLiveMembers_promotes_whileUnreachableLivePeerStillBlocks`, `backfill_freshOwnerEmptyRing_blindSurvivorUnreachableAndNotLive_promotesWithoutWaitingForTheBound`]
- **Cluster events no longer expire while the stream is merely not yet promoted.** `ClusterEventRedelivery` restarts an event's 5-minute horizon only on a promotion refusal, by exact type: `StreamError.OwnerNotActivated`, `StreamError.StreamConfigNotYetVisible`, and either of them as a forwarder sees it (`StreamForwardError.RemotePublishRetryable` carrying that refusal's own text). Every other failure ages as before, other `Cause.Transient` timeouts and `PublishOutcomeUnknown` included. An event held past the horizon keeps the once-per-node ERROR (it is kept, not dropped), and a buffer-overflow drop is logged when it happens. The 1024 bound is unchanged. [verified: `ClusterEventRedeliveryTest.redeliver_streamRefusesPromotionPastTheHorizon_eventStillLandsWhenOwnerActivates`, `redeliver_transientNonPromotionCause_expiresAtTheHorizon`, `redeliver_promotionRefusalHeldPastTheHorizon_logsTheErrorOnce_andKeepsTheEvent`]
- Promotion safety rules, the owner-activation gate and the ISR rules are unchanged. A wedged JVM that keeps handshaking while answering nothing is still an outage until the member is removed (#1563, #1579).
- Acceptance: `EmberBlackholedReplicaRecoveryTest` (every survivor reads events and every stream accepts a publish within 150 s of a replica follower's blackhole); red on rc4 in 2 of 2 bigboy runs.
