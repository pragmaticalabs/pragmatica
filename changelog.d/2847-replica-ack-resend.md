### Fixed (2026-11-08 — #2847: a refused replica ack turned an acknowledged publish into 503 "outcome unknown")
- **A replica re-sends an ack its transport refused to write** (#2847). The owner's await of a `confirmation_factor` 2 publish needs an ack from every in-sync replica and waits up to 5 s for it, while the entry node's management forward to the STREAMING governor gives up at 2.5 s. A replica sent each ack once, so an ack refused by a backpressured FORWARD lane held the publish past the hop and the client got 503 "outcome unknown" for a publish every replica held (`EmberFirstPublishRefusedCatchupTest`, about 1 run in 60). An ack the transport proves it never wrote is now sent again, the same message, after 20 ms doubling to 500 ms, until it is written, a newer ack of the partition supersedes it, or 5 s from its first send have passed, as #2820 re-sends a refused replicate chunk. An ack that was written and then lost is still the owner's to time out. [verified: `EmberRefusedReplicaAckTest` (red on the old send-once path), `ReplicationAckResendNotSentTest`]
