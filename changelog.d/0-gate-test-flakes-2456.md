### Fixed (2026-10-29 — #2456: leader-reconnect, drain-eviction and HTTP/3 gate test flakes on CI runners)
- **Two tests could fail on a starved runner for reasons that are not in the product, and one could not say why.** `EmberDrainEvictionWiringTest` sent its DRAINING pong as soon as the cluster deployment manager logged the first line of its activation, which could meet a manager still entering and be ignored; activation now logs its last line and the test waits for it. `Http3RequestGateWireTest` used a 5 s client idle bound, which a stalled first exchange on a loaded runner reached (status `closed`); the bound is 20 s, above the 10 s answer wait. `LeaderReconnectTransportTest` timed out at 15 s with a fixed sentence; the message now carries the follower's FSM state, its topology and both ends of the link, so the next failure names its cause. `EmberReadCommittedFailoverTest` re-published a record whose replication wait had timed out, but the publish appends before it waits, so the record was appended twice and the exact-list control saw `acked-1` twice; it now appends once and retries only the wait. `DynamicConfigurationProviderAtomicallyTest` closed its write window from a callback that returned at once, leaving the close to the writer thread against the reader's 5 ms wait bound; the callback now waits until the writer has left the window. Tests, plus one INFO line at the end of the manager's activation.
