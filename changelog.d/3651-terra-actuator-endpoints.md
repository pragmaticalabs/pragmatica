### Added
- Terra: Actuator-compatible management endpoints, part 1 (#3651). New `terra-management` module serves `/actuator` discovery, `health` (liveness/readiness groups, livenessState/readinessState/ping/diskSpace components, Spring status-to-HTTP mapping), `info`, `metrics` and `prometheus` with Spring Boot's `management.*` keys and defaults (default exposure: health only), v3 media type, authentication for all but health and info, and a separate management port. `terra-http` gains host mounts (`TerraHttpMount`). Aether modules are banned from depending on Terra artifacts (enforcer rule in `aether/pom.xml`) and from naming `/actuator` in main sources (tripwire test).
