### Fixed (2026-11-26 — #3754: scheduling bound to its owner's stop, part 2: SWIM)
- **`SwimProtocol` schedules every task through one `OwnerLifetime` that `stop()` ends**: the probe tick, every probe
  timeout (and its PingReq re-arm), the seed ANNOUNCE and the join-announce loop. Before this, `stop()` cancelled only
  the tick and the announce loop, so a probe timeout scheduled before the stop still ran after it: it sent a PingReq,
  re-armed itself and raised `onMemberSuspect` on a stopped protocol. A tick or probe timeout already dispatched onto the
  state executor re-reads the lifetime before its effects, so it does nothing after the stop. The lifetime is created
  fresh per `start()`, and every body receives the lifetime it was scheduled on, so a re-arm cannot reach the next
  activation. The `announceStopped` latch is replaced by the ended lifetime (#501 behaviour kept).
  [mechanism: `OwnerLifetime` publish-then-read pair plus a second read on the state executor before each effect]
  [verified: `integrations/swim/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/swim/SwimStopLifetimeTest.java`, fake-seam tests through the real `stop()`]
- **A restart begins with no pending probe of the previous lifetime**: a probe in flight at `stop()` kept its pending
  entry, because its timeout was cancelled with the lifetime, and the next activation read it as a live probe of that
  peer, which suppressed the transport-hint probe of a floored suspect. Each pending probe now names the lifetime that
  sent it, and `start()` sweeps the entries of an ended lifetime.
  [mechanism: lifetime tag on the pending probe plus a sweep queued on the state executor by `start()`]
  [verified: `SwimStopLifetimeTest#restart_probeInFlightAtTheStop_doesNotGateTheHintFlooredProbe`]
- **Not claimed**: a body that read the lifetime as live before `end()` and is already sending may complete that one
  send. Every effect of this component is a wire message or a local listener event from the sole writer of this node's
  state, harmless after stop, so no receiver fence is needed. Operator recovery action: none.
