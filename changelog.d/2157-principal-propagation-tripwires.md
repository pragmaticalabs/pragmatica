### Documented (2026-11-08 — #2157 part a: the caller's principal does not cross an inter-slice invoke)
- **The gap is now documented and pinned.** `SecurityContext` is not carried on `InvokeRequest`: a downstream slice on another node sees an empty `SecurityContextHolder` and an empty `InvocationContext.currentPrincipal()`; a local call keeps the binding only because its chain is synchronous; a continuation resolved after the Terra/edge binding scope ended, or on another thread, reads an empty holder. Enabled tripwires (`PrincipalPropagationTripwireTest`, `TerraHttpServerTest#holder_*`) assert today's behaviour and fail with "#2157: propagation landed; flip this tripwire to the real assertion"; the real assertions are `@Disabled` beside them. No production change. Propagation itself is #2157 part b.
