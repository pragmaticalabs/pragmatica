### Added (2026-11-08 — #2157 part a: the caller's principal does not cross an inter-slice invoke)
- **The gap is now documented and pinned.** `SecurityContext` is not carried on `InvokeRequest`: a downstream slice on another node sees an empty `SecurityContextHolder` and an empty `InvocationContext.currentPrincipal()`; a local call keeps the binding only because its chain is synchronous; a plain `map` continuation resolved after the Terra/edge binding scope ended, or on another thread, reads an empty holder, and an executor hop does not carry the `SecurityContext`. Enabled tripwires for the `InvokeRequest` shape and the caller context on the wire (`PrincipalPropagationTripwireTest`) assert today's behaviour and fail with "#2157: propagation landed; flip this tripwire to the real assertion", with the real assertions `@Disabled` beside them; the executor-hop tests (`PrincipalPropagationTripwireTest`, `TerraHttpServerTest#holder_*`) are twin-less hazard tripwires, and the receiver-ambient, local-hop, body-route and plain-continuation tests are permanent pins with their own messages. No production change. Propagation itself is #2157 part b.
