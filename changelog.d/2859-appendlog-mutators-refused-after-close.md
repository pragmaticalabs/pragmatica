### Fixed (2026-11-08 — #2859: AppendLog truncate/truncateSuffix/clampAckedMarker/compact and recordAckedMarker could mutate files after close())
- **A mutator that passed its `closed` check and then waited on a lock while `close()` completed ran against the closed log**: `truncateSuffix`/compaction fail-stopped it and wrote `<log>.failstopped` (the #2855 false fail-stop through another entry), and `recordAckedMarker` rewrote `<log>.acked` (it took only `markerLock`, which `close()` did not).
- `cutSuffix`, `compact`, `clampAckedMarker` and `raiseAckedMarker` re-check `closed` under their locks and refuse with `WAL_CLOSED`; `close()` now also takes `markerLock` (order `writeLock`, `syncLock`, `markerLock`, as a cut). [verified: `integrations/storage/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/storage/AppendLogCloseRaceTest.java`, one test per path, each red with its re-check removed]
