### Fixed (2026-11-08 — #2859: AppendLog write, write-attributed, truncateSuffix, truncate, clampAckedMarker, recordAckedMarker and the four epoch-history mutators could mutate files after close())
- **A mutator that passed its `closed` check and then waited on a lock while `close()` completed ran against the closed log**: `write`/`truncateSuffix`/compaction fail-stopped it and wrote `<log>.failstopped` (the #2855 false fail-stop through other entries), `write` with an epoch key and `recordEpochStart`/`truncateEpochsAbove`/`truncateEpochsAboveHead`/`dropEpochStartsAboveHead` rewrote `<log>.epochs`, and `recordAckedMarker` rewrote `<log>.acked` (it took only `markerLock`, which `close()` did not).
- `write` (both forms), `cutSuffix`, `compact`, `clampAckedMarker`, `raiseAckedMarker` and the four epoch-history methods re-check `closed` under their locks and refuse with `WAL_CLOSED`; `close()` now also takes `markerLock` (order `writeLock`, `syncLock`, `markerLock`, as a cut). The epoch-history methods take `writeLock` first; the history's own lock stays innermost. Not covered: `deleteFiles` (runs after close by contract) and read-only methods. [verified: `integrations/storage/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/storage/AppendLogCloseRaceTest.java`, one test per path, each red with its re-check removed]
