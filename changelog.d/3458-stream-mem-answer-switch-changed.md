### Changed (2026-11-20 — #3458: a forwarded cause with no kind answers 503)
- **Causes whose read status changed (500 to 503):** on the stream read routes, a failed read whose cause is neither an `HttpStatusAware` cause nor a `StreamError` now takes the row of `ReadRefusal.answerOf` instead of the funnel's unclassified 500: `StorageError`, a `SegmentError` (the two a read carries answer `NN_SURFACE`, the rest `UNKNOWN`'s row, both 503), a composite of such causes, and a forwarded `StreamForwardError` (including the `ReadForwardFailed` a kindless cause becomes on the asking node) other than `ForwardNotEncoded`, `PublishNotEncoded` and `READ_RESPONSE_OVERSIZED`, which stay 500. At the base, `ManagementStatus` lists none of these types, so `ManagementStatus.unclassified` answered 500 (base `ManagementStatus.java:253`, `return HttpStatus.INTERNAL_SERVER_ERROR;`; the file is identical at this head). **Unchanged:** every `StreamError` keeps its pinned status (a kindless one pinned at 500, such as `WalReplayMismatch`, `WalHeadLost`, `SeedRejected`, `ProvenanceMismatch`, still answers 500), and a cause that already carries a status (`StreamRefused`, for example `NOT_FOUND`) is returned as it is. [verified: StreamErrorStatusTest.AnswerStatus.aStreamErrorPinnedAt500_keeps500_onTheHelperAndOnBothReadRoutes, aStorageError_takesTheUnknownRow_503, anAlreadyTypedCause_keepsItsStatus, aKindlessCause_answersTheSameStatusLocallyAndOnceForwarded]
- **Wire generation 5 to 6:** `ReadRefusal.Kind` gained `STREAM_NOT_MATERIALIZED` (ordinal 15), and the codec forces `UNKNOWN` last, so `UNKNOWN` moves from ordinal 15 to 16. A mixed pair misreads a refusal: a newer node's `UNKNOWN` (16) and `STREAM_NOT_MATERIALIZED` (15) reach an older node, which reads 15 as its own `UNKNOWN` (text) and 16 as out of range. Upgrade is stop-the-world (`JoinGenerationGuard.WIRE_GENERATION` bumped from 5 to 6). [verified: JoinGenerationGuardTest.thisBinarysGeneration_isSixAfterTheReadRefusalKindRenumber]
