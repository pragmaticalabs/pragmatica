### Changed (2026-11-20 — #3458: a forwarded cause with no kind answers 503)
- A forwarded read cause that carries no kind used to answer 500 on the asking node; it now takes the row of `UNKNOWN` (503), the same as the node that raised it. [verified: StreamErrorStatusTest.AnswerStatus.aKindlessCause_answersTheSameStatusLocallyAndOnceForwarded]
- **Wire generation 2 to 3:** `ReadRefusal.Kind` gained `STREAM_NOT_MATERIALIZED` (ordinal 15), and the codec forces `UNKNOWN` last, so `UNKNOWN` moves from ordinal 15 to 16. A mixed pair misreads a refusal: an older node decodes the new 15 and 16 as its own `UNKNOWN` or out of range, and a newer node decodes the older `UNKNOWN` (15) as `STREAM_NOT_MATERIALIZED`. Upgrade is stop-the-world (`JoinGenerationGuard.WIRE_GENERATION` bumped to 3). [verified: JoinGenerationGuardTest.thisBinarysGeneration_isThreeAfterTheReadRefusalKindRenumber]
