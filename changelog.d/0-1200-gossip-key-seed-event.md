### Fixed
- The seed nodes now report gossip they cannot decrypt for want of the key (#1200): the operator event `gossip-key-unknown-from-peer` (subject: the sender's address, without its port) when a peer's datagram arrives under a gossip key id they do not hold, and `gossip-key-peer-decrypted` when that sender's gossip next decrypts. Bounded against spoofed UDP sources: at most 16 new senders per minute raise an event, and one `gossip-key-unknown-senders-suppressed` event states how many more were not reported. The per-datagram log WARN stays.
- Corrected the claim that a node booting after a gossip-key rotation is stranded in a deadlock (SECURITY.md, the failure almanac, and a comment in `AetherNode`). It dials the cores it was configured with itself after a 2 s isolation grace, so it forms quorum and replays the rotation record without any gossip decrypting. The limit that matters is stated instead: rotation protects only against a leaked gossip key, not a leaked `cluster_secret` (#2474).
- The rc4 release notes for #683 round 2 ("A rotated cluster cannot GROW until an operator acts ... unable to self-heal") are superseded by the above: that claim is withdrawn.
