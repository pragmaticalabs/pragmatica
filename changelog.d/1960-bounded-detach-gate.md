### Fixed (2026-11-08 — #1960: a successor's cursor fetch waited unboundedly on a wedged detach-flush commit)
- **A consumer re-subscribed on the same node no longer waits forever for the previous consumer's detach flush.** The gate (#1403) now waits at most 30 s (`DETACH_WAIT_BOUND`, a guess, not measured), then the successor fetches its cursor and starts; the condition is raised as the new WARNING `stream-consumer-detach-wait-exceeded`, closed by the INFO `stream-consumer-detach-wait-settled` (same subject) when the held flush settles. A flush commit that never settles is never closed; `close` still reports it unsettled at its shutdown bound. `[verified: aether/aether-stream/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/aether/stream/DetachAwaitsInFlightAdvanceTest.java#wedgedFlushCommit_successorStartsAfterBound_andIsNotReaped]` (component level, one node, a stub store).
- **A successor waiting on that gate is marked awaiting-cursor-fetch,** so the 60 s idle reaper no longer removes it before it starts. `[verified: same test]`
- **A late old flush commit landing after the successor's is refused at consensus** by #1961's scoped advance-only arm, and the resume keeps the successor's cursor although the node-local cursor ref is lowered by the late write. `[verified: aether/node/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/aether/node/stream/ClusterCursorStoreTest.java#lateOldFlushCommit_afterTheSuccessorsCommit_isRefused_andTheResumeKeepsTheSuccessorsCursor]` (the real applier, one store; no multi-node test).

- **Delivery past the bound is at-least-once, never a skip.** When the 30 s wait expires before the previous consumer's detach flush commits, the successor resumes from the last checkpoint that actually committed, so it can re-deliver events the previous consumer already delivered after that checkpoint; it never skips an event. `[verified: v2899 probe P1, oss rc5-planning/v-2899-report.md]`
