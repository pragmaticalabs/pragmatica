### Fixed (2026-11-18 — #3367: PgSchedulerTestBase teardown iterated a gate list its handler threads append to)
- **`PgSchedulerTestBase.stopSchedulers` no longer throws `ConcurrentModificationException`** (#3367, seen as `PgSchedulerLivenessTest.firingsInFlight_neverExceedBatchSize` in a merge-queue run). `hang()` runs on scheduler threads while firings are in flight and appended to a plain `ArrayList` the test thread's teardown was iterating. The gates are now guarded by one lock, the teardown opens a snapshot taken under it and sets a `stopping` flag in the same step, and a gate created after that point is created already open, so a late handler never waits on a gate nobody opens. `PgScheduler.close()` was not changed: it waits at most `closeGrace` for in-flight firings and then releases their claims (`PgScheduler.java` `drain`), so a handler may outlive `close()` by design (documented at-least-once). Test-only.
