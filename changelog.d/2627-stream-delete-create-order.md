### Fixed (2026-11-03 — #2627: a stream delete then create of one name could delete a stream reported as created)
- **Every `StreamConfigKey` submission for one stream name is ordered through a per-name commit lane.** The create `Put`, the publish-path auto-create `Put` and the destroy `Remove` used to be submitted without waiting for one another, and Rabia commits pending batches in content-hash order, so a `Remove` could commit ahead of the `Put` of a later life (refused by the life fence, then the `Remove` deleted the new stream) or behind an auto-create `Put` that had outlived its wait (a ghost stream re-hydrated). Each submission now starts after the previous one for the name has settled [verified: `StreamDeleteCreateOrderTest`, commit order forced reversed against a real `KVStore`]. A submission the engine still holds after its apply timeout is not covered; that is the engine-side withdrawal (#2629).
- **`createStream` reports a Put the life fence refused as `STREAM_ALREADY_EXISTS`, never as success.** It reads the applier's answer to its `Put` (the replaced committed value) instead of inferring success from the absence of an applied config on this node.
