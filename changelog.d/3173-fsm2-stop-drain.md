### Fixed (2026-11-14 — #3173, #3174 part A: an FSM2 node stop no longer waits out the 10 s drain bound)
- **`FsmRuntime.drain()` waited for a consumer that was never started**, so a node refused before `start()`, such as a retired NodeId, held its stop for the whole 10 s drain bound and outlived Ember's node stop bound. It now resolves at once when no consumer exists. `[verified: CloseAndOverrunProbeTest.drain_ofANeverStartedRuntime_isResolvedAtOnce_withoutWaitingForWhatMustHoldAfterTheMarker]`
- **The `SUSPEND` local op awaited the route unpublish**, a consensus write that cannot commit without quorum, so every FSM2 stop waited out the drain bound with the QUIC port still bound. `SUSPEND` now fires the unpublish and forgets it, as legacy does. The local route table and bridge are still removed at once. `UNPUBLISH`, which runs under quorum, still waits. `[verified: SliceRuntimeAdapterTest.suspend_isDoneAtOnce_withoutWaitingForAnUnpublishThatCannotSettle, SliceRuntimeAdapterTest.unpublish_stillWaitsForThePublisher]`
- **Ordering kept (design rule H3b)**: the adapter holds each `SUSPEND`'s unsettled route removal per artifact, and the next `PUBLISH_ROUTES` of that artifact awaits it (bounded at 100 s, above the publisher's 3 x 30 s retry chain; on expiry it logs and proceeds), so a reactivation's `Put` cannot be overtaken by the old incarnation's late `Remove`. The stop drain still does not wait on the write. A timed-out consensus attempt is not cancelled in Rabia (pre-existing). `[verified: SliceRuntimeAdapterTest.publishRoutes_waitsForTheSuspendsUnsettledUnpublish]`
- **Operator:** a `PUBLISH_ROUTES` that proceeds after that 100 s bound raises the WARNING `route-unpublish-await-expired` (subject: node and artifact) once per unsettled removal, and the INFO `route-unpublish-await-settled` when that removal settles.
