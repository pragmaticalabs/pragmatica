### Changed (2026-11-20 — #3303: the wire-generation check accepts a declared semantic wire break)
- **A change in how replicas judge an existing wire value can now bump `WIRE_GENERATION` through the baseline.** `scripts/wire-generation-check.sh` refused every bump when no TAG, SHAPE or ENUM line had changed. The baseline now takes a `SEMANTIC <value> <one-line old->new judgement>` line; a `SEMANTIC` line added at HEAD (absent at the merge base) requires `GENERATION` = base + 1, exactly as a changed TAG, SHAPE or ENUM line does. `SEMANTIC` lines already at the merge base never re-trigger, removing or editing one is refused, a malformed one is refused, and an add-only change still must not bump. `WireAssignmentTripwireTest` skips `SEMANTIC` lines when comparing the assignment and pins their form; `WireAssignmentBaselineWriter` keeps them across a re-record. [verified: `scripts/wire-generation-check-selftest.sh` (cases C8a-C8k), `WireAssignmentTripwireTest`]
