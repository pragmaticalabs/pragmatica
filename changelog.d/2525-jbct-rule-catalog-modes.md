### Added (2026-11-17 — #2525: JBCT rule catalog with modes, and the linter mode filter)
- **Every lint rule now declares the modes it runs in (`LintRule.modes()`), and `CstLinter` computes its rule list from `LintContext.mode()`.** `LintRule` is a sealed supertype over `CstLintRule` (and the empty `RunRule`); `modes()` defaults to `{business, infrastructure}`. A rule list under `infrastructure` omits the business-only rules, and under `format-only` and `disabled` it is empty (both modes are still refused by the `jbct.toml` parser; the filter answers for them regardless). [verified: `jbct/jbct-lint/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/jbct/lint/cst/CstLinterModeFilterTest.java`]
- **ARCH-01, ARCH-02, ARCH-03, ARCH-04 and MIX-01 are business-only.** A project with `mode = "infrastructure"` (the repo root's `jbct.toml`) no longer gets those rules; in this repository the only finding that disappears is ARCH-02 (5 findings in `aether/aether-config` and `aether/aether-deployment`), every other rule count over the `jbct:check` reactor is unchanged. `RuleRegistryInvariantTest` pins that every registered rule's modes are a subset of `{business, infrastructure}` containing `business`. [verified: `jbct/jbct-lint/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/jbct/lint/cst/rules/RuleRegistryInvariantTest.java`]
