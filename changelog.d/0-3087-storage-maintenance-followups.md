### Fixed (2026-11-13 — #3087: storage maintenance follow-ups to #800)
- **A throwing retry of a pending activation no longer escapes the maintenance tick, and the not-started warning closes on leader loss** (#3087). `StorageMaintenanceDriver.tick()` now runs the retry as its own pass (`retry-pending`): a throw is the tick's failed pass, raised as `STORAGE_MAINTENANCE_PASS_FAILED` for that part, counted in the progress record with its real cause and closed by the next good pass, instead of surfacing three intervals later as "overdue". `DelegatedStorageAdapter.deactivate()` closes an open `STORAGE_MAINTENANCE_NOT_STARTED` warning, since a non-leader is no longer expected to activate. [verified: `StorageMaintenanceDriverProgressTest`, `DelegatedStorageAdapterTest` (new cases red before the fix)]
