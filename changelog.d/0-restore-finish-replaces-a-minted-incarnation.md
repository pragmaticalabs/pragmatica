### Fixed
- **A backup restore resumed after another lineage's genesis now finishes.** While a restore is in progress, a leader without `[backup]` has no restore gate and can commit an incarnation of its own lineage. The restore's finish then had to replace it, and could not: a transaction cannot remove and re-put one key, so the remove and the put were folded into one mutation, which the version fence on the incarnation refused on every retry. The restore never completed and the restore gate stayed closed. The finish now replaces the incarnation the way `aether backup declare-genesis` already did, with one shared write: two leader transactions in one batch, the first removing the committed incarnation under the in-progress marker, the second writing the restored one where the first left the key empty. One batch is one consensus decision, and the second transaction's preconditions are all established by the first, so the pair lands together or not at all.
