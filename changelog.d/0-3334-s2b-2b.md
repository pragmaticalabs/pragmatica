### Changed (2026-11-18 — #3334: entity records above 1 MiB are refused at the write)
- **The entity `max_event_size` is 1 MiB, down from 4 MiB** (#3334). An entity record larger than 1 048 576 bytes is refused at the append with `EventTooLarge(size, 1048576)` before anything is stored; a record of exactly 1 MiB is acked. The entity ring window (10 000 entries, 64 MiB, finite max age) now lives in `aether-stream` as `EntityRingWindow`.
