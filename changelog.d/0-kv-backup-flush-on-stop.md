### Fixed
- A graceful node stop now flushes the KV backup before it completes (know 3b01e624f1): the leader commits and pushes the changes still inside the debounce window, bounded by 10 s. A flush that fails or overruns raises the operator warning `backup-stop-flush-failed` and the stop proceeds. A crash gets no such flush; the backup then trails by up to about 5.5 s (documented beside `[backup]`, #1569 for the crash case).
- A stale KV restore no longer loses the old life of a stream (know 6d0c58fd67): adopting a stream's committed life used to delete the sealed-segment refs of every other life of the name, which after a restore from a backup older than the stream's config is the life holding acknowledged data. Adoption now deletes nothing. Each other life (WAL directory and/or sealed segments) is kept and announced once per process by the operator warning `storage-stream-wal-orphaned`, naming the stream, both incarnations and the sealed-segment count. An explicit stream destroy still reclaims the life it served.
