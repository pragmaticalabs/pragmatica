### Fixed (2026-11-24 — #3627: two TLA+ rows timed out in the small profile on every rc6 push)
- **`RabiaSlot/ctl_logprefix` reaches its `LogPrefix` violation in about a minute**: the known-bad row searched 21.1M distinct states (977 s on a build host, 1096 s on a fast CI runner, exit 124 at the 1200 s budget on slower ones) to reach its depth-30 counterexample. No model change caused this: the module and config were byte-identical before and after #3601, so the row was marginal from the start. The new constraint `NoBatches` (no node holds a client batch; it restricts `Init` only, and `LogPrefix` reads no value) finds the same depth-30 violation in 1,054,662 states (56 s). The flip with every fix on under the same constraint finds no violation through depth 34.
- **`DHTDepartureDrain/live` is a `heavy` row**: at its small overrides (N=3, RF=2) it is an exhaustive pass of 4,729,016 distinct states that takes 1256 s on a build host, so it cannot fit the 1200 s budget. The small profile now records it as SKIPPED-HEAVY by name instead of as an error. Its liveness property `DrainCompletes` stays in the push profile through two reduced rows: `livesmall` (one drainer, one stale entry; 89,590 states, 13 s) and `livecodrain` (two co-drainers, no stale entry; 129,902 states, 32 s). Not in the push profile: two co-drainers together with a stale entry, which is the heavy `live` row.
