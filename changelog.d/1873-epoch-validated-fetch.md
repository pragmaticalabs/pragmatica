### Fixed (2026-10-04 — #1873 / #1730 phase 2: a consumer no longer skips records re-assigned by a restarted or failed-over owner)
- **Epoch-validated fetch (KIP-320).** A stream partition whose owner restarted without a WAL, failed over to a replica that held less, or was re-created assigned offsets the consumers had already read to NEW records, and a consumer whose cursor sat past the new head silently skipped them. Every consumer read, and every resume from a checkpoint, now carries the owner epoch it was read under; the serving node checks it against the committed epoch starts and answers a typed `EpochDiverged(resumeAt)`, and the consumer re-reads from where the new epoch began.
  - An owner commits where its epoch begins before it serves, and a re-activation of a rebuilt ring bumps the epoch (new ownership-record fields; the start is a guarded write of the exact committed record).
  - The cursor checkpoint (and the node-local cursor block) records the owner epoch; the subscribe-time clamp to the partition's head, which could fail forever against an owner that was down, is gone.
  - A consumer of an earlier life of a re-created stream is refused from the new life's first offset.
  - An owner is activated for the RING it committed its epoch for: a stream destroyed and re-created in one process leaves the committed record untouched, so the gate re-runs on the new ring and bumps the epoch; an owner with no ring commits no start (it refuses with `OwnerNotActivated` until a ring exists, and a write that reaches an owner before its ring is built builds it first).
  - The record keeps the newest 16 epoch starts and folds the dropped ones into the oldest it keeps, so a consumer older than the history is admitted only at or below that offset and otherwise resumes at it (it may re-read, never skips). That rewind is logged at INFO; `stream-consumer-rewound` (WARNING) is raised only for an exact boundary.
  - Operator surface: the cluster event `STREAM_LINEAGE_RESTARTED` (INFO) when an owner begins a new epoch under an unchanged owner, and the per-node warning `stream-consumer-rewound` when a consumer re-seeks.
- Pre-GA, so a checkpoint written before this change (no owner epoch) has no compatibility path: it resumes unchecked, as before, and every checkpoint written from now on carries the epoch.
- **Wire (pre-GA, no mixed-version support):** `ReadForward` gains the consumer epoch, `ReadForwardResponse` the owner epoch and the divergence offset, the cursor checkpoint the owner epoch, the ownership record its epoch starts; new tags 1748 (`EpochStart`) and 1749 (`ClusterEvent.StreamLineageRestarted`).
- **Cost of the no-WAL seal snapshot (#1862), measured on bigboy (16 cores, load 1.1-1.8 with only this bench running, 3 interleaved rounds):** each no-WAL seal takes one metadata snapshot under a single lock, so a node seals at most about 100-120 segments per second with up to 2,000 refs and about 60 per second with 20,000, flat in the number of concurrent sealers (more partitions add latency, not throughput; p50 per seal 9-16 ms, p99 up to several seconds at 32 concurrent sealers). Not coalesced: a no-WAL stream is the explicit non-durable mode, and one seal covers a segment's worth of events, not one event.
