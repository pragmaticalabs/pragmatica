### Fixed (2026-11-11 — #2635: the last consumer group member leaving left its partitions assigned to it)
- **An empty consumer group now clears its committed assignments** (#2635). The coordinator skipped the rebalance when the member list was empty, so the committed `ConsumerGroupKey` entries kept naming the departed consumer: status and metrics showed a ghost owner and a later joiner saw partitions owned by a member that no longer existed. The last leave now mints the next epoch (same refusals as a rebalance) and writes an epoch-bearing `ConsumerGroupTombstone` (wire tag 2900) at that epoch for EVERY partition, not only the committed ones. The applier's `Put` fence then refuses any older assignment that commits later, whatever order Rabia commits batches in (a join still in flight at the leave, or rebalance batches derived before it); the registry reads a tombstone as "no owner". The next rebalance mints higher and overwrites the tombstones; a group that never rejoins keeps one tombstone entry per partition.
