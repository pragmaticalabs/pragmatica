### Fixed (2026-11-11 — #2635: the last consumer group member leaving left its partitions assigned to it)
- **An empty consumer group now clears its committed assignments** (#2635). The coordinator skipped the rebalance when the member list was empty, so the committed `ConsumerGroupKey` entries kept naming the departed consumer: status and metrics showed a ghost owner and a later joiner saw partitions owned by a member that no longer existed. The last leave now mints the next epoch (same refusals as a rebalance) and removes each committed assignment with a witness stamped with that epoch, so a removal can never delete a newer rebalance's assignments.
