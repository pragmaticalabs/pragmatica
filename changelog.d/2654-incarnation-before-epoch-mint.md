### Fixed
- No ownership or lineage epoch is minted before the life's cluster incarnation commits (#2654). After a whole-cluster restore, the leader could commit a stream ownership record at incarnation 0 (`ClusterIncarnation.NONE`) before the restore committed the new incarnation, so the new life's epoch ranked below the restored life's (the #1529 hazard); a fresh cluster's first records could be minted at 0 the same way. The stream partition ownership writers, the consumer assignment writer, the DHT core-partition bootstrap, the consumer group coordinator and the projection rewind mint now refuse a NONE base with the typed cause `INCARNATION_NOT_COMMITTED` and write nothing. Re-drive per site: the stream ownership driver re-arms its retry, the bootstrap module is re-driven by the `ClusterIncarnationKey` commit, the consumer assignment writer by its periodic reconcile; a consumer group join and a projection rebuild return the refusal to their caller, which retries after the commit. A refused projection rebuild is refused before it clears the read model, which keeps serving its last state. [verified: `EmberOwnerRestartAckedTailTripwireTest#ownerAfterColdRestart_lifeTwoOwnershipIncarnation_isAboveLifeOnes`] for stream ownership after a restore; [mechanism: every other site is pinned by unit tests at the mint seam] for the rest. Operator action: none; the refused writes land once the incarnation commits. Not covered: the governor community epoch never carries the cluster incarnation in any life, a separate defect that needs its own fence design (see #2659).
