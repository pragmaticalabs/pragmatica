### Fixed (2026-11-02 — #2580: the QUIC backpressure WARN fires on every backpressured write)
- **The cluster transport logged `Backpressure on peer … lane …` at WARN on every write that found a stream unwritable**, though its comment promised one line per backpressure entry. One forge run logged it 785,923 times in 4.5 minutes, 758k of them for one peer's CONSENSUS lane: log volume landing exactly when the cluster is overloaded.
- **Now one WARN marks a (peer, lane) entering backpressure, and one INFO marks it clearing**, at the first write that finds the lane writable again. The INFO reports how many writes found it unwritable and over how many milliseconds. Per-write detail is at DEBUG. [verified: `integrations/consensus` `QuicConsensusBackpressureTest$BackpressureLogEpisodes`]
- An episode also closes, with its summary, when the peer's bound channel closes or the peer departs, so entries for gone peers do not accumulate: worker node ids are new per incarnation. [verified: `QuicConsensusBackpressureTest$BackpressureLogEpisodes` (departure and channel close)] The per-message `refusing message (backpressure)` WARN of the fast-fail lanes is unchanged: it reports a dropped message, not the entry.
