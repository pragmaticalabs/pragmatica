### Fixed (2026-11-02 — #1881: ForgeProxyApiKeyForgeTest control arm is GC-timing flaky)
- **`ForgeProxyApiKeyForgeTest.forgeEventPoll_reusesOneClient_acrossPolls` failed in isolation (2 of 12 runs) for two reasons, both in the test.** First, its control counted the selector threads of per-fetch HTTP clients that nothing referenced. A JDK client's selector thread ends once the client is collected, so a GC between the fetches and the count could drop the control below its bound. Second, its first keyed poll ran 0.2 s after leader election without awaiting a successful poll; the management server was already bound, but `/api/v1/events` reads the `system:cluster-events` stream, whose partition was still promoting, so the first read could fail transiently. Test-only change. The control pollers are now held in a list, the poller under test is held by a reachability fence until it is counted, and the poller is awaited until it can poll, as the sibling test does. [verified: `aether/forge/forge-tests` `ForgeProxyApiKeyForgeTest`]
