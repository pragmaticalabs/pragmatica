### Fixed (2026-11-02 — #1881: ForgeProxyApiKeyForgeTest control arm is GC-timing flaky)
- **`ForgeProxyApiKeyForgeTest.forgeEventPoll_reusesOneClient_acrossPolls` failed in isolation (2 of 12 runs) for two reasons, both in the test.** First, its control counted the selector threads of per-fetch HTTP clients that nothing referenced. A JDK client's selector thread ends once the client is collected, so a GC between the fetches and the count could drop the control below its bound. Second, its first keyed poll ran without waiting for the management API to serve, so it could fail on a cluster still starting. Test-only change. The control pollers are now held in a list, the poller under test is held by a reachability fence until it is counted, and the poller is awaited until it can poll, as the sibling test does. [verified: `aether/forge/forge-tests` `ForgeProxyApiKeyForgeTest`]
