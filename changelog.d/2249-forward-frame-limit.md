### Fixed (2026-10-18 — #2249: a forward over the cluster frame limit closed the peer's FORWARD lane)
- **A request whose encoded forward exceeded the 32 MiB cluster frame was sent anyway.** The receiving node's frame decoder rejected it and closed the whole FORWARD lane to that peer, losing every other forward in flight on it, and the request was retried on further nodes. Such a request is now answered 413 ("too large to forward") and is never sent. The 413 detail reports the size of the refused frame, the quantity the limit bounds, not the size of the request payload inside it. A forward frame that cannot be encoded at all is answered 503 "nothing was applied; safe to retry", not 413. [verified: aether/aether-invoke/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/aether/http/forward/HttpForwarderFrameLimitTest.java; lane close pinned by integrations/consensus/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/consensus/net/quic/OversizedLaneFrameTest.java]
- An integer `max_request_size` was suspected of being ignored. It is not: it is read as a byte count and clamped to `Integer.MAX_VALUE`, and is now pinned by tests. [verified: aether/aether-config/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/aether/config/ConfigLoaderSecurityTest.java]
- The response leg is checked too. A response over the frame limit is never sent: the serving node answers the ingress with a small 502 ProblemDetail ("response too large to forward"), so the lane stays open. [verified: aether/node/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/aether/http/AppHttpServerForwardBudgetTest.java]
