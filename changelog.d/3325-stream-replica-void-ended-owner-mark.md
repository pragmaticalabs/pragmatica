### Fixed (2026-11-24 — #3325: a stream replica served from the previous owner life's announced mark)
- **After an owner change, a replica that did not restart kept serving `GOVERNOR` reads from the mark the previous owner announced, for as long as the new owner's activation stayed blocked** (#3325). Since #3308 an owner announces nothing until it holds the role, a valid lease and an activated ownership, so nothing replaced the old mark. The replica now treats an announced mark as void once the committed ownership epoch is strictly after the epoch the mark was announced under (`StreamPartitionManager.behindCommittedOwner`): its `GOVERNOR` read forwards to the owner, which answers or refuses with the typed transient `OwnerNotActivated` / `OwnerLeaseLapsed`. The same rule serves persistent and in-memory streams. The guarantee is stated per operation in `aether/docs/reference/guarantees.md` (non-owner `GOVERNOR` read, arm (c)). [verified: `StreamReadRouterTest.governorRead_afterAnOwnerChange_forwardsInsteadOfServingThePreviousOwnersMark`, `OwnerAdvanceLeaseTest.markOfAnEndedOwnerLife_isVoid_whileTheNewOwnersActivationIsBlocked`]
- **Not covered:** `NEAREST` and `ANY_REPLICA` reads of a covering replica do not consult this rule (unchanged); a same-epoch restart of the owner is #3307's mechanism (the replica's `announced` flag), not this one.
