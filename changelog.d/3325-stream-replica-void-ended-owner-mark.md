### Fixed (2026-11-24 — #3325: a stream replica served from the previous owner life's announced mark)
- **After an owner change, a replica that did not restart kept serving `GOVERNOR` reads from the mark the previous owner announced, for as long as the new owner's activation stayed blocked** (#3325). Since #3308 an owner announces nothing until it holds the role, a valid lease and an activated ownership, so nothing replaced the old mark. The replica now records the epoch of the owner life that announced its mark, and treats the mark as void once that epoch is strictly before the committed ownership epoch or before the owner epoch the copy follows (a batch of the new owner arriving first does not end the void; the new owner's announcement does) (`StreamPartitionManager.replicaStaleness`, `ReplicaStaleness.VOID_OWNER_MARK`). Its `GOVERNOR` read then forwards to the owner and never reads the copy: the answer is the owner's, or the forward's own cause (the typed transient `OwnerNotActivated` / `OwnerLeaseLapsed`, a transient transport failure such as the forward timeout, or a fault no retry changes); a forward that fails with a cause that does not say the read may be retried (`PARTITION_NOT_LOCAL`, a text-only failure), and a read with no forward target (owner unknown or this node, no forward client), answer the transient `ReplicaNotVerified` from the read's offset. A copy that is only behind the mark, or has heard nothing in this life, keeps the previous degrade to its local read. The same rule serves persistent and in-memory streams. `ReplicaNotVerified`'s message now reads "cannot serve this read at or above offset N: the current owner has not confirmed this copy", true for both of its cases (wire code unchanged). The guarantee is stated per operation in `aether/docs/reference/guarantees.md` (non-owner `GOVERNOR` read, arm (c)). - **Not covered:** only the `GOVERNOR` route consults the rule. `NEAREST` and `ANY_REPLICA` reads of a covering replica do not (unchanged), nor do the validated consumer read (`StreamReadRouter.readValidated`) and a forwarded read served by a target placed as a REPLICA; whether either can serve from an ended life's mark is open in #3700. While a partition's mark is void and its owner cannot be asked, a fetch over all partitions fails whole [design intent — unverified: no test fetches all partitions across a void mark]. A same-epoch restart of the owner is #3307's mechanism (nothing announced in this node's life), not this one.
