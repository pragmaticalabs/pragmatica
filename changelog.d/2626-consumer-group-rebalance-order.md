### Fixed (2026-11-03 — #2626: two consumer-group rebalances in one commit latency could commit the older assignment last, permanently)
- **A consumer-group assignment now carries a leader-term-stamped `Epoch`, and the applier refuses a Put whose epoch is older than the committed one.** `ConsumerGroupCoordinator.rebalance` submits its assignment commands without waiting, and Rabia commits pending batches in content-hash order, so after two joins (or a join and a leave) within one commit latency the older member list could be the last one committed: a new consumer was starved, or a departed consumer kept its partitions, and nothing re-derived the assignment. `ConsumerGroupValue` is now `EpochBearing<Epoch>`; every rebalance stamps all its partitions with `Epoch(incarnation, leaderTerm, counter)`, taken together with the member snapshot, so the newest rebalance stands whatever the commit order [verified: `ConsumerGroupRebalanceOrderTest`, committing the held batches against a real `KVStore` in reversed order].
- **A deposed leader's late batch is refused, and two leaders can no longer tie.** The leader term dominates the counter, so a successor always stamps above its predecessor and a deposed leader's batch carries a lower term; within one term a single coordinator owns the counter. A rebalance whose epoch is not after the committed one (a deposed leader, or a lagging term source) is refused at the coordinator with a warning and a `SUPERSEDED` failure instead of being submitted and dropped silently by the applier; a leader re-adopted at the same term re-seeds its counter above the committed one [verified: `DeposedLeader`, `LaggingTerm`, `ReAdoptedTerm`; wiring: `ConsumerGroupCoordinatorWiringTest`].
- **Not covered:** a deposed leader's batch that commits after the successor was elected but before the successor has committed any assignment for the group is not refused; the successor's first rebalance overrides it. When the last member leaves, no command is submitted (pre-existing, tracked separately).
- **API / wire:** `ConsumerGroupValue` carries `epoch` (appended last); `ConsumerGroupValue.consumerGroupValue` takes it; `ConsumerGroupCoordinator.consumerGroupCoordinator` takes the node's `ConsumerGroupRegistry` and leader-epoch source; the wire baseline is re-recorded.
