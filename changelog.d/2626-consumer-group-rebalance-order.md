### Fixed (2026-11-03 — #2626: two consumer-group rebalances in one commit latency could commit the older assignment last, permanently)
- **A consumer-group assignment now carries a monotonic `generation`, and the applier refuses a Put that would lower it.** `ConsumerGroupCoordinator.rebalance` submits its assignment commands without waiting, and Rabia commits pending batches in content-hash order, so after two joins (or a join and a leave) within one commit latency the older member list could be the last one committed: a new consumer was starved, or a departed consumer kept its partitions, and nothing re-derived the assignment. `ConsumerGroupValue` is now `MonotonicFenced` on `generation`; every rebalance stamps all its partitions with the next generation, taken together with the member snapshot, so the newest rebalance stands whatever the commit order [verified: `ConsumerGroupRebalanceOrderTest`, committing the held batches of two joins and of join+join+leave against a real `KVStore` in reversed order].
- **A new leader starts above the committed generation.** The coordinator seeds its counter from the highest generation the node's `ConsumerGroupRegistry` already holds for the group, so a successor leader's first rebalance is not fenced out by its predecessor's. Two leaders can still stamp the same generation in a handover window, and the fence accepts equal: that cross-node ordering is not settled by this change.
- **API:** `ConsumerGroupValue` gained a fourth component (`generation`); `ConsumerGroupValue.consumerGroupValue` takes it; `ConsumerGroupCoordinator.consumerGroupCoordinator` takes the node's `ConsumerGroupRegistry`.
