### Fixed (2026-11-17 — #3307: acknowledged stream events unreadable after a restart)
- **Acknowledged events could stay unreadable after a cluster restart while every read landed on a replica** (#3307). After a restart the WAL was replayed on the owner and both replicas, but the owner raised `visible` from its acked marker only at its first admission, which only a read forwarded to the owner triggers. A replica that did not classify itself as behind served every read from its own ring, empty, indefinitely (`MultiPartitionCrashDurabilityTest`, 259 s with the events durable on three WALs). The owner now raises `visible` from the trusted acked marker when its ownership activates, guarded by the owner role still holding; an activation that finished while the lease was lapsed is retried when the lease is regained. Invariant: an owner's `visible` at activation is at least its trusted acked marker, independent of read routing. [verified: `AckedMarkerTest` (owner activation alone raises visible; lapsed lease)]
- **Not covered:** why the replica was not classified as behind the owner is still open. `commitAdvanced` now logs the announced mark, its epoch and the replica's local visible at DEBUG, so the next occurrence can be explained.
