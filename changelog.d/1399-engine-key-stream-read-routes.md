### Added (2026-11-26 — #1399: topic and entity streams are readable through the management API)
- **`GET /api/v1/engine-streams/{engineKey}/events` and `/consumers` read a stream by its raw engine key** (#1399). The four-part engine key of a durable-topic or entity stream (`topic:<ns>:<topic>:<ver>`) cannot be carried by the `{namespace}/{stream}/{version}` routes, so no management route could read its events or consumer positions. The raw-key routes resolve the key through the same catalog lookup and answer from the same owner-reported bounds as the catalog routes, on any node.
