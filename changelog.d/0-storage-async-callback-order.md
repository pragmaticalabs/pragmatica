### Fixed
- Storage-path effects that a caller depends on now land in chain order, before the operation's promise is observable, instead of in a detached callback that could run after the caller's continuation: a publisher sees its own acked write after the replication barrier, a failed start rolls the disk encryption marker back and settles the DHT admission gates before the refusal surfaces, a DHT put or remove settles the stale-writer clock and the write-pending mark before it resolves, a block delete and a cache-tier promotion are visible in the lifecycle metadata when they resolve, a JPA failure is rolled back before the caller continues, a Postgres COMMIT or ROLLBACK fires its hooks before the caller continues, and a committed consumer cursor is reported to its projection before the commit resolves.
