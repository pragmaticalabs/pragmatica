### Fixed (2026-11-04 — #2201 B-04/B-17, #1463: streams PR-2f, a restarted owner hid its acked tail and could expose a prefix sealed while unacknowledged)
- **After a restart, an owner held every record it had already made visible invisible until its replicas re-acknowledged them, and a prefix sealed while nothing acknowledged it read as visible.** Acknowledgement state was not persisted, and recovery seeded the visible position at the sealed floor.
- Each partition log now keeps an acked marker beside it (`<log>.acked`, one CRC-checked line): the owner's visible position, written on its 1 s tick and at a graceful stop (temp file and rename, no fsync), lowered durably before any WAL cut, and clamped to the recovered head at open. A log created by this build starts with a durable `-1` marker.
- At the owner's first admission after a restart (its first served read or publish, once the owner gate admits it), a trusted marker raises visible to it without waiting for replica re-acks. Trusted means the log's epoch history attributes the marker's offset to the marker's epoch, and the committed ownership record names this node with that prefix still in its lineage; a NONE- or REPLICA-role ring never raises from it [verified: aether/ember/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/aether/ember/EmberOwnerRestartAckedTailTripwireTest.java `ownerAfterColdRestart_seesItsAckedTailWithoutReplicaAcks`] [mechanism: StreamPartitionManagerAckedMarkerTest pins each conjunct at component level].
- Recovery seeds visible no higher than the marker, so a prefix sealed while unacknowledged stays invisible until a live ack [mechanism: component-level pin TieredReadVisibleBoundTest `coldRestart_overAPrefixSealedWhileUnacknowledged_readsItAsNotYetVisible`].
- Limits: a kill within one tick of the last advance loses that advance (those records wait for re-acks, bounded as before); a log written before this build, a torn or CRC-bad marker, and a partition with no WAL have no marker and restart as before, so their first restart can still expose a prefix sealed while unacknowledged. Recovery: none needed; the next replica ack restores visibility.
