### Added (2026-11-24 — #2787: Terra: cross-process schema-migration lock for multi-instance deployments)
- **Several Terra processes can share one database.** Terra's startup migrations already ran under the shared runner's database migration fence (#2591; a session-scoped lock held on a dedicated session outside the connection pool), but a second process that met the fence held failed its startup with `MigrationFencedInDatabase`. It now waits and re-runs under the fence's held-outcome rule (no failure; 15 s, then every 45 s, for as long as the holder's session lives; a warning naming the holder from the third consecutive wait), and its re-run finds the migrations applied. Every other migration failure still fails startup. [verified: `TerraMigrationsFenceTest` (two instances against live PostgreSQL 16, JDBC and pg-async: one applies, the other waits then applies none, the script is entered once)] [verified: `TerraMigrationsHeldRetryTest`]
- Guarantee: at most one session runs a datasource's scripts at a time while it holds the fence; a script in the history is not re-run; a holder that dies inside a script with non-transactional statements (history recorded separately) can lead to a re-run. [mechanism: the session-scoped fence, #2591; the history read under it]
- The wait has no timeout. To end it, terminate the holder's database session (the warning names it). [mechanism: the fence ends with its session, design of #2591]
