### Changed (2026-11-18 — #3334: Ember's tests run in six shards)
- **With the rc5 test classes forward-ported, five Ember shards planned 1522 s against the 1500 s budget**, so the planner refused and every `ember-shard-N` job failed. The plan was over capacity (about 7600 s of tests, 43 of 119 classes at the default weight), not unbalanced: each of the five shards held within 1 s of the others. Ember now runs in six shards (`ember-shard-1` .. `ember-shard-6`, and the same six in `fsm2-engine.yml`), planned at about 1270 s each. The merge gate must also require `ember-shard-6`.
