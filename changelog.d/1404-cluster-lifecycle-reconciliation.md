### Fixed (#1404: unfinished cluster lifecycle work must survive lost events and leadership changes)
- Core replacement accounting includes committed core allocations that have not entered the installed electorate. An uncertain create (DISPATCHED) always counts, so expiring a local replacement observation no longer authorizes another create; a provider-confirmed allocation counts only within the join grace, so the unjoined-replacement deficit (#1783) still reappears. Diagnostics identify `AWAITING_DURABLE_CAPACITY`. A create that was committed but never reached the provider (leadership lost between the two) is released once its instance leads again instead of staying an uncertain allocation. A refused source admission defers the replacement and is still counted, logged and trips the provisioning circuit after three consecutive refusals.
- Continuing source inventory confirms missing observed allocations and releases their capacity together with their stale worker placement. Uncertain creates remain reserved and cannot be released by an empty listing. Admission needs every source of the committed configuration inventoried (per source), and is refused when the configuration changes while the inventory is being taken; termination needs only a ledger inventoried at all.
- The periodic lifecycle poll runs source inventory reconciliation and the core orphan sweep at a low bounded rate (once per five provisioning windows, counted from activation); a node already marked unconfirmed is left to the mark re-check. Automatic departed-core deletion respects disabled auto-heal (#1038).
- Community, worker-entry and inventory passes isolate failures in bounded lanes; a stalled item delays later items in its lane by at most the observation deadline, and provider create admission is bounded per source binding.
- `READINESS_DELAYED` lets a movement that missed its readiness deadline resume without replacing its reserved identity, keeps the previous worker's allocation exclusion, and escalates delayed evacuation. A source binding outage keeps the interrupted phase and resumes when the recorded binding is restored.
- The specification records provider uncertainty and physical-scale limits; these changes do not claim 10K-node/WAN validation or close the scale gates #367/#368/#599. Implicit worker topologies are unchanged and a DISSOLVED community stays terminal; their ownership is left to the deployment-FSM work.
- Two operator events with recoveries: `core-capacity-awaiting-durable` (a core deficit held longer than the join grace by committed allocations that never joined; recovery `core-capacity-durable-resolved`) and `provision-admission-deferred` (a core replacement refused by capacity admission; recovery `provision-admission-resumed`).
- An allocation dispatched under an earlier leader term is confirmed absent by the next leader (three consecutive provider misses, after every committed source is inventoried; an instance that appears is adopted) and released with `capacity-dispatch-released`. A provision that waits for a complete inventory is counted and logged but no longer trips the provisioning circuit.
