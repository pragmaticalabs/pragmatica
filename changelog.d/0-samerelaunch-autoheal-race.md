### Fixed (2026-10-15 — EmberSameIdentityRelaunchTest read an auto-heal replacement's vote as a refused process's)
- **`relaunchedSameIdentityProcess_*_neverRestoresQuorum` was racy, not the boot-token gate.** When the leader's first reconcile pass saw the full membership before the early kill of `btk-3`, auto-heal provisioned a fresh-NodeId core 15 s later, and that core plus `btk-1` committed the post-kill write the test reads as "a refused process voted". The test now blocks provisioning (`withComputeProviderDecorator`) and asserts the live set and the survivor's MEMBER records are exactly the expected electorate before the `btk-2` kill; the heal arm gets the same. `[verified: aether/ember/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/aether/ember/EmberSameIdentityRelaunchTest.java]` The old test goes RED with an 8 s pause before the kill (forced auto-heal); the new test stays GREEN under the same pause with the refused-provision counter positive; with `BootTokens.admit` always admitting, the new test goes RED (the refused process never exits).
- **The class's other reds were harness defects too.** Its private scan of four 23000-23300 blocks (step 100) overlapped through SWIM's UDP at the QUIC port + 100, so a live or leaked cluster poisoned its neighbour and the candidates ran out ("no free port block ... after 90000 ms", 2 of 5 runs failed on an idle box); it now uses the shared probed pool (17 candidates). `coreKilledWhileForming...` failed whenever its two repetitions both missed the race; it now attempts the scenario up to 14 times (miss probability 0.5^14 = 6.1e-5) and fails only on a wrong outcome or on never reaching it. `[verified: EmberSameIdentityRelaunchTest, 6 consecutive green runs]` The sockets that stay bound after a refused or force-killed node are a separate lifecycle defect, not fixed here.
- **`EmberCluster` teardown stops what it clears (harness leak).** A node that `relaunchNode` or auto-heal added while an abort or a stop was already in flight was not in the teardown's snapshot, so `nodes.clear()` dropped it unstopped and its QUIC, management and app-HTTP sockets stayed bound until the JVM exited. Abort, failed-start and `stop()` now also stop every node the registry gained after their snapshot. `[verified: EmberClusterTeardownWiringTest#abortStart_stopsANodeAddedWhileItsStopsAreInFlight, #stop_stopsANodeAddedWhileItsStopsAreInFlight]` RED before, GREEN after. The forming-kill test now kills `btk-3` when `btk-1` reports it connected, not on a clock; the blind early kill can miss the race (the original is never seen), and whether it misses every attempt depends on the box (it reached the refusal in 4 of 4 verifier runs on a loaded Mac, through the 14-attempt retry loop, which is what makes the test robust). The demotion assertions (`awaitDemoted`, `requireElectorate`) now require the killed original to LEAVE the survivor's effective set (DEPARTING or DEAD), not merely stop being MEMBER: a link eviction moves it to SUSPECT, which still votes in the count. `[verified: EmberSameIdentityRelaunchTest#coreKilledWhileForming_andSupersededByTheSameNodeId_isDemotedOnTheSurvivor]` RED with #2173's SwimProtocol fix reverted.
