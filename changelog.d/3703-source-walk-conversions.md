### Changed (2026-11-25 — #3703: source scans in tests walked build output under a parallel build)
- **The repository's source-scanning tests now list files through `SourceWalk`, and `FlakeScanTest` refuses a new `java.nio` source walk** (#3703). A scan that walked a module tree with `Files.walk`, `Files.find` or `Files.walkFileTree` could meet a `target/` directory that a parallel build was writing and fail on a vanished or half-written entry. The stream, ember, forge-core, node, slice, resource-api, testing, cli-docs-gate and jbct-format source guards now use the pruning walk, and `FlakeScanTest` gains a `REPO_WALK` rule that flags any other source walk unless it is declared in its exception list (77 declared walks at this change). Test-only; no production behaviour changes. [verified: `FlakeScanTest` (rule and declared-walk controls), a mutant per converted scan that empties the walk goes red on the scan's own reach control]
