### Fixed (2026-11-19 — #2048: the blueprint fixture preflight checked presence, not currency)
- **The fixture preflight refuses a fixture built before the route-security contract** (#2048). `verify-blueprint-fixtures.sh` only checked that each referenced coordinate had a jar, so a slice jar left by an older slice processor reported present and then failed at deploy (`routeSecurityContract=0, current 1`), taking every suite that deploys it down for an unrelated reason. It now reads the `routeSecurityContract()` stamp of every `SliceRouterFactory` registered in the coordinate's own jars and in the slice artifacts its `blueprint.toml` lists, compares it with `SliceRouterFactory.ROUTE_SECURITY_CONTRACT` of the tree, and exits 1 naming each stale jar, its stamp and the rebuild command. [verified: `test-fixture-contract-preflight.sh` (4 of 6 cases red on the old script), 5 mutants red]
