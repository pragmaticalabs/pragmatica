### Fixed (2026-11-13 — #3140: owner activation gate could probe a peer inside its own refusal hold)
- **A second activation gate could probe a refusing peer at once, inside the hold the first one had just set.** A caller checked the partition's refusal wait, a gate run by another thread (an admit or the redrive timer) was refused and parked the partition, and only then did the caller win the partition and start its own gate on the stale check. The check is now repeated once the caller owns the partition: it releases the partition untouched and re-drives at the hold's end. The consumer's twin was #2875. `[verified: OwnerActivationHoldYieldTest]`
