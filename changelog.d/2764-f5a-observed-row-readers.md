### Changed
- Under the FSM2 deployment engine, the readers outside the two deployment state machines (endpoint registry, artifact deployment tracker, deployment map, control loop, cluster event aggregator, the drain slice-ownership query, the scheduled-task hosting hint, the worker metadata index) read the current observed slice rows instead of the `NodeArtifactKey` rows: a row stale on positive evidence (both boot tokens known and different) and the UNLOADED tombstone read as absent, and the endpoint registry also drops the endpoints of a node with a committed DEPARTED record (#2764). LEGACY behaviour is unchanged.
