### Added (2026-11-14 — #2981 step 3b: the owner-activation move fence gate, not yet wired)
- `OwnerActivation` gains a late-bound move fence (`moveFence(MoveFence)`: `FenceDeadlines` plus a `FenceProbe` of the fence target `L`). With it set, a gate whose ownership record names `L` probes `L` whether or not it is a live placement member, with the record's `(ownerEpoch, ownershipTerm)`, and races the answer against the term's deadline. `L` first: every answer of that fan-out is used, `L`'s included. Deadline first: the answers collected so far are discarded and the other members are probed again. `L` unknown (a start without an owner): nobody is probed before the full deadline. The fence completes before the lineage commit, and a gate with the fence and `LineageCommit.NONE` refuses (`FENCE_WITHOUT_LINEAGE`). [mechanism: unit-tested with a fake probe and clock, `OwnerActivationFenceTest`; not on a live path until #2981 step 3c wires the probe protocol and `StreamAssembly`]
- `FenceDeadlines.deadline` captures now as the term's base at the first read when no base was delivered, so a same-term re-gate finds the deadline passed instead of a fresh now + W; a read for an older term keeps the newer term's base. [mechanism: `FenceDeadlinesTest`]
