### Fixed (2026-11-19 — #3203: a deposed leader's late settle could reorder the operator's view of a family warning)
- **A late WARNINGS settlement no longer reaches the operator out of order** (#3203). A deposed leader that settled its own write after the successor committed a newer row of the same key published an opener the successor had already closed (left open), or a recovery the successor had re-raised (wrongly closed). The notification now carries the committed row's per-key revision; it is dropped when the node's mirror already holds a newer row of the key, and the warning sink drops a family warning older than the newest revision delivered for its `(code, subject)`.
