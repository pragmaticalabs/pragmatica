### Fixed (2026-11-20 — #3203: a deposed leader's late settle could reorder the operator's view of a family warning)
- **A late WARNINGS settlement no longer reaches the operator out of order** (#3203). A deposed leader that settled its own write after the successor committed a newer row of the same key published an opener the successor had already closed (left open), or a recovery the successor had re-raised (wrongly closed). A family warning is now published from the committed `FsmWarningKey` put (`ClusterEventAggregator.onFsmWarningPut`, through the committed-event gate: one publisher per KV index, in commit order), and no settlement publishes one on any node. The node's mirror of the warning rows takes a row only above the revision it holds, so a late settle never regresses it.
