### Changed (2026-10-18 — #2201 A-07 follow-up: the ring now applies the stream's declared max-age)
- **Behaviour change: events older than a stream's `max-age` now leave the in-memory ring on a 60 s node tick, even when the ring is not full.** Until now the age limit was never applied to the ring (`OffHeapRingBuffer.evictByAge` had no production caller); the ring kept events until capacity pressure and `max-age` governed the sealed tier alone. `StreamPartitionManager.evictRingsByAge`, scheduled by `AetherNode` every `RING_AGE_TICK_INTERVAL` (60 s), calls `evictByAge` on each materialized ring of a stream that declares a finite age, and hands the evictees to the sealer exactly as space eviction does, so every evicted event is still sealed and a consumer positioned older than `max-age` reads it from the tier. Owner ruling Q8 A (`600fe6686a`).
  `[verified: RingAgeTickTest (evicts and seals the old events, spares the fresh ones, a refused hand-over keeps them); RingAgeTickBootTest (AetherNode arms the tick on a booted node)]`
- **Who is affected:** `time`-, `size`- and `compound`-typed streams and API-created streams with the default policy (24 h); in general every non-entity stream whose config carries a finite max age, which includes durable-topic streams (their declared retention is a max age), their dead-letter streams (14 d) and the cluster-events stream (24 h by default). Not affected: `count`-typed streams (a `[streams.x]` section with no retention key declares no age, so the tick skips it) and durable-entity `entity:` rings. A stream whose retention mode this node cannot read (`UNKNOWN`, written by a newer node) is skipped, the same fail-closed stance as the sealed tier. Only `evictByAge` is wired; `applyRetention` and the tier-aware path stay unwired. Granularity is the tick: an event leaves the ring up to 60 s after it passes `max-age`. A `compound` stream under `retention-mode = "all"` is cut by age alone.
  `[mechanism: the entity exclusion and the skip of Long.MAX_VALUE are pinned by RingAgeTickTest through the sweep count and the untouched rings; the entity fixture arms the tier-aware branch (updateLastSealedOffset) so a tick that reached it would be seen]`
- **Behaviour change for `GET /api/events` (the cluster-events view):** `ClusterEventAggregator.events()` reads from the retained ring tail by design, so its window is now bounded by the stream's max age (24 h by default) instead of by ring capacity alone; older events are no longer listed. The raw stream read of the same stream is ring-only and answers `CursorExpired` (410) below the tail, unchanged.
- **Fixed: the dead-letter inspection (`DlqStreamSink.read`) listed nothing once the ring tail had moved past offset 0.** It read the ring from offset 0 and answered an empty list on `CursorExpired`, which the age tick would have made routine at the DLQ's 14 d max age. It now reads through the validated tiered read (`readServingTiered`), so dead letters that left the ring are served from the sealed tier, oldest first. Consumers of a durable topic and the pull API already fell through to the tier.
