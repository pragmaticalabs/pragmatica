### Fixed (2026-09-30 — #1766: durable entity append flattened transient failover failures into a permanent storage fault)
- **A "not ready yet" from the stream substrate read as a permanent storage failure.** `PartitionFencedDurableEntity` wrapped every non-fence append failure as `StorageFailed`, including the stream's transient failover causes (`OwnerNotActivated`, `OwnerCatchupPending`); the owner-forward wire then reported `ForwardRefused`.
- A cause the substrate marks `Cause.Transient` now becomes the new transient `EntityError.StorageUnavailable`, carried across the forward by its `failureType` and reconstructed on the sender, so callers see a retryable type (a 503 at a `Cause.Transient` boundary). Genuine storage failures stay `StorageFailed`.
