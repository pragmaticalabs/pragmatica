### Fixed (2026-11-24 — #3358: the 'scaling held' warning never closes when the held change disappears)
- **With auto-heal disabled, `auto-heal-scaling-held` stayed raised after the change it described was gone**: the core subject was asked only when a surplus existed, the worker subject only when its count differed, so a surplus that vanished by itself or by an explicit `aether nodes drain` left the warning open with no live condition, and a later re-enable raised no resume until a surplus appeared again.
- Every asker of a subject now asks on every pass it evaluates, with whether a change is pending (`ClusterTopologyManager#scalingHeld(subject, asker, pending)`, askers `RECONCILER`, `RETIREMENT`, `WORKER_DELTA`, `COMMUNITY`). A subject is held while the union of its askers' latest answers is non-empty and closes, with `auto-heal-scaling-resumed`, only when every asker has answered "nothing pending", while auto-heal stays disabled. The `core` subject has two askers (the leader's surplus drain and the voter-excluded core retirement, which decide different things), so a kept-both replacement pairing, where one has a victim and the other no drain room, holds it open instead of flapping held/resumed. The held and resumed events are published on the edges of that union, from the one atomic update of the subject, so they cannot invert. A leader change drops every answer. A `community` hold still closes only by enabling auto-heal or a committed value; a hold whose subject was removed from the configuration, or whose value changed, is closed on the next core ask (every quorum-safe pass of the leader), whichever subject is asked.
