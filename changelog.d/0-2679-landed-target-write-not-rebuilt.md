### Fixed (2026-11-06 — #2679: a landed slice-target write was rebuilt over a later write)
- **A fenced slice-target put or removal that landed is no longer rebuilt** (#2679). The confirmation of a blueprint's target put (and of a deleted blueprint's target removal) runs in the continuation of its apply, on another thread, and decided "refused" by re-reading the store. A write that committed after it but before the continuation ran was misread as a refusal: the put was rebuilt and wrote the blueprint's version back over the later target (the extra submission of the flaky `SliceTargetOrderingTest$AwaitedResubmission`), and the removal was rebuilt and removed a target created after it. The outcome now comes from the transaction's own `TransactionResult`, matched on its id; only a write the results do not report accepted re-reads.
