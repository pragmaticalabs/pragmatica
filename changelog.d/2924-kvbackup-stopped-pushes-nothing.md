### Fixed (2026-11-09 — #2924: a stopped KV backup could still push)
- **A KV backup pass in flight at the stop could push after `stopFlushed` had returned** (#2924). The stop's interrupt could be swallowed while git's output was read, and nothing checked the stop before the push. The interrupt now stays visible to the worker, and a stopped service leaves its commit queued locally instead of pushing.
