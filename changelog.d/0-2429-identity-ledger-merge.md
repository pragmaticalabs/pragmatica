### Fixed
- A core whose identity ledger file was lost can rejoin once a majority of the configured core seats is reachable, instead of staying isolated for good (#2429). The QUIC Hello now carries the retirements the sender can vouch for (a ledger that is loaded and not wiped); a wiped receiving core records the offers, and when a majority of the seats has offered it unions them into its own ledger, forces it to disk, and only then admits anyone. A wiped core admits no new peer until a majority of the configured core seats has offered, counting its own seat and never the offer of the peer being admitted, so a process for a retired NodeId with an empty ledger cannot release it by connecting first; with too few seats reachable it stays isolated (fails closed). The Hello gains a sixth component, a wire change under the pre-GA ruling.
