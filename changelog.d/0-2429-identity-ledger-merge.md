### Fixed
- A core whose identity ledger file was lost can rejoin once a majority of the other configured core seats is reachable, instead of staying isolated for good (#2429). The QUIC Hello now carries the retirements the sender can vouch for (a ledger that is loaded and not wiped). A wiped core admits no new peer: it keeps each seat's offer durably, and when the offering seats are a majority of the other core seats and at least one is not the peer in hand, it writes the union, forces it to disk, and only then judges peers (the peer that ended the state on its redial). A retired stray with an empty ledger can neither end the state alone nor be admitted by it; with too few seats reachable the core stays isolated (fails closed). The Hello gains a sixth component, a wire change under the pre-GA ruling.
