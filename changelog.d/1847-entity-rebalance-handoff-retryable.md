### Fixed (2026-10-02 — #1847: entity owner answered a terminal UnknownKeyspace during a rebalance handoff)
- **A CDM rebalance that moved an entity slice made the old owner refuse forwarded requests terminally for ~4-9 s.** Unloading unregistered the keyspace's forward target at once, but the node stayed the COMMITTED owner until its reconcile pruned its registration and the leader re-minted (5 s each). In that gap the owner answered `UnknownKeyspace`, which the sender kept as a plain `ForwardRefused`, so neither clients nor the 02w harness retried (run 8: 4/40 creates, 1 readback).
  - A node with no local target that committed state still names as host or owner of the keyspace now answers the Transient `EntityError.OwnerTransitioning`; the sender rebuilds it as a Transient cause, so callers retry on the existing transient policy. "Named by committed state" is a `(keyspace, self)` hosting registration OR an `entity:<keyspace>` ownership record naming self (`CommittedEntityStake`), the second covering the gap between the node's prune and the leader's re-mint.
  - A node with neither keeps the terminal `UnknownKeyspace`: a genuinely unknown keyspace is never retried.
  - The sender also rebuilds the owner-side not-ready answers `OwnershipNotYetCommitted` and `FoldInProgress` as `OwnerTransitioning`; they were previously flattened to the terminal carrier on the forward wire.
  - A COMMITTED owner that does not hold the partition's ring yet (materialize pending, e.g. #1805) now answers the same transient `OwnerTransitioning` instead of a terminal `PartitionNotHeld`; a node that is not the committed owner keeps the terminal `PartitionNotHeld`. If the ring never materializes, callers now retry until their own deadline instead of failing at once.
  - The unload path now requests an immediate reconcile (`EntityOwnershipReconciler.retract`), and a committed registration removal wakes the leader's mint, instead of each waiting for its periodic tick. The tick is `synchronized`, since it now has three triggers. Ownership still leaves the node asynchronously, so a short window remains by design; the transient answer is what makes it retryable. No sleep, grace timer or retry loop was added.
  - 02w's `ENTITY_TRANSIENT_FAILURE_TYPES` gains `OwnerTransitioning`. No wire change: the type travels as the existing `failureType` string.
