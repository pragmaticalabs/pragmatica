### Fixed (2026-11-26 — #3471: the released-port control could not tell a correct skip from a refused free port)
- **The test-port allocator records why it skipped a candidate, one cause per skip path, and the released-port controls assert on that record** (#3471). `ClusterTestPortsTest`'s "accepted once released" control re-probed the port after the call, so a contender that bound and released the port inside the allocator's probe failed it as "refused although it is free" (third occurrence after #2902 and #3188; it ejected #3775 from the rc6 queue). The allocator now records, per skipped candidate, a closed `SkipCause`: `OutsideWindow`, `LockRefused` (this JVM already holds the port, or another JVM holds the lock) or `ProbeBusy`, which carries EVERY probe that read the candidate busy (cluster UDP, cluster TCP, SWIM UDP, probing on to completion) and cannot be built from an empty set (its factory refuses one). The control passes a refusal only for a `ProbeBusy`; a lock or window skip is never read as a probe and its failure names the cause. Every skip path releases the locks it took and leaves no probe socket. No retry, window or sleep.
