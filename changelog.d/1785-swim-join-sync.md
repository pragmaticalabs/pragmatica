### Fixed (2026-09-30 — #1783: SWIM joiners could miss each other for good)
- **The SWIM ANNOUNCE reply now carries the responder's membership view.** A member is gossiped only to the peers its gossiper probes, and each update is evicted after `maxDisseminations` peeks, so two nodes that joined at different times could each miss the other's join gossip and never learn each other (two replacement cores minted 30 s apart in the rc4 cloud run). The seed now answers an accepted ANNOUNCE with its ALIVE and SUSPECT members as unsolicited `Ack`s (sequence 0; at most four datagrams, each within the piggyback byte budget, order shuffled per reply). The joiner merges them through the same precedence as gossip, so a stale entry cannot resurrect a FAULTY or removed member. The joiner's own pings then carry its self-ALIVE to every member it learned, which closes the other direction.
- No new wire type, so no wire tag: an old node merges the piggyback and ignores sequence 0; an old seed sends no reply (the previous behaviour).
- Not included: periodic push-pull. Healing of lost gossip after the join phase stays with gossip and probing.
