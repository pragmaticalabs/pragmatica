### Security (2026-10-18 — #2248: no read or idle timeout on the HTTP listeners)
- **A client could hold a connection and its aggregation buffer open indefinitely by stalling or trickling its body.** A new `[app-http] read_timeout` setting (default `60s`; `HttpServerConfig.readTimeout`) closes an HTTP/1.1 connection that sends no byte for that long while no response is pending. Over HTTP/3 the same setting bounds the whole request body from its headers, answering 408 past it, because QUIC's 5 s idle timeout never fires on a stream that trickles. Upgraded WebSocket connections are exempt. [verified: integrations/net/http-server/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/http/server/HttpReadTimeoutTest.java, Http3RequestHandlerTest]
- Over HTTP/1.1 the timeout bounds silence, not rate: a client sending a byte just inside the timeout is still not bounded. A minimum-rate limit belongs to the upload overhaul.
