### Security (2026-10-18 — #2248: no read, idle or minimum-rate bound on the HTTP listeners)
- **A client could hold a connection and its aggregation buffer open indefinitely by stalling or trickling its body.** Two bounds now apply with the same semantics over HTTP/1.1 and HTTP/3. `[app-http] read_timeout` (default `60s`) refuses a body idle that long. `[app-http] min_body_rate` (default `1KB` per second, after a 10 s grace, averaged over 10 s windows; http-server `BodyRateLimit`) refuses a body arriving slower. Over HTTP/1.1 the connection is closed. Over HTTP/3 the request gets 408, its stream input is reset, and it is never dispatched. A 1 B/s trickle is refused on both protocols, and a 5 MiB upload at 50 KiB/s completes on both. HTTP/3 no longer bounds the whole body by a deadline. Upgraded WebSocket connections are exempt.
