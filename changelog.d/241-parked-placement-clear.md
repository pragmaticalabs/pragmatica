### Added (2026-10-20 — #241: an operator verb and an event for parked community placement operations)
- **A community placement operation parked in `DRAIN_UNCERTAIN` (drain acknowledgement deadline passed) or in a `BLOCKED` whose target is never observed had no way out**: the reconciler left it for ever and the only trace was a generic lifecycle event with no recovery. The reconciler now raises `community-placement-parked` when an operation enters either phase (naming the operation, both nodes and the command), and its recoveries `community-placement-resumed` (the operation left the phase by itself, a late drain acknowledgement or the target observed) and `community-placement-cleared`.
- **New operator verb `aether cluster clear-placement <community> --reason <text>`** (`POST /api/v1/cluster/communities/placement/clear/{id}`, OPERATOR, leader-bound): closes ONE parked operation with a single expected-value write to `COMPLETE`, audited with the authenticated operator and the reason. It removes, drains and terminates nothing; the previous worker keeps its directive and placement. It is refused for a community with no operation (404), for an operation that is not parked or that resumes by itself, and on a node that is not the leader (409).
