### Fixed (2026-11-26 — #3740: the worker quorum-loss tests began a second replacement on a signal the leader had not yet reached)
- **`EmberWorkerQuorumLossTest` and `EmberWorkerRealQuorumLossTest` wait for the replacement's `DONE` on the leader, the node whose index `begin` is refused from** (#3740). They waited for "any node shows `DONE`"; every node applies the leader's commits at slightly different moments, so a follower could show `DONE` while the leader still held the record live and the next `begin` was refused as `AlreadyReplacing`. The product was consistent: `status` and the refusal read the same leader-local index, pinned by a new `NodeReplacementServiceTest` case. The wait is now the shared `ReplacementSignal.settledOnLeader`, unit-tested with a follower ahead of the leader. Test-only; no product change.
