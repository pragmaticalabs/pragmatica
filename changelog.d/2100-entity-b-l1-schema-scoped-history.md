### Added (2026-11-14 — #2100 E-B L1: schema-scoped migration history in `db-migrations`)
- **`SchemaMigrations.schemaMigrations(TimeSpan, Option<PgSchemaName>)` and `SchemaHistoryRepository.schemaHistoryRepository(Option<PgSchemaName>)`** keep the history, meta and owner tables inside a PostgreSQL schema, so each schema has its own version sequence and ownership claim; the database fence stays per database. The runner creates an absent schema after the fence is held. A role that may not is refused with `MigrationError.SchemaCreateDenied` (nothing ran); any engine other than PostgreSQL is refused with `MigrationError.SchemaQualifierUnsupported` before the fence. With `none`, every history statement is byte-identical to the unscoped form.
