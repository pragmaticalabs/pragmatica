The committed governor now relays a member's death — only when its OWN failure detector (SWIM) reached terminal DEAD for that member — as a `MemberDeparture(node, bootToken)` in its next health report (new wire tag 2132; `Report` gains `departures`). A governor's death or silence relays nothing, so it evicts nobody; staleness is never a removal signal.
