### Fixed (2026-11-25 — #1030: a failed bootstrap named cloud-init logs it had just deleted)
- **Bootstrap failure advice states the outcome for the VMs, not the intent** (#1030). The formation-timeout and health-gate failures told the operator to check the VMs' cloud-init logs even when the failed run had removed those VMs. Now: with `--keep-on-failure` the error names the logs and where they are (`ssh aether@<vm-ip>`); when the cleanup succeeded it says the recorded VMs were removed, the logs did not survive, and names `--keep-on-failure` for the next run; when the cleanup FAILED (including an unreadable ledger) it says VMs may still be running and names the logs, never "removed". One text source (`BootstrapError.VmSurfaceFailure`), resolved from a three-valued `VmFate` inside `decorateAfterCleanup` after the cleanup has run. [verified: `BootstrapVmSurfaceAdviceTest`]
