### Fixed (2026-11-25 — #1030: a failed bootstrap named cloud-init logs it had just deleted)
- **Bootstrap failure advice follows what happens to the VMs** (#1030). The formation-timeout and health-gate failures told the operator to check the VMs' cloud-init logs even when the failed run was already removing those VMs. With `--keep-on-failure` the error now names the logs and where they are (`ssh aether@<vm-ip>`); without it, it says the VMs are being removed and names `--keep-on-failure` to keep them on the next run. One text source (`BootstrapError.VmSurfaceFailure`), resolved by `decorateAfterCleanup` from the flag the caller already holds. [verified: `BootstrapVmSurfaceAdviceTest`]
