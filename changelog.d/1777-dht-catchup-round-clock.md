### Fixed (2026-10-04 — DHT catch-up gate: a test pinned a 45 ms real-time margin, and a decision stamp lived in two fields)

- **`DHTCatchUpGateTest.roundDecidedOnAnswersInHand_survivesTheNextTick_untilItsSlowPullLands` failed on CI** (1 of 325 in `integrations/dht`, at the readiness assertion). It slept 5 ms between the decision and the next tick against a 50 ms "kept for one more round timeout" window, so a stall of more than 45 ms between the two ticks was the documented replacement of the round, not a defect. Measured on a loaded machine: 14 of 250 runs red under CPU load, 0 of 250 in isolation, every failure with the CI signature (`expected: SERVING but was: CATCHING_UP`).
- **Catch-up rounds now read their ages from an injectable clock** (`System.nanoTime` in production), so the test moves time by hand: the kept-pulls window and its far edge are both pinned deterministically. After the fix, the same interleaved isolation-versus-load measurement shows 0 of 500 in each arm.
- **A round's decision and its time stamp are now one atomic step** (`Long.MIN_VALUE` means undecided, so no clock origin, a negative one included, can collide with it). They were two fields, so a tick between them could read "decided" with a stamp of zero and replace the round. Not the cause of the CI red (single-threaded test), but the same decision.
