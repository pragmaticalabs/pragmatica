### Removed (2026-11-12 — #3002: Promise on*Async aliases)
- **Breaking: `Promise.onResultAsync`, `onResultRunAsync`, `onSuccessAsync`, `onSuccessRunAsync`, `onFailureAsync` and `onFailureRunAsync` are removed.** They delegated to the synchronous forms and promised an asynchrony they did not add. Use `onResult`/`onResultRun`/`onSuccess`/`onSuccessRun`/`onFailure`/`onFailureRun` (unchanged semantics), or `continueOn(...)` for a real thread hop. `JBCT-ASYNC-01` no longer lists them, and its documentation now states where event callbacks actually run: on a promise pending when they are attached, together on one virtual thread in sequence after resolution; on an already-resolved promise, inline on the attaching thread.
