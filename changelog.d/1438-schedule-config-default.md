### Fixed (2026-09-29 — #1438: both documented `[scheduling.x]` shapes failed to bind and published no task)
- **An interval-only or cron-only `[scheduling.<name>]` section now binds and publishes its task.** `ScheduleConfig` had no `DEFAULT`, which is the only way the config binder supplies an absent key. So the two shapes in `resource-reference.md` each failed with "Config section not found: ScheduleConfig.cron" (or `.interval`), logged one WARN, and let the slice reach ACTIVE with no scheduled task. Only a section listing all three keys worked. [verified: aether/aether-deployment/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/aether/deployment/node/fsm/NodeDeploymentStateScheduledTaskPublishTest.java — both documented shapes publish their task; red before the fix]
- **A section with neither `interval` nor `cron` now fails the slice's activation** (the slice reports FAILED), where before it bound-failed silently and the slice activated without a task. Operator action: add `interval` or `cron` to the section and redeploy. [verified: same test]
