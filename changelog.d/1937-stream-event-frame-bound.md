### Fixed (2026-10-05 — #1937: stream max-event-size had no bound, and two stream catch-up alarms hid each other)

- **`max-event-size` above what one transport frame can carry is now refused at config validation.** A single event travels alone in one replication or catch-up message, so a stream declaring more than the frame less its envelope could be created and then fail at its first oversized event. The ceiling is `OutboundMessageLimit.MAX_TRANSFER_BYTES` (the 32 MiB frame less its reserve) minus a 4 KiB envelope reserve, and the reserve is pinned against the real messages: `StreamEventFrameBoundTest` encodes `ReplicateEvents` and `CatchupResponse` through the node's codec registry (worst-case names, a catch-up history) with a payload of exactly the ceiling and requires them to fit; measured envelopes are 324 bytes (replicate) and 306 plus 72 per history entry (catch-up). Refusal is the typed `StreamDeclarationError.ValueAboveMaximum`.
  [verified: `StreamConfigParserTest` (ceiling accepted, one byte over and 40 MB refused), `StreamEventFrameBoundTest`; reserve = 0 reddens all three frame pins]
- **A promoted owner's backfill now raises `STREAM_EVENT_EXCEEDS_READ_CAP` once per transition**, like the activation gate, instead of a WARN on every redrive. The condition ending and returning raises again.
  [verified: `PartitionBackfillTest...raisesTheBlockOncePerTransition`, `OperatorWarningWiringTest.assembly_givesTheSinkToTheBackfillBlockAlarm`; removing the raise, the dedupe or the wiring each reddens its test]
- **While one peer is refused for an oversized event, another peer's unreachable alarm is no longer masked.** The two are independent conditions: the unreachable timer and its block have their own slot, and the oversized refusal no longer returns before the timer is evaluated. A recovered silent peer ends its own block and leaves the oversized one standing, never re-raising either.
  [verified: `ByteCappedPeerGateTest.oversizedPeer_doesNotMaskAnotherPeersUnreachableAlarm`, `...unreachablePeerRecovers_...`; reverting to the early return reddens both]
  [limit: the status read (`ownerActivationBlock`) shows one block per partition, the oversized one when both stand]
