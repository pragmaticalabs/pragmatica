### Added (2026-11-20 — #3458 stream-mem M-9a0: forward-client reads that carry the life)
- **The stream forward client gains two additive reads; no caller changes.** `readRemoteLinearizable` forwards a `LINEARIZABLE` read stamped with the epoch the caller last read under and resolves with the events and the epoch they were served under. `boundsWithLifeRemote` is `boundsRemote` that also keeps the life the bounds were answered under, as `BoundsUnderLife`.
- No wire change: both reuse the existing `ReadForward` components through one new factory, `linearizableValidatedReadForward`. A client that does not override them forwards a plain `LINEARIZABLE` read and reports no epoch [verified: aether/aether-stream/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/aether/stream/forward/StreamForwardClientLifeReadsTest.java].
