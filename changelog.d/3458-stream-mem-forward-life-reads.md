### Added (2026-11-20 — #3458 stream-mem M-9a0: forward-client reads that carry the life)
- **The stream forward client gains two additive reads; no caller changes.** `readRemoteLinearizable` forwards a `LINEARIZABLE` read stamped with the epoch the caller last read under and is not usable yet: the owner honours the life only once M-9a2 lands, and until then answers `Epoch.ZERO` (pinned by a tripwire through the real handler); no caller uses it at this head. `boundsWithLifeRemote` is `boundsRemote` that also keeps the life the bounds were answered under, as `BoundsUnderLife`.
- No wire change: both reuse the existing `ReadForward` components through one new factory, `linearizableValidatedReadForward`. A client that does not override them forwards a plain `LINEARIZABLE` read and silently drops the life, so it reports no epoch [verified: aether/aether-stream/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/aether/stream/forward/StreamForwardClientLifeReadsTest.java, aether/aether-stream/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/aether/stream/forward/StreamForwardLifeReadsHandlerTest.java].
