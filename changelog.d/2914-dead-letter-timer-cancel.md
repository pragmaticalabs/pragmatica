### Fixed (2026-11-11 — #2914: a dead-letter retry timer keeps running after unsubscribe, so a cancelled consumer can still append a dead letter)
- **A consumer unsubscribed while its dead-letter append was backing off could still append the dead letter when the timer fired**, after its detach flush had committed the cursor; the successor then re-delivered the event and could dead-letter it a second time.
- Every dead-letter append (first attempt and backoff retry) now claims its in-flight slot in one step with the cancel check (`ConsumerState.claimAdvance`, the same monitor as `cancel()`), as deliveries and delivery retries already did (#1960): a timer that fires concurrently with the unsubscribe either runs its append before the detach flush commits, or does nothing. [verified: `DeadLetterTimerAfterCancelTest`]
- The dead-letter retry timer is scheduled through the runtime's existing delayed-task seam (`SharedScheduler` in production), so the test fires it without waiting.
