### Fixed (2026-09-19 — #1243: projection dedup cannot be made atomic through its interface)
- **`ProjectionClaims` exposed only `get`/`put`, so the projection guard's check-then-record pair could never be atomic**, and its javadoc promised suppression under an "atomic and shared backing" condition no implementation could meet. Two attempts on one message that both read before either recorded both applied.
- `ProjectionClaims` now takes a leased, fenced claim in one step — `claimIfAbsent(key, lease)` answers `Claimed(token)` / `DONE` / `IN_PROGRESS` — and the fold runs under it: finalize on success, release on failure, both conditional on the claim still carrying the caller's token (otherwise `STALE`: no change, logged at WARN), and a PENDING claim whose lease expired is reclaimable under a new token. An attempt that outlives its lease therefore cannot finalize or release its successor's claim. An attempt that finds a live claim fails retryably instead of being acknowledged. `Projection.withClaims` now takes the lease. `[mechanism: the single claimIfAbsent step decides; fold runs only on Claimed; finalize/release are token-conditional]`, pinned in-JVM by `ProjectionTest.IdempotencyGuard` against in-memory fakes.
- The guarantee is now stated per operation in `Projection` and `durable-pubsub-spec.md` §8: concurrent attempts on one `(projection, generation, messageId)` are suppressed to one apply among instances sharing a backing whose claim is one indivisible step; a crash between fold and finalize re-applies after lease expiry, and an attempt outliving its lease still folds beside its successor (the token fences the claim, not the write), so a non-idempotent fold is at-least-once in that window. `[unverified: no production ProjectionClaims backing exists yet; cross-instance suppression has not run on a live multi-node path]`
