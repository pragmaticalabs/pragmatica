### Fixed (2026-11-18 — #3384: PartitionBackfillJoinedRerunTest read the backfill slot before its release)
- **Two `PartitionBackfillJoinedRerunTest` tests could fail with "a later call pulls" on a loaded host** (#3384). They waited for a flight's slot release with a single `parkNanos`, which may return early (a CI failure ran the whole test in 9 ms), so the later call joined a flight still in the slot and pulled nothing. The tests now wait for the release itself through the release test seam, with a deadline, and the shared `sleep` parks until its deadline. Test-only: the product releases the slot on every landing and at every hold's end. [verified: `PartitionBackfillJoinedRerunTest`]
