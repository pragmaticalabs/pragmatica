### Fixed (2026-11-14 — #2539: AppendLog.close races group commit at stop: a late append fails as 'WAL append failed: null')
- **An append that raced or followed a stop failed with a null-detail `WAL append failed: null` and fail-stopped the log.** A channel closed by an interrupt (`ClosedByInterruptException`) or an asynchronous close surfaced as a null-message I/O fault, which logged an error and wrote `<log>.failstopped` at a stop already in progress.
- `AppendLog.close()` now sets an intent flag before it takes any lock. A closed channel (`ClosedChannelException`, including its interrupt and asynchronous-close subclasses) found while that flag is set reports `WAL_CLOSED`, as after `close()`: no fail-stop, no error log, no mark. The same exception with no `close()` begun (a stray interrupt, a channel closed beneath the log) is a fault: the log fail-stops, writes the mark, reports to the fail-stop listener and logs an ERROR whose detail is the exception class name. Every other I/O fault still fail-stops with its detail, never null. [verified: integrations/storage/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/storage/AppendLogCloseAppendTest.java]
- An interrupt that reaches a writer before `close()` has begun is a fault by this rule, so a stop must call `close()` before it interrupts the threads still appending, or that interrupt fail-stops the log. Recovery from a fail-stop is unchanged: reopen (node restart) recovers the valid prefix. [design intent — unverified for the stop order of callers]
- An append admitted before `close()` is committed by the close-time force, also when the closing thread is interrupted: the close clears the interrupt around that force and restores it afterwards. One that loses to `close()` or follows it gets `WAL_CLOSED`. [verified: integrations/storage/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/storage/AppendLogCloseAppendTest.java]
