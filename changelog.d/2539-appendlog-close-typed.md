### Fixed (2026-11-13 — #2539: AppendLog.close races group commit at stop: a late append fails as 'WAL append failed: null')
- **An append that raced or followed a stop failed with a null-detail `WAL append failed: null` and fail-stopped the log.** A channel closed beneath the log (an interrupted thread, an asynchronous close) surfaced as a null-message I/O fault, which logged an error and wrote `<log>.failstopped` at a stop already in progress.
- Such a channel now reports `WAL_CLOSED`, as after `close()`: no fail-stop, no error log, no mark. Every other I/O fault keeps its detail, falling back to the exception class name, so the detail is never null. An append admitted before `close()` is committed by the close-time force; one that loses to `close()` or follows it gets `WAL_CLOSED`.
