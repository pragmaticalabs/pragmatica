### Changed (2026-10-18 — stream config keys are snake_case; dashed keys are refused)
- **BREAKING (pre-GA, no aliases): every key of a `[streams.<alias>]` section, and of its `[streams.<alias>.consumers.<group>]` sub-sections, is spelled snake_case, matching topic keys and the node `[streaming]` keys.** Renamed: `retention-value`, `retention-mode`, `max-age`, `max-count`, `max-bytes`, `auto-offset-reset`, `max-event-size`, `encryption-key-id` (stream section) and `checkpoint-interval`, `batch-size`, `on-failure`, `max-retries`, `dead-letter`, `read-preference` (consumer sections) become `retention_value`, `retention_mode`, `max_age`, `max_count`, `max_bytes`, `auto_offset_reset`, `max_event_size`, `encryption_key_id`, `checkpoint_interval`, `batch_size`, `on_failure`, `max_retries`, `dead_letter`, `read_preference`.
- A dashed key now fails loudly instead of being read: the stream parser refuses it with `UnknownStreamKeys`, whose message names the snake_case spelling (`'max-event-size' (did you mean 'max_event_size'?)`), at deploy validation and at slice activation. A dashed consumer key is refused the same way rather than ignored, which would have applied its default silently. Before, the refusal ran the other way: snake_case was refused and the dashed spelling was named. Update any blueprint `resources.toml` or `aether.toml` that declares a stream; the examples, test blueprints and docs are updated.
