### Fixed (2026-11-16 — #3297: CI jobs still hit Maven Central 429 after the retry settings)
- **CI jobs no longer share a first-saver-wins Maven cache** (#3297). setup-java saved the cache from whichever job finished first under one key shared by jobs that resolve different artifacts, so a cache hit could still lack artifacts (jackson-dataformat-xml, plexus-utils) and send the job to Central. Now forge-tests (full reactor) is the only saver, on success, and every other job in ci.yml and fsm2-engine.yml restores it with a previous-generation fallback.
