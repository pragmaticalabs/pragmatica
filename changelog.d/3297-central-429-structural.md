### Fixed (2026-11-16 — #3297: CI jobs still hit Maven Central 429 after the retry settings)
- **CI jobs no longer share a first-saver-wins Maven cache** (#3297). setup-java saved the cache from whichever job finished first under one key shared by jobs that resolve different artifacts, so a cache hit could still lack artifacts (jackson-dataformat-xml, plexus-utils) and send the job to Central. Now forge-tests (full reactor) is the only saver, on success, and every other job in ci.yml and fsm2-engine.yml restores it with a previous-generation fallback. [unverified: needs real CI runs; the 429 rate on Central cannot be forced on demand]
- **Bootstrap gap closed** (#3297, 2026-11-16). The first merge-queue attempt of this fix failed: GitHub caches are scoped by ref, so the merge-group run could not read the `m2-full` cache saved on the PR ref, and rc5 held none. Every restore step now also falls back to the base branch's older `setup-java-Linux-x64-maven-` cache. With neither cache present (first run, eviction) jobs resolve from Central with the #3280 retries.
