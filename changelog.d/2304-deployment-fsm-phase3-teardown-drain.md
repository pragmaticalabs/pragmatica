### Added (2026-10-19 — #2304: deployment state machine rewrite, phase 3 P3-E: teardown steps, stop drain, restore and drain-report paths, backlog sampler)
- **The slice runtime adapter now executes every local step.** `PREPARE_TEARDOWN`, `DEACTIVATE`, `UNLOAD`, `FORCED_CLEANUP` and `NOTIFY_CONFIG` run for real, so under FSM2 `ConfigChanged` is bound on a core (a worker's hook stays a counted drop) and the step switch is exhaustive: a step added to `LocalStep` is a compile error. The teardown batch is built from copies of the legacy `doUnpublish*` builders, compared with them by a golden test; a forced cleanup releases only the node-scoped legs and keeps the cluster-scoped ones `[guess: the direction of the error]`.
  [verified: `aether/aether-deployment/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/aether/deployment/wiring/SliceRuntimeAdapterTest.java`, `aether/aether-deployment/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/aether/deployment/wiring/SliceLegBuildersGoldenTest.java`, `aether/aether-deployment/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/aether/deployment/wiring/TeardownChainTest.java`]
- **A FSM2 node's stop drains before the router is quiesced.** PASSIVE is routed, the node interpreter resolves a marker queued behind the quorum loss and waits for the local-op executor to be idle (the `SUSPEND`s have run), and only then is the router quiesced. The wait is bounded (10 s per interpreter `[guess: settled by E-07]`): on expiry `deployment-fsm-stop-timeout` is logged and the stop goes on, and a consumer stuck in an input is not waited for a second time.
  [verified: `aether/node/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/aether/node/deployment/Fsm2SystemTest.java`, `aether/aether-deployment/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/aether/deployment/wiring/CloseAndOverrunProbeTest.java`]
- **A drain report and a snapshot restore reach the cluster FSM under FSM2.** `NodeDraining` is the cluster FSM's input; `onStateRestored` re-incarnates a leader FSM that is Active or Rebuilding (`Deactivate`, `Activate`, a fresh snapshot) and does nothing, counted, when it is Dormant, which is the ordinary case.
  [verified: `aether/node/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/aether/node/deployment/Fsm2SystemTest.java`, `aether/aether-deployment/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/aether/deployment/wiring/ClusterFsmInterpreterTest.java`]
- **The deployment FSM mailboxes are watched.** Every 5 s the depth of each mailbox is read: `deployment-fsm-backlog` (WARNING) is raised above 10,000 queued inputs and `deployment-fsm-backlog-cleared` (INFO) below 1,000, with hysteresis between; the subject is `<node>/<fsm>`. The thresholds and the cadence are guesses pending the E-13 high-water.
  [verified: `aether/aether-deployment/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/aether/deployment/wiring/BacklogSamplerTest.java`, `aether/node/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/aether/node/deployment/Fsm2SystemTest.java`]
- **Named limits.** The order of `AetherNode.stop()` (PASSIVE, drain, quiesce) is pinned by a text-level scan of the source, not by a booted node. The worker arms of the drain report and the restore are counted drops (no `FsmRuntime` on workers yet).
  [verified: `aether/node/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/aether/node/deployment/StopSequencePinTest.java`]
