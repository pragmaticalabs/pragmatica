### Fixed (2026-11-20 — #3477: EmberReplicationBatchingTest.singlePublishes_areBatchedIntoFewerMessages is timing-bound)
- **The test measured the scheduler.** The 1 ms production batch window closed after a few events whenever 16 callers took longer than that to make 192 publishes, so a loaded runner counted 101 messages per replica against a bound of 96. `replicateEvent` is fire-and-forget, so the callers never wait on replication and nothing paced them to the window.
- The test cluster now runs with a 500 ms `replication_batch_max_delay` and the test waits for delivery instead of sleeping 1 s; the bound (96) is unchanged and is out of reach unless the burst outlasts about 95 windows. No product change.
