### Fixed (2026-11-20 — #3477: EmberReplicationBatchingTest.singlePublishes_areBatchedIntoFewerMessages is timing-bound)
- **The test measured the scheduler.** A publish waits for its replicas' acks, so 16 callers are a closed loop and a round of 16 events shares one batch window. The 1 ms production window split a round whenever one caller lagged the others by more than 1 ms, so a loaded runner counted 101 messages per replica against a bound of 96.
- The test cluster now runs with a 20 ms `replication_batch_max_delay` (about 12 messages per replica for 192 events, bound 96 unchanged) and the test waits for delivery instead of sleeping 1 s. No product change. Longer windows (100 ms, 500 ms) are deliberately not used: they stalled 1 and 4 of 30 repeated runs for the 5 s ack timeout, a separate defect.
