### Fixed (2026-11-11 — #2688: stop() could quiesce past a leader change in flight when it took the wait)
- **`stop()` drains queued leader changes before reading the pending deactivation** (#2688). A `LeaderChange(self)` that passed the stopping gate before `markStopping` and was still running its route on the `LeaderManager` executor left the stop's wait resolved, and the deactivation enqueued after the drain marker was dropped by the quiesce, so the leader-toggled components could stay active. The wait now drains the executor first, then reads the pending deactivation, then drains again.
