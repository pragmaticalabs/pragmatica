### Changed
- `STREAM_RETENTION_CHANGED` (#3334) is published through the committed gate of the cluster events derived from KV notifications: the node named by the committed ownership record of the cluster-events partition (or, with no record, the committed leader) publishes it, instead of whichever node's local view claims the partition. The eventId it carries is the raiser's, unchanged. Nothing raises the event yet; the raiser follows in a later step. [mechanism: unit-tested only, `ClusterEventAggregatorCommittedGateTest` (two aggregators fed the same notifications)]
