### Fixed (2026-10-11 — a leader change mid-replacement trimmed a healthy follower)
- **A voter is removed only on observed evidence of failure.** A freshly elected leader ranked every voter it had not yet observed as unready, so its heal could drop a healthy voter during a replacement swap and the surplus trim then drained it (`FAILED_KEPT_BOTH`). Now a voter is removable only when it was observed not READY or membership holds it Dead or Departing; a never-observed voter is never removed, however long the leader has led. A swap's leaver is always the original. [verified: `CoreVoterReconcilerTest`; reverting the evidence filter turns three of its cases red]
- **A startup config race defers instead of rolling back.** A replacement that began before the bootstrap source configuration was committed was rolled back with "Committed source configuration required for fleet inventory". Provisioning now waits for the configuration, bounded by the provisioning deadline, and a configuration that never appears ends the replacement with a cause naming the missing committed configuration. [verified: `NodeReplacementEnvTest`]
