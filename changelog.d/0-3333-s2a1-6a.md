### Changed (2026-11-20 — #3333: `[retention]`, `[truncation]` and `[hot]` are parsed under `[streams.<alias>]`)
- A stream section may now carry the sub-sections `[streams.<alias>.retention]` (`max_age`, `max_bytes`, `max_count`, `mode`), `[streams.<alias>.truncation]` (`policy`, `group_idle_expiry`) and `[streams.<alias>.hot]` (`max_bytes`, `max_count`). The parsed, resolved declaration is stored in `StreamConfig.declaration`: a stream with no `[retention]` block resolves to `max_age` 24 h, `max_bytes = max(1 GiB, P x 2 x seal_merge_bytes)` and `mode` any; a block stores exactly the bounds it names plus the default `mode`; `[hot]` resolves each key it does not name (`max_bytes = max(4 MiB, max_event_size)`, `max_count` the ring default); `[truncation]` resolves `policy` to `window`. Today's top-level retention keys still drive the retention policy the engine enforces; nothing enforces the stored declaration yet. [mechanism: unit-tested, `StreamConfigParserDeclarationTest`]
- **Behaviour change, refusals at deploy and at activation:** a `[retention]` block that names none of `max_age`, `max_bytes`, `max_count` is refused (`retention-undeclared`); `max_count` without `max_bytes` is refused (`retention-count-only`), also beside `max_age`; `truncation.policy = "until_consumed"` is refused as not yet available (`not-yet-available`, release S3) instead of being accepted and ignored, and that check runs after every other refusal. An unknown key inside one of the three sub-sections is refused as an unknown stream key, naming the nearest known key (for example `retention.max_bites` suggests `retention.max_bytes`). `expected_ingress` and other sub-tables, such as `storage`, remain unknown keys. [mechanism: unit-tested, `StreamConfigParserDeclarationTest`, `StreamResourceValidatorTest.DeclarationRuleIds`, `StreamSectionBindingTest`]
