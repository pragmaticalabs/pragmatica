### Fixed (#1038: disabling auto-heal suppressed the replacement but not the deletion)
- With auto-heal disabled the cluster no longer deletes the VM of a node it lost, so its journal, JVM output and cloud-init logs stay readable. The hold now covers the retry of a failed reap and the periodic re-check of an unconfirmed termination as well as the first reap, and every held reap raises the operator event `instance-reap-held` (WARN log line included) naming the node and the path that was held. Enabling auto-heal again lets the reap go ahead and raises the recovery `instance-reap-released`. Auto-heal enabled: behaviour unchanged. A drain or retirement an operator requested is not held.
