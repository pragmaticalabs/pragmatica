### Fixed (2026-11-21 — #3558: tests whose claim is "a refused connect is typed", pinned without the kernel)
- **The redis, http-client and aether-cli tests no longer rely on a held loopback port refusing a connect** (#3558, second failure after #3319/#3321: on Linux CI `RedisConnectionTest.unreachableServerIsConnectionFailed` saw `Timeout` after 10.02 s). Each claim is now pinned deterministically: redis through a scripted client channel factory (new package-private `RedisConnection.connect`/`RedisClient.start` overloads; a refused connect and a connect that never completes, ended by the connect timeout, are each typed `ConnectionFailed`), http-client and `ClusterHttpClientDrainStateTest` through a `RefusingHttpClient` handed to `JdkHttpOperations.jdkHttpOperations(HttpClient)`, and the child-JVM `UnreachableClusterExitTest` through a `DeadEndpoint` that accepts and resets every connection.
- `UnreachablePort` and its test are deleted from `test-logging`; it had no users left.
