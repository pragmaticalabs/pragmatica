### Fixed (2026-09-29 — #1408: `jbct:package-slices` dropped a slice dependency silently when it matched no packaging rule)
- **`jbct:package-slices` now fails the build when a slice declares a dependency that would reach no `[slices]` entry.** A consumer compiled without `-Aslice.groupId` / `-Aslice.artifactId` got a package-derived coordinate for its sibling provider. The goal matched only `groupId:artifactId-` and dropped it without a word, the runtime read an empty `[slices]` section, and the slice died at load with a `NoClassDefFoundError` naming the provider interface. The failure names the interface, the emitted coordinate and the two compiler options. A dependency is accounted for when it is a sibling this module packages, or when a direct Maven slice dependency's manifest declares its interface. [verified: jbct/jbct-maven-plugin/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/jbct/maven/PackageSlicesDependencyReconciliationTest.java]
