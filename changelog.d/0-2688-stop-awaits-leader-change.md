### Fixed (2026-11-05 — #2688: stop() returned before the leader-toggled components were deactivated)
- **`AetherNode.stop()` resolved, and quiesced the router, before the deactivation of the leader-toggled components had run** (#2688). The passive notification ends in `LeaderChange(none)`, which the leader FSM routes onto the `LeaderManager` react executor, so `DeploymentManager`, `AbTestManager` and the other `toggle*OnLeaderChange` routes ran on another thread after `route()` returned; a quiesced router drops that reaction.
- `stop()` now runs, in this order: route PASSIVE; wait until a deactivation has been delivered to the toggles (`LeaderDeactivation`, recorded on their route, at once on a node that is not the leader) and the `LeaderManager` reactions queued before it have run (`ReactExecutor.drained()`), bounded by `LEADER_DEACTIVATION_BOUND` (2 s, past which it logs a `Leader deactivation not delivered` WARN and goes on); quiesce the router; close the FSM2 interpreters; stop the components (`controlLoop` through discovery deregistration, rc5's order, now after the quiesce rather than before `stop()` returns); wait for the backup and sealer flushes; `NodeStopSequence`. The promise `stop()` returns waits for all of it. The quiesce and everything after it run on a pooled thread, never on the `LeaderManager` executor or the shared timeout scheduler. [verified: `EmberStopDeactivatesLeaderTogglesTest`, `LeaderDeactivationTest`, `Fsm2SystemTest`, `StopSequencePinTest`, `ReactExecutorTest`]
- The wait is keyed to the delivery, not to the queue: a `LeaderChange` that a concurrent leader-FSM dispatch enqueues after the stop's own dispatch returned (it won the CAS into `QuorumLost` and had not yet run the entry action) is still awaited. [verified: `LeaderDeactivationTest#delivered_whileLeader_waitsForADeactivationEnqueuedAfterTheWaitWasTaken`]
- Known residual (not fixed here): the wait does not cover a `LeaderChange(self)` still queued and undelivered on the `LeaderManager` executor when `stop()` takes the wait (the node is not yet recorded as leader, so the state is resolved) together with its matching deactivation enqueued after the drain marker; the quiesce drops that deactivation and the leader-toggled components may stay active. It needs two racing leader-FSM dispatches around `stop()`. Documented at `LeaderDeactivation`.
