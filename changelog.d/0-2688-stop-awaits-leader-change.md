### Fixed (2026-11-05 — #2688: stop() returned before the leader-toggled components were deactivated)
- **`AetherNode.stop()` resolved, and quiesced the router, before the deactivation of the leader-toggled components had run** (#2688). The passive notification ends in `LeaderChange(none)`, which the leader FSM routes onto the `LeaderManager` react executor, so `DeploymentManager`, `AbTestManager` and the other `toggle*OnLeaderChange` routes ran on another thread after `route()` returned; a quiesced router drops that reaction. `stop()` now quiesces the router, and resolves, only after that reaction has run (`ReactExecutor.drained()`). [verified: `EmberStopDeactivatesLeaderTogglesTest`, `ReactExecutorTest`]
