### Fixed (2026-10-20 — #1337: an ephemeral publisher fanning out to a durable-topic (context) subscriber threw a ClassCastException)
- **An ephemeral-tier delivery to a subscriber whose handler takes `ContextualEvent` is now refused with a typed `TopicTierMismatch`, not a `ClassCastException`.** `DefaultSliceBridge.invoke` (the bare-event path) used to hand the decoded event to the generated context adapter, which threw and was tagged `SliceDefect.MethodThrew`: a tier mismatch read as a broken slice version to the all-instances-failed detector, and `TopicPublisher.publish` still returned `Success`, in silence. The refusal is deliberately not a `SliceDefect`. [verified: aether/slice/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/aether/slice/DefaultSliceBridgeTierMismatchTest.java]
- **The loss is visible.** `TopicPublisher` keeps its documented ephemeral result (at-most-once, `TopicDurability.EPHEMERAL`; one subscriber's failure does not fail the publish), and now counts every refused or failed per-subscriber delivery on `aether.topic.delivery.undelivered` (tags `topic`, `address`, `slice`, `reason` = `tier-mismatch` | `failed`), and raises the operator event `topic-tier-mismatch` (naming the topic, the publishing slice and the subscribing method) once per subscriber, with the INFO recovery `topic-tier-mismatch-resolved` when a later delivery reaches it. A refusal relayed from a subscriber on another node is recognised from its text. [verified: aether/aether-invoke/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/aether/invoke/TopicTierMismatchTest.java, aether/aether-invoke/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/aether/invoke/PublisherFactoryTest.java]
- [limit: the publisher still cannot know a remote subscriber's handler shape before delivering; refusing the deployment that creates the mismatch needs the tier on the subscription row, which is a separate change]
