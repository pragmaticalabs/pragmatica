### Added (2026-11-20 — #3458: stream-mem M-1b, the one answer switch and the cursor-expired reason)
- **One switch from a failed read to its answer.** `ReadRefusal.Kind.answer()` and `ReadRefusal.answerOf(Cause)` give every read cause a row (`Answer`), and `StreamErrorStatus.statusOf(Answer)` gives the row its status; the stream read route answers through `StreamErrorStatus.typedRead`. [verified: aether/aether-stream/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/aether/stream/forward/ReadRefusalTest.java, aether/node/src/test/java/org/pragmatica/aether/api/routes/StreamErrorStatusTest.java]
- `StreamError.CursorExpired` carries a reason (`EXPIRED` or `LOST`; the two-argument form is `EXPIRED`), and it rides the existing `ReadRefusal` flag slot, so bytes written by the old form decode as `EXPIRED`. Both reasons answer 410. No raise site sets `LOST` yet.
- New `StreamError.StreamNotMaterialized` (503, transient) and its refusal kind `STREAM_NOT_MATERIALIZED`, placed just before `UNKNOWN`.
