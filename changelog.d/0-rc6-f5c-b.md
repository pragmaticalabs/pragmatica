### Changed (2026-11-07 — rc6 f5c lane B: community capacity sources are ranked by profile)
- **The community reserve path now ranks its short locations with `SourceRanking.rank`** (no deadline, no `max_nodes` counts) instead of source-name order, and `fallback = "forbid"` is honoured: an unavailable location whose source forbids fallback keeps its full share (its capacity is not spilled to the other locations) and is provisioned only by its probe. With every new profile field at its default (`preference = 100`, `cost_class = "ondemand"`, `fallback = "allow"`) the order is exactly today's `locationOrder()`. `max_nodes` still has no effect. `[verified: CommunityPlacementRankingWiringTest]`
- **Upgrade note:** these fields already parse at rc5, where they are inert. A config that sets `preference`, `cost_class` or `fallback = "forbid"` now changes which short location fills first (and, for `forbid`, how an unavailable location's share is held) on upgrade, with no switch. Final per-location counts are never changed by `preference` or `cost_class`.
