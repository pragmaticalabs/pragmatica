### Added (2026-10-20 — #241: growth policy for derived communities)
- **A source with more workers than a community's cap sat in ONE community**, and nothing split it. With `[community_policy] growth = true` (off by default) a worker joining a source that declares no `[community.*]` takes the community a pure policy chooses: an undersized FORMING or DEGRADED one first, then the fullest ACTIVE one below `max_size`, then a new `<source>-w-N` up to `max_communities`, then the least loaded one up to the 200 ceiling, then a refusal. A member holds a slot only while the leader has not seen it absent, and the joins the leader has decided but not yet seen commit are counted, so one leader does not hand the last slot to two joiners. Growth never moves a member: assignment stays immutable and a new community fills from joiners. [verified: `CommunityGrowthPolicyTest`, `ClusterDeploymentStateCommunityGrowthTest`]
- **The placement pass now follows the committed policy for derived communities** (never for declared ones): it re-stamps their `target_size` when `max_size` changes, and with growth on dissolves one that has been empty for `dissolve_grace_seconds` while another community of its source exists, in two committed steps (DISSOLVING, then DISSOLVED) so a joiner that raced the first step reopens the community instead of being stranded. The last community of a source is never dissolved. [verified: `DerivedCommunityPlanTest`, `CommunityPlacementDerivedTest`]
- **New operator warning `community-growth-blocked`** (recovery `community-growth-resumed`) when a join cannot be placed because every community of the source is at the ceiling and the source is at `max_communities`. [verified: `ClusterDeploymentStateCommunityGrowthTest`, `OperatorWarningCodeTest`]
