### Fixed (2026-10-09 — #1895 PiggybackBufferConcurrencyTest flake)
- **`PiggybackBufferConcurrencyTest.faultyCount_neverReadsZero_…` no longer fails on the reader/ticker speed ratio.** Test defect, not a `PiggybackBuffer` race: the ticker looped until the reader finished and could exhaust the FAULTY entry's dissemination budget (3 × maxSize peeks) when the reader was descheduled, tripping the "never evicted" control. The ticker now does a fixed `maxSize` peeks, so eviction is impossible by construction, and the reader reads while it is alive. Overlap is not guaranteed (a descheduled reader reads an idle buffer), so it is checked per trial: up to 3 trials, asserting only on a trial where a peek landed during a read, and an Assumption abort (skipped, neither green nor red) when none did. `PiggybackBuffer` is unchanged. [verified: the old test body fails 37 of 40 runs with a 500 ms or 2 s reader stall; the new test is green 100 of 100 unmutated, red 100 of 100 with `synchronized` removed from `faultyCount` and 100 of 100 from both `peekUpdates`, and skipped 10 of 10 with the reader stalled before its first read, on the correct and on the mutated product]
