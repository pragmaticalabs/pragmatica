### Fixed (2026-11-05 — #2682: a new stream owner nothing demanded stayed behind the tail a surviving replica held)
- **A new partition owner runs its promotion gate as soon as the committed ownership record names it** (#2682). The gate, which pulls the appended tail from the highest live replica before the owner serves, used to start only when an owner action (a publish, an owner read) called it. After a failover to the replica that missed the last record, an idle partition therefore stayed one record behind its other survivor indefinitely. The committed record now starts the gate at once, with no demand, at most `[streaming] reshuffle_concurrency` such gates at a time (the cap is a guess; a demand is never capped). The election itself is unchanged and head-agnostic on purpose: an acknowledged record is on every in-sync replica; only an unacknowledged tail can be missing, and the gate pulls it.
