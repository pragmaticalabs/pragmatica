### Changed (2026-10-29 — #2201 PR-0b part 3: the DHT tier of stream segments is bounded; closes #2258)
- **BREAKING (pre-GA flag day): two DHT wire records change.** `PutRequest` gains `repair` and `PutResponse` gains `capacityExceeded`, `usedBytes` and `maxBytes` (`wire-assignment-baseline.txt`, tags 64 and 65). A mixed cluster must not run DHT puts. Upgrade by coordinated replacement.
- **Fixed: nothing bounded the DHT's heap for stream segments, so a stream workload could exhaust the heap of every node.** Each node's DHT engine now counts the live value bytes and the tombstone bytes it holds per prefix (`stream-segments`, `artifacts`, each `<name>-blocks`, and `other`), on every path that changes an entry. `[streaming] dht_segment_max_bytes` (default: the larger of 64 MiB and a quarter of the maximum heap, 128 MiB on the shipped `-Xmx512m` launchers) caps the live bytes of `stream-segments`: a replica refuses a client put above it and stores nothing. The writer sees `DHTError.ReplicaFull` (ahead of timeouts and fence refusals when the put misses its write quorum), and `DhtStorageTier` maps it to `TierFull(REMOTE, used, max)`, so the seal fails and its local-disk copy is undone; publishes slow or stop instead of the heap filling. The refusal is always on (Q14, `820deb4c34`).
- The bound is per node and binds client writes only: catch-up, migration and hand-off are applied through `putReplica`, which accounts them and never refuses, and the fallback read's re-homing is a client-path write marked `PutRequest.repair`, which is likewise accounted and never refused, so re-replication can take a node past it; a put accepted by the write quorum while one replica refused has fewer copies until anti-entropy repairs it; a put that fails `ReplicaFull` may still be stored on the replicas that accepted it, nothing rolls those copies back unless a replica fenced the put, and they are not reclaimed if the seal is abandoned; tombstones are never capped (they count toward the near-full warning); other prefixes are counted and gauged, not bounded. Entity checkpoint blocks count against the cap and are not reclaimed until PR-3c, which lands right after this PR and should ship in the same release.
- `DHTClient.localUsage(prefix)` and `DhtStorageTier.usedBytes()`/`maxBytes()` report this node's live bytes of the tier's prefix and its cap. New gauges `aether.dht.prefix.bytes{prefix}`, `aether.dht.prefix.tombstone.bytes{prefix}`, `aether.dht.prefix.max.bytes{prefix="stream-segments"}` and the counter-style gauge `aether.dht.prefix.refused.puts`.
- New operator warnings, each closed by its partner: `stream-segment-dht-near-full` (85% of the cap in live plus tombstone bytes, cleared below 80%), `stream-segment-dht-full` (once per episode, at the first refusal, cleared when live bytes fall below 85%) and `stream-segment-disk-pressure` (the durable local-disk tier at its warning ratio, once per episode, on every node; cleared by `stream-segment-disk-pressure-relieved`). Runbook entries are in `incident-response.md`.
