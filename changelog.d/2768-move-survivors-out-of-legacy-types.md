### Changed (2026-11-19 — #2768: move the types that outlive the legacy deployment engine out of it)
- **Internal refactoring, no behaviour change.** Three groups of members that the deployment code still uses were nested in types of the legacy deployment engine, which is being removed. They now live in their own types: `DeploymentAtomicity` (package `org.pragmatica.aether.deployment.cluster`, was nested in `ClusterDeploymentManager`), `SchemaHold` (same package; the schema-hold predicates and the owning-blueprint lookup, were static members of `ClusterDeploymentState`) and `DeploymentConfigFacades` (package `org.pragmatica.aether.deployment.wiring`; the no-op config facade and the `ConfigService` adapter, were members of `NodeDeploymentManager`). Method bodies are unchanged; every reference in main code, tests and the reference docs points at the new location. `SchemaHold` reads the blocking schema statuses from `Schemas.BLOCKING`. `[mechanism: pure move, body-for-body; tests change by imports only]`
