### Fixed (2026-11-20 — #3452: an observer promotion or a quorum return decided on the caller thread could overwrite or drop a queued quorum-loss pause)
- **`RabiaEngine` decides an observer's promotion on its apply executor, in the same step as the write.** `authorizeActivation` tested `isObserving()` on the caller thread and queued a promotion that wrote `Idle` unconditionally, so a quorum-loss pause queued ahead of it was overwritten and the engine left a pause it should hold. The promotion now applies only from `Observing` and only to a node the installed roster still holds; otherwise it is a no-op, and the authorization is kept (`observerMode` is cleared), so a paused observer resumes as a voter. A self-removal applied after the promotion was queued leaves the node an observer instead of activating a removed node.
- **A quorum `ACTIVE` is dispatched on the apply executor too.** The same caller-thread read in `handleClusterActive` saw an engine whose pause was still queued as active, dropped the `ACTIVE` as an echo, and left the engine `Paused` with quorum present until the next notification.
- Pinned by `RabiaObserverPromotionRaceTest` (the executor is parked, the pause and then the promotion or the quorum return, or a self-removal and then the promotion, are queued behind it; each arm red without its guard).
- **A restore is announced before the Decisions buffered during synchronisation are applied.** `activate`, `activateAsObserver` and `doResumeFromPause` drained the buffer first, so live Decisions applied, and notified, ahead of the restore's replay burst, against its "world-as-of-N, then N+1 onward" contract; on an observer the announce fired from inside the drain. Pinned by `RabiaAnnounceBeforeDrainTest` (voter and observer activation, and the resume from a pause; each red under the swapped order). On the resume path no API sequence leaves a restore unannounced, so that arm sets the mark through a test hook.
