### Added (2026-11-14 — #3164: TLA+ models of the Rabia deviations)
- **Three standalone TLA+ models of the Rabia engine** under `tools/tla/standalone/` (`RabiaSlot`, `RabiaEpoch`, `RabiaAdopt`): slot garbage collection and the past-slot guard, in-log reconfiguration with exact-epoch ballot counting, and state adoption after a crash, pause or reconfiguration. 75 configs are listed in `tools/tla/models.tsv` with an expected verdict each; `run.sh` and `check.sh` accept a root that holds `<Module>.tla` and its `<variant>.cfg` files. Results are bounds over the stated constants, not proofs.
- Known-bad and paper-rule configs are listed with the property each must violate; known-bad configs that stay green (the removed rule is redundant at those bounds) are listed as `pass` and say so.
