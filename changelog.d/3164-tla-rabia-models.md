### Added (2026-11-14 — #3164: TLA+ models of the Rabia deviations)
- **Three embedded TLA+ models of the Rabia engine** (`RabiaSlot`, `RabiaEpoch`, `RabiaAdopt`): slot garbage collection and the past-slot guard, in-log reconfiguration with exact-epoch ballot counting, and state adoption after a crash, pause or reconfiguration. The skeleton and the 75 configs sit in `integrations/consensus/.../rabia/package-info.java`; each action sits in a doc comment on the `RabiaEngine` or `PhaseData` method it models. The configs are listed in `tools/tla/models.tsv` with an expected verdict each. Results are bounds over the stated constants, not proofs.
- Known-bad and paper-rule configs are listed with the property each must violate; known-bad configs that stay green (the removed rule is redundant at those bounds) are listed as `pass` and say so.
