### Added (2026-10-28 — worker side of the replica feed, dark)
- **A worker can follow the replica feed, but nothing uses it yet.** The client subscribes to a core uplink first and then pulls the filtered snapshot in chunks (live entries buffer meanwhile and drain after the install), resumes a held anchored position with `FROM_ANCHOR`, and falls back to a snapshot on any refusal that makes the position unusable. A refusal ends its request id: later messages for it are discarded. Readiness is one computation (tag equal, heartbeat at most 30 s old, caught up to the heartbeat that is at least 5 s old, own directive WORKER, initial catch-up done, cluster directory installed) and the engine's passive-client flag now follows it both ways through `RabiaEngine.setPassiveClientReady`; `completePassiveRestore` fires the post-restore listeners after a snapshot install. Production builds nodes with `ReplicaCutover.DARK`: workers still read the metadata projection and the engine latch, and construct, route and schedule nothing of the new client.
