### Fixed (2026-11-24 — #1055: an OVERPROVISION drain could select a replacement seconds after it joined)
- **A surplus drain no longer takes a node that has just joined** (#1055). Victim selection exempted ephemeral (CTM-provisioned) nodes that own no slices from the drain-safety grace, so a replacement could be drained the moment it joined, and a delivered DRAIN cannot be undone. The grace (`split_timeout` × 2, 30 s at the default) now shields every candidate before any tier ordering applies, whatever the node's shape or slice ownership. When every candidate is young, the surplus is deferred: no drain, a WARN with the deferred count and the young ages, and one follow-up reconcile when the oldest young candidate matures. [mechanism: one age filter on the candidate pool in `LeaderReconciler.selectDrainVictims`, pinned by `LeaderReconcilerTest$DrainSafetyGrace` and `$DrainVictimSelection` (red on the old selection)] Operator action: none. A deferred surplus clears on its own once the candidates age past the grace.
